### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, The Lumberyard on Modern Marvels."*

> From a $200 pile of plywood to a stack of rare African veneers worth 20 grand. This is where you'll find the various woods used by the construction industry. Whether it's timber from last week, or 100 years ago, or 50,000 years ago. It's all cut, shaped, and prepared by an army of workers with an arsenal of mighty tools. Now, The Lumberyard on Modern Marvels. You can find virtually any shape, size or species of wood here. the lumber yard. It's a vast, busy and noisy complex where technology is brought to bear on stacks and stacks of lumber that will eventually form the structures we live in. One of the things we're relearning today is that wood has a unique appeal to us as individuals. We want to be surrounded by wood to build our homes and to beautify our homes. And that's That's where the lumber yard is the last mile for delivering that source and those values to both homeowners and builders. I got a guy calling in on that. I want to check that out. In Richmond, California, Channel Lumber is a traditional lumber yard that sells primarily to the construction industry. Over the last 50 years, we started with industry, moved to the local contractors, and then moved to the elite contractors that actually build America. People that build Skys Creek in San Francisco. And at one time we had five bridges going here. We were delivering to all the five bridges. And the highways, people don't realize, but they use a lot of timbers for supporting the concrete. It all starts here, in stands which have taken decades or even centuries to mature.
