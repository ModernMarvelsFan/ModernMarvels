### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Police Pursuit on Modern Marvels."*

> They can run, but they rarely get away. Night or day, in rural and urban settings, high-speed chases can be dangerous and terrifying. But when equipped with the latest technology and the best training, law enforcement has the upper hand. Now, Police Pursuit on Modern Marvels. At speeds in excess of 100 miles per hour, a reckless criminal with no regard for the people or property in his path will do anything and go anywhere to avoid being caught. For law officers, these are worst case, stress-filled scenarios with unpredictable outcomes that unfold second by second. They've committed a violation, and they just want to get away, and they have total disregard for their safety as well as anybody else's. That's what I found out to be the case. Any time an officer gets in pursuit, there's excitement. I mean, you're chasing this car. You have no idea why it's running. Your heart is in your throat a few times when you go through some of these intersections because it's because even though you got the siren blaring, you slow down, people don't pay attention. They got the windows rolled up and they're listening to the radio. The thing about when you're in a pursuit, I never was afraid I was going to get killed or hurt. My concern was always what the offender would do, and I think that's pretty common for a police officer. You talk to him. I was scared what he was going to do to somebody innocent. When they get out there, you've got a 4,000-pound vehicle. That's a weapon when it goes at a high rate of speed. And if you're in a pursuit situation, you're in danger in the life of the officer, as well as citizens. But police officers have more and more technologically sophisticated help. This patrol car is equipped with the latest in pursuit technology. Recently, the California Highway Patrol implemented heads-up display systems that provide important data on see-through screens near the windshields of the patrol cars.
