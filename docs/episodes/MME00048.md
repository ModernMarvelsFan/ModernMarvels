### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the story of visionaries who challenge the heavens to build great towers on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They're the center of celebration, the site of great discoveries, spires of power that spur dreamers to push the limits, symbols that inspire national pride. Now, the story of visionaries who challenge the heavens to build great towers on Modern Marvels. Since the dawn of time, humans have dreamed of breaking the shackles of our landlocked existence, to soar through the sky and see the world from above. It can free the spirit from the burdens of life. Throughout history, people have built towers to seek the euphoric sensation of height. When you're happy, when you're satisfied, when you're in a good mood, you say, well, I'm on top of the world. The earliest towers were built to bring man closer to God. Let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top reaching heaven. That's how the Bible describes the world's first known tower, built thousands of years before Christ, the Tower of Babel. Towers touch more than mankind's fascination with the heavens. They fulfill a human need for power. Well, it's in human nature, this demand that he has for power, for domination and being served. So when you go up a tower and you're in the observatory and looking down, you dominate the area, you dominate the people that are there. You satisfy, consciously or unconsciously, a need to dominate, a need to be powerful."*

</details>
