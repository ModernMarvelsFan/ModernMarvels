On size alone, it tops the charts. Twice the square footage of Texas, with 33,000 miles of shoreline. The state could stretch all the way from Florida to California. Alaska's highway system serves just a third of the state, with only 5,600 miles of roads. That's the same amount as in Vermont, yet Alaska is 62 times bigger.
