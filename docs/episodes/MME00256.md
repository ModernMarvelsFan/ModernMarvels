### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"You thought you had the basics pretty much figured out. Think again. Science and technology are changing the way the world engages in its favorite pastime. Now, high-tech sex on Modern Marvels. High-tech sex? It sounds like a contradiction in terms. Isn't sex one thing that doesn't require any technology? It's a biological function, a drive for which people are pretty much fully equipped at birth. And yet, the human impulse to take a good thing and make it incredible has driven people since ancient times to contrive and devise potions, gadgets, devices, and toys for l'amour. Every technological advance from ceramics to silent films to CD-ROMs and DVDs has immediately been coupled with sex. It never ceases to amaze me the speed with which humans deploy their technology for sexual purposes. It's got to be some unit of measurement. It's like speed of light, speed of sound, time it takes to get technology to a sexual expression. Sex has never been totally natural. People have always been trying to insert some kind of mechanical device in it. They've been trying to toy with it. They've been trying to make it better. I mean, with this huge brain, we have created a high-tech world out of a very primitive, basic drive. That drive is on the verge of morphing into some mind-boggling new forms. Like holographic sex, in which a programmable 3D image floats to life before your eyes. Or virtual sex, not just emails and chat rooms, but sophisticated computer add-ons that make it possible to literally turn someone on across the Internet."*

</details>
