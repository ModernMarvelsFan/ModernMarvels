> *"Got $10,000 for one fish? From the open seas, to the cannery, to the school lunchbox, it's the fish that means so much to so many. Powerfully built, they're sleek, and they're fast. If there ever was a sexy fish, this is it. But due to overfishing, the most prized of their species is in trouble. Wherever they swim, they're the most sought-after fish in the world. Today, people across the globe are working to save these ocean giants. But can they be bred in captivity? Now, it's Tuna on Modern Marvels. Every morning in seaports and fishing villages throughout the world, fishermen are gearing up, collecting bait, and heading out. All with the same goal in mind, reeling in that most powerful and delectable of fish, tuna. In an average year, nearly 4 million tons of tuna are hauled aboard ship. That's almost one and a half pounds for every man, woman and child on earth, making it the most popular food fish in the world. What most people think of tunas is as a tuna sandwich. Well, actually, these are magnificent creatures, but people don't know about them. The tuna is probably the best designed fish in the world. If you had to design something that was perfect at moving through the water, you would end up designing a tuna. The name tuna is derived from the Greek verb tuno, meaning rush, which is appropriate given the tuna's remarkable swimming ability."*
