### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, more coin operating on Modern Marvels."*

> They'll dispense almost anything in return for a few coins. They're a dime a dozen and one of a kind. They're everywhere and they want money. So grab some coins. Treasure, treasure. There we go. And get ready to go deep beneath the surface of the coin-operated world. Now, more coin operating on Modern Marvels. Americans love change, at least the loose kind. According to the United States Treasury, there's about $15 billion of it spread across the country. When it comes to spending those coins, The options are endless. But each year, about 39 million of us choose to empty our pockets here. Las Vegas, Nevada, where day and night, gamblers flock to casinos. And at least 59% of them will drop their money into America's favorite gambling device, the slot machine. While most modern slot machines been given a high-tech makeover. Among serious enthusiasts, nothing beats a coin-operated one-armed bandit, especially here at the East Side Cannery, where staff and patrons alike are keeping an old Las Vegas tradition alive. Seven days a week, we are always crowded in here. The clientele in here are regulars. A lot of people have come through all over Vegas because we're the only Las Vegas casino to offer a coin-operated slot room for our guests. We come here specifically to play these machines. It's the old time Las Vegas. Playing coin-operated slots is a hands-on experience. This is definitely hardcore. Get your hands dirty and go at it. Customers spend an average of three hours dropping quarter after quarter into these games.
