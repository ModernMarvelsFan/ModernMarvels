> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Ben Franklin Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> He tamed lightning and built the world's first battery. He created an instrument that inspired Mozart, currency that foiled counterfeiters, and a stove that fought off the coldest winter. This founding father was one of his era's greatest scientists and an inventor whose vision still shines today. Now, Ben Franklin Tech on Modern Marvels. Find the highest point on any tall structure, and you'll see an ordinary-looking device hidden among the antennas and decorative spires. The unremarkable metal point, known as the lightning rod, was once the ultimate symbol of science's triumph and a reminder of the terrifying power of nature. Invented in 1749, it was the first practical application of the once mysterious force known as electricity. Today, lightning rods protect millions of structures, large and small, in the United States alone. You'll find one at the top of one Liberty Place, the 950-foot skyscraper that looks down over Philadelphia, where the lightning rod was invented by the city's most famous resident, Benjamin Franklin. His scientific genius made him a mythic figure. while his practical inventions and straight talk made him an intimate friend of the people to this day.
