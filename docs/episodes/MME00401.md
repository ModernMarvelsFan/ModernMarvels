### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, more of the world's biggest machines on Modern Marvels."*

> Gigantic, incredible, amazing, and stupendous. You have to see them to believe them, and even then, you might not. Now, more of the world's biggest machines on Modern Marvels. Welcome to the world of extreme machines. Those giants who lay claim to being the biggest, longest, heaviest, and most powerful on Earth. Supersized monsters that can literally move mountains. When I say it's beyond imagination, it is beyond imagination. Big brutes that boggle the mind. The typical reaction is, oh, my gosh, I never knew how big it was. Miracle machines that can take your breath away. When we say it's the biggest big screen on the planet, there's just nothing like it anywhere in the world. Giants that can do the impossible. We could suck all the air out of Madison Square Garden in about two minutes. These are machines like no other. These are the world's biggest machines. And many of them are built to fly. Ever since the Wright brothers took flight in the first successful airplane in 1903, we've been on a never-ending quest to thrust more payload into the sky. From Howard Hughes' spruce goose flying boat to the ever-dependable B-52 bomber, up to today's airline workhorse, the Boeing 747, we've been driven to build them big. big, and no one more so than the Russians. The Antonov 225 is the world's largest airplane. Its dimensions are staggering. It's nearly a football field in length from nose to tail, and its wingspan stretches an amazing 291 feet.
