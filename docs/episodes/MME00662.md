### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Breaking Point on Modern Marvels."*

> Everything from a house to an eggshell has its point of failure. When we study that instant of destruction, the lessons we learn are invaluable. Because knowing can spell the difference between life and death. Now, Breaking Point on Modern Marvels. You can't truly know a thing until you see how strong it is. That means you have to break it. Only then can you perceive its flaws, recognize its dangers, and learn if it will serve you or kill you. Materials Engineering Labs test the things we use every day. They blow them up, stress them, and push them until they can't take it anymore. Some of the most dramatic results involve the vehicles we depend on. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety, funded by automotive insurance companies, documents hundreds of tests each year. Since 1969, the organization has researched road safety, studying cars, the people who drive them and the environments in which they're driven. Specialists use high-speed cameras to capture every millisecond of a crash in ultra-slow motion, providing a detailed look at multiple points of failure.
