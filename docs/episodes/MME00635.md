### 🎙️ Show Introduction

This episode features multiple distinct broadcast cuts with different opening narrations:

#### Cut A Opening Narration
> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, the untold story of Coin Operated on Modern Marvels."*
>
> They're everywhere. All wanting is to dig deep into our pockets and purses and feed them. They can do just about anything. They're made of metal, plastic, and miles of wire. They're one of a million. Everybody needs to wash their clothes. Or one of a kind. This is made by a San Quentin prisoner. They can be addictive. Annoying. and sometimes really weird. Now, the untold story of Coin Operated on Modern Marvels. Small change is big business, and vending machines are the biggest source of revenue in the coin-operated industry. Every 15 minutes, over 3.5 million coins are inserted into coin-operated vending machines in the United States alone. You can buy anything, from aspirin to ice cream. But by a wide margin, the most common vending machines are those that dispense drinks and snacks. The food and refreshment vending industry in the United States is north of $40 billion a year. 100 million people a day walk up to nearly 7 million machines in the United States and purchase a product. There are approximately a million people employed in the industry and over 13,500 companies providing service. One of the latest developments in vending machine technology makes the jobs of those who maintain them much more efficient. Here at Ace Vending we use handheld computers. What the driver does is open the machine, plugs into the computer board of the vending machine, downloads the financial and sales data, and then we collect that data, bring it back to the warehouse. Every time the drivers finish their rounds, they download the data into the company's computer system. This gives an exact accounting of the money collected and of every product sold.

#### Cut B Opening Narration
> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, more coin operating on Modern Marvels."*
>
> They'll dispense almost anything in return for a few coins. They're a dime a dozen and one of a kind. They're everywhere and they want money. So grab some coins. Treasure, treasure. There we go. And get ready to go deep beneath the surface of the coin-operated world. Now, more coin operating on Modern Marvels. Americans love change, at least the loose kind. According to the United States Treasury, there's about $15 billion of it spread across the country. When it comes to spending those coins, the options are endless. But each year, about 39 million of us choose to empty our pockets here. Las Vegas, Nevada, where day and night, gamblers flock to casinos. And at least 59% of them will drop their money into America's favorite gambling device, the slot machine. While most modern slot machines have been given a high-tech makeover, among serious enthusiasts nothing beats a coin-operated one-armed bandit, especially here at the East Side Cannery, where staff and patrons alike are keeping an old Las Vegas tradition alive. Seven days a week, we are always crowded in here. The clientele in here are regulars. A lot of people have come through all over Vegas because we're the only Las Vegas casino to offer a coin-operated slot room for our guests. We come here specifically to play these machines. It's the old-time Las Vegas. Playing coin-operated slots is a hands-on experience. This is definitely hardcore. Get your hands dirty and go at it. Customers spend an average of three hours dropping quarter after quarter into these games
