### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> Modern Marvels is going big and counting down. No series has celebrated technology and ingenuity over as many episodes as we have. And now, get set to punch that time clock and report to work as we count down America's top ten most amazing jobs. From the wild blue yonder to the depths of a secret underworld. It's a dirty job, but somebody's got to do it. From the daffy to the dangerous to the downright creepy, these nine-to-fivers are redefining the outer limits of earning a living. I'm a tailor for people. I just work in metal. Join our nationwide trek to payday when we crown our number one pick. Now, amazing jobs on this edition of Modern Marvel's Mega Marvels Countdown. The variety of jobs in America is endless, and more than 135 million workers are the driving force behind our gross domestic product of $15 trillion. But not all jobs are created equal, and a rare few are so incredible that they have to be seen to be believed. The top 10 contenders in our countdown show just how innovative and daring we are as a nation. Our pick at number 10 qualifies on both those counts. Imagine a career that advances medical science by getting hands-on with deadly rattlesnakes. That's the amazing job of the fearless crew we call the snake milkers. The perilous task of extracting snake venom is all in a day's work for the scientists at Texas A&M's Natural Toxins Research Center. As dangerous as their job is, it's worth it, because the venom they collect holds a surprising secret. Although it can kill, it can also cure. We work with investigators all over the world and trying to discover molecules in snake venom that are useful in strokes and heart attacks and cancer and other medical problems.
