### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Digitech on Modern Marvels."*

> Ones and zeros, ons and offs are fueling the new wave of gotta have gadgets and gizmos. The toys, correction, the essentials that guys see and say, yeah, I need that. From phones to flat screens, CDs to MP3s, it's become a digital world. Now, Digitech on Modern Marvels. Welcome to one of the holy shrines of DigiDevotion, an electronics superstore. A temple of technology where men come to worship those things that help them see the bigger picture. Get back in touch with those things that matter. And ponder the meaning of life. Yeah, it's all about toys. In the 21st century, a man is measured by his RAM and his ROM, as well as his brain and his brawn. We have a ritual now among guys. It's the spec roll call. You know, well, mine has a, you know, 600 gigahertz processor. What does yours have? It's our way of one-upping each other. If you ask Shaquille O'Neal, he'll tell you he's got to have the very latest, greatest tech gadget that's out there before any of his teammates have it. This has your entire CD inside of it. What is that? This is called a music clip from Sony. It is all a measure of him being cooler than everybody else. Better, faster, cheaper. It's somewhat amazing when you see faster processor speeds and at the same time, less expensive processors so that you get the benefit of both. But buying bragging rights costs money. Today, electronics manufacturers do just about anything to grab attention.
