### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Shipyards on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Shipyards. They're giant waterside construction sites, where the extraordinary take shape. Places where the largest of machines are constructed, retrofitted, and repaired. In times of peace, they create the vessels that carry commerce and international trade. And during war, they build the mightiest weapons the world has ever known. On the shores of the Chesapeake Bay sits one of the United States' largest and oldest shipyards. The sprawling 500-acre Northrop Grumman Newport News facility is situated on nearly two miles of waterfront. Workers at the facility are responsible for building and maintaining U.S. aircraft carriers, the most complicated warships to ever rule the waves. When we bring people into the shipyard, invariably that's the first thing. When you look at an aircraft carrier, they walk up to it, and it's hard to conceptualize that what they're looking at is a ship because it takes up the entire horizon. And when you think about putting that ship together starting with a plate of steel or that piece of pipe, it is an awesome challenge, and it is a deep sense of pride when you get to the finish line. Carrier construction can take nearly eight years and relies on nearly every muscle that the shipyard has to offer. Because multi-ton ship parts can't be picked up at the local home improvement center, Shipyards have to build everything they can't buy. Shipyard is a huge place. At one point in time, we had the capacity to do everything that was in the ship. Over the years, we've become more specialized. We've retained certain core capabilities in the shipyard. One example is our foundry."*

</details>
