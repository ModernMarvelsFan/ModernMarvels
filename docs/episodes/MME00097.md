### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"With one foot of water, Hoover Dam will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"The task, monumental, build the world's largest dam and do it in less than seven years. Nothing like it had ever been attempted. Now, Hoover Dam on Modern Marbles. Located in the middle of America's Southwest Desert stands a monumental product of human ingenuity and accomplishment. A vessel of life-giving water and a structure of awe-inspiring size. 726 feet high weighing 6.6 million tons and creating a lake 150 miles long Hoover Dam is impressive and controversial so were the people who built it personalities such as the stern Sims Ely the law of the land and Frank Crow the feared and respected head of construction thousands labored to build Hoover Dam, workers constantly struggled against the environment and often their employers. The damming of the Colorado River created Lake Mead, the largest man-made reservoir in the world. Ironically, it's located in one of the driest places on earth, 30 miles southeast of Las Vegas, Nevada. Hoover Dam symbolizes the human spirit's discontent to accept nature's rules. Building the Hoover Dam was a colossal undertaking in 1931, the largest government project ever attempted. Constructed in the depths of the Depression, its purpose was to provide flood control, irrigation, drinking water, and electricity. It promised to change barren to bountiful. Water is essential to life. And nowhere is that better understood than in the dry climate of the American Southwest."*
