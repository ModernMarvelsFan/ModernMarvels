The ferris wheel turns and fits and starts, its riders hanging nervously in space. The breeze has picked up and swirls all the familiar aromas of the park together in a single pleasing aromatic breath. Popcorn, cotton candy, corn dogs, and the oil and gear scent of heavy machinery. An amusement park, whether a traditional boardwalk midway or contemporary theme park, is a feast for the senses. There are good things to see and eat, games of skill and games of chance, rides that whirl and lift and make you laugh. But for those seeking real thrills, there is really only one attraction in the amusement park. It's the undisputed king of the park, the roller coaster. The Giant Dipper roller coaster was built in 1924 by a man named Arthur Liu. It is the seventh oldest roller coaster in the United States, and we think it's the best. It's definitely our most popular ride here right here at the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk. I think people just love the speed of it. They love getting the air time. And they also love being able to go up a 70-foot hill and then go down it and look at the beautiful Pacific Ocean right next to it.
