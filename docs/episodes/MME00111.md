Mankind uses methods both primitive and complex to protect what he holds dear. Today, 21st century technology safeguards our most precious possessions Night vision cameras and infrared sensors seek out intruders Computers measure the minute details of fingers, eyes and faces To ensure positive identification Three-dimensional holograms verify the authenticity of valuables from currency to fine cigars.
