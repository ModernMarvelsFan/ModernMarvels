### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now a wonder of the ancient and the modern world, the Great Wall of China on Modern Marvels."*

> For centuries, the shame of a nation. Now a symbol of national pride. This imposing mass of stones and buried bones is a monument to the thin line between vision and paranoia, between industriousness and madness. Now a wonder of the ancient and the modern world, the Great Wall of China on Modern Marvels. The Great Wall of China became the unofficial symbol of the Chinese nation in 1972, when then-President Richard Nixon walked upon it during his tour marking U.S. diplomatic recognition of China. I think that you would have to conclude that this is a great wall and that it had to be built by a great people. But the Chinese themselves have not always taken such pride in the Great Wall. The traditional Chinese view of the wall was that it was a symbol of oppressive government and military weakness and futility. It was a symbol of the suffering of ordinary Chinese people under despotic rule. It rises where the mountains meet the sea at Shanghai Kwan and snakes its way across northern China to the edge of the Gobi Desert, forming the spine of a system of so-called long walls that sprawl over thousands of miles of the Chinese landscape. Estimates of its length range between 1,500 and 4,000 miles, and its grandeur has been widely celebrated in myth and legend. Yet the existence of the Great Wall remained unknown to the Western world for more than 1500 years. Incredibly, it was never depicted in classical Chinese paintings of the period, nor did Marco Polo mention it in writing about his travels in China in the 13th century. In fact, the Chinese did not even refer to a Great Wall of China until the 20th century, after the captivated West gave it a name.
