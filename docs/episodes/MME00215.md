> *"A world of luxury, adventure and sport. Driven by wind or motors, take a cruise through time on vessels ranging from classic yachts to sport boats to the ultimate floating palaces. Now, Pleasure Boats on Modern Marvels. To alter all the sails or power up, the experience is timeless. We're out on the water, intoxicated by a potent combination of wind and sun and spray. Fueled by a need to escape, a passion for adventure, our senses come alive. Inside of all of us, there's a little bit of a kid in us. And when we get a little bit older, that still has an opportunity to come out in boating. I think probably the biggest difference between adults and little kids is maybe the price of our toys. We'll track the world. From Southern California, where Catalina Yachts makes some of the most... To the upper reaches of Lake Michigan, the home of America's premier yacht builder, Palmer Johnson Yachts. To the Millionaire's Playground of Fort Lauderdale in Tampa Bay, Florida, where Lazera Yachts combines technology. Across the Atlantic in Italy, design and luxury are the anchors of Azamut Bonetti yachts. And as we navigate north to Holland, we find Fedship, whose prestigious line of vessels have brought it to the forefront of the mega-yacht world. Wherever we go, pleasure boating is king. It's the ultimate magic carpet, really. It allows you to take command of your destiny. While yachting at this level is obviously the domain of the Megara, it began thousands of years ago as the exclusive realm of royalty. Historical records are scant, but we do know that Cleopatra enjoyed herself on her barge."*
