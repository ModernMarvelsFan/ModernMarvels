> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"It's Halloween Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> It's that one night where things aren't always what they see. Blood flows without wounds. Pumpkins are paired with power tools. Here we go. It's gotta get loud. Cuddly theme parks turn creepy. Come on down to the farm. Monsters are made of latex and paint. Terror becomes a virtue. And laughter morphs into screaming. Don't try to run. Because there's nowhere to hide. It's Halloween Tech on Modern Marvels. October 31st. It's the night that encourages us to create, frighten, and be frightened. It's Halloween. But this holiday is more than child's play. The proof is in the cash register. Halloween generates over $5 billion in sales every year in the United States alone. It's second only to Christmas, which annually pulls in a staggering $450 billion, simply because of the presents. But Halloween is still a ghoulish opportunity to decorate, give, and indulge. All reasons cited by consumer groups for Americans to spend, spend, spend. Every year, Knott's Berry Farm in Orange County, California, cashes in on the festivities. Knott's is home to the largest Halloween attraction in the world. For the entire month of October, the park gets a macabre makeover. and after regular park hours, it reopens as Knott's Scary Farm
