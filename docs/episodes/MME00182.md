### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, saloons on Modern Marvels."*

> Hand-carved bars and Victorian water filters, liquor guns and brew kettles, crown caps and secret wine cellars. Now, saloons on Modern Marvels. Billy Bob's in the Fort Worth, Texas, Historic Stockyards District. 120,000 square feet of space, nearly three acres. It's a Texas-sized saloon that can hold up to 6,000 customers. This entertainment center is so massive, it has 32 bar stations. Two of the bars are 100 feet long. Bottled beer is their biggest seller. Back in 1986, 16,000 bottles sold at the Hank Williams Jr. concert in about two hours. It was also in the mid-'80s when Merle Haggard bought a round of Canadian Club. For the entire house, 5,000 people. The singer's tab, $13,000. And Billy Bob's made it into the Guinness Book of World Records. All that and a half million dollars worth of video games, slot machines, pool tables, you name it. Billy Bob's calls itself the world's largest honky-tonk. But it might be even more. Inside the saloon, there's a professional bull riding rink. An arena where rodeo cowboys ride some of the toughest bulls in Texas. The top names in country western and western rock music headline here. Talking about entertainment and all the major stars playing here, I've got an old saying here at Billy Bob's, we get them on their way up. And somewhere in the middle, but if they move on to the bigger venues, They go away from us, but it's a funny thing. They always seem to come back.
