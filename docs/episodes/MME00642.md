> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Corrosion and decomposition will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Our world is falling apart. Bridges collapse. Pipelines explode. Buildings decay. It's an all-out war on rust and rot. This is a massive generational undertaking of what caused billions of dollars over several decades. The Navy has taken the battle indoors, while robots take on pipeline intruders. And automakers dream up new torture tests for vehicles. It takes roughly 20 weeks to simulate 10 years of corrosion. But mountains of decaying garbage produce energy. This is like the epicenter of world bioreactor research. And the termite that attacks 600,000 homes each year may lead to an energy promised land. Now, corrosion and decomposition on Modern Marbles. To chronicle the failing infrastructure in the United States, every four years, the American Society of Civil Engineers issues a report card. The report card on America's infrastructure breaks the country's infrastructure into 15 categories, ranging from bridges, airports, water, wastewater, sewers, and gives each of them a grade similar to what someone would get in a report card in school from A to F. ASE has given the nation's infrastructure an overall grade of D. The primary cause for our failing infrastructure, corrosion. All of the categories that were rated by ASC for the infrastructure are affected by corrosion. It's lack of maintenance that shortens the life of infrastructure. Corrosion costs the U.S. economy $300 billion each year. That's 4% of our gross domestic product, nearly half of what we spend on foreign oil,"*
