### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Glass on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Telescopes and window panes. Crystal goblets and light bulbs. Glass is everywhere, from the core of a volcano to the core of your telephone line. In fact, you're probably looking at it right now. Now, Glass on Modern Marvels. Corning, Inc. in Corning, New York, began making glass over 150 years ago. Back then, they made bottles. Today, they make, among other things, glass optical fibers as thin as human hair. If all of the optical fiber currently installed were strung together, it would reach to the moon and back 160 times. These fibers have revolutionized communication because information can travel inside light, and light can travel inside glass. It comes from combining that light onto a single fiber, carrying of that light down the fiber, then amplifying that light so it can go further and further. The principle behind fiber optics is demonstrated with a flashlight and a stream of water. Shine the light into the water and it will illuminate the whole length of the stream because of a principle called total internal reflection. Total internal reflection is a phenomenon whereby light is trapped inside a media where any two media interact. For instance, the surface between water and air is a highly reflective surface. Fiber optic cable traps light inside a core of glass."*

</details>
