### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, police technology on Modern Marvels."*

> From a simple billy club to an arsenal of weapons designed to sniff out the tiniest clue or knock down the toughest bad guy. Now, police technology on Modern Marvels. Clad in full body armor and ballistic helmets, with goggles that can see through darkness and smoke, and weapons that can stun, shock, and disable, today's SWAT teams are the embodiment of high-tech cops. and the future is just around the corner. The Urban Robot is a surveillance device designed to go places no human would dare. Able to climb stairs, maneuver through tight spaces, open doors, and even blow a hole through a wall. It has video, audio, and even infrared sensors for letting those on the outside know what's going on inside. In case of danger, it can hide from sight or escape to safety from a second story window. The urban robot may or may not be the policeman of the future, but it is certainly a considerable change from the policeman of the past. The precursors to modern day police date back at least as far as the Roman Empire's Praetorian Guard. Essentially a private army, they wielded their swords to protect the emperor and do his dirty work. In the Middle Ages, nobles appointed local constables to uphold the law. But for the most part, they were unpaid and unarmed. In 17th century France, Louis XIV established a small central police force whose main job was to spy on dissidents and, when necessary, suppress them. But the first true police force as we know it wasn't formed until 1829 in London, England.
