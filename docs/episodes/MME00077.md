An expert skydiver prepares for another jump, soars through thin air, and makes a perfect landing. Ordinary, everyday activities, except that each of these extraordinary people has lost a limb, and most are double amputees. But thanks to the miracles of space-age materials and design, they are all able to lead active, fulfilling lives. Lives that were an impossible dream just a few decades ago. Man's efforts to replace missing body parts with some sort of prosthesis or artificial device go back to the dawn of history.
