### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Car Crashes on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Worldwide, they kill over 2,000 people every day and injure over 30 million every year. But your odds are getting better. Now, Car Crashes on Modern Marvels. Every day, the men and women of America's law enforcement witness a massacre. 120 Americans die on the road every 24 hours. Over the course of a year, millions will suffer loss and shattered lives due to what many refer to as an accident. We don't refer to a crash as an accident anymore. It's actually a traffic collision. Most crashes we've seen here, someone did something stupid. I've seen rear-end collisions, I've seen head-ons, I've seen broadsides, I've seen big rigs that overturn where the wheels are pointing at the sky. I've seen vehicles go off the paved roadway. So really, I've seen almost everything in terms of types of accidents that may be out there. So nothing shocks me anymore. 42,000 Americans are killed every year. But the number of deaths when compared to the number of drivers on the road has never been lowered. The dramatic decrease in auto fatalities is the culmination of research and development that has led to safer roads, quicker emergency response, and, of course, better cars. Still, nothing in the course of human evolution has prepared us for the forces involved during a wreck. Frontal car crash is over in a tenth of a second. I have heard from the doctors that it takes about a tenth of a second to blink your eye. This is a crash about to happen. A driver speeds down the highway. Without warning, another vehicle pulls into traffic and the driver has a split second to avoid a collision."*

</details>
