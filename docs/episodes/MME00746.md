### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"You'll never think of that loose change the same way again on Modern Marvels."*

> For thousands of years, they've provided us with everything from gum, to stamps, to holy water, offering better entertainment, communication, and transportation. These coin-operated machines can be addictive, annoying, and sometimes really weird. This is made by a San Quentin prisoner. said to contribute to an immoral society. And today, they contribute to billion-dollar industries. You'll never think of that loose change the same way again on Modern Marvels. Small change is big business. And vending machines are the biggest source of revenue in the coin-operated industry. A hundred million people use them every day. Most stay open 24 hours, seven days a week. And you can buy anything, from aspirin to ice cream. But the most common vending machines are those that dispense drinks and snacks. The food and refreshment vending industry in the United States is approximately $37 billion a year, and 100 million Americans a day walk up to nearly 5 million machines and make a purchase. There are approximately a million people employed in the industry and over 13,500 companies providing service. Making the job of those who maintain vending machines more efficient is having technology at the tip of their fingers. Here at Ace Vending, we use handheld computers. What the driver does is open the machine, plugs into the computer board of the vending machine, downloads the financial and sales data, and then we collect that data, bring it back to the warehouse. Every time the drivers finish their rounds, they download the data into the company's computer system. This gives an exact accounting of the money collected
