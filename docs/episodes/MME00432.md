### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, more Doomsday Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> The technology to prevent our own extinction by an asteroid. The power to wipe out our species with an engineered super disease. The battle against cyber terror and the double-edged sword that is genetic engineering. Now, more Doomsday Tech on Modern Marvels. Welcome to the year 2012. This insignificant space rock, a pockmarked orphan from the birth of our solar system, is about to exterminate the human species. It's a backdoor asteroid, masked by the light of the sun, and able to slip past the sparse telescopes in our southern hemisphere. Eight kilometers in diameter, it hurtles through space at 44,000 miles per hour. In a split second, the asteroid shoots through our atmosphere and makes a land impact. It releases the equivalent of 44 million megatons of TNT, 8,000 times the destructive power of the world's entire nuclear arsenal. It can really screw up the whole planet. First with a big explosion, then with lifting material up, blasting it off the earth, and it then falls back down into the atmosphere, where it bursts into incandescence, and the whole planet is enveloped in a firestorm with the forests and the grasslands burning. And then finally, by leaving much of this dusty material in the stratosphere where it blocks the sunlight and plunges the entire planet into darkness and cold that might last for years. It is this kind of environmental catastrophe that leads to a mass extinction. It wouldn't be the first time a successful species was destroyed by a rock from space.
