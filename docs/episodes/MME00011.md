The stadium concept has its origins in antiquity in the Greek and Roman periods. Prior to that, games did not exist, and it was the Greeks who built the first stadia for horse racing and so on. And then the Romans, of course, went much further because they used it as a method of pacifying their populations, especially in places like the Roman Colosseum, where they did a lot of killing of people for entertainment purposes. Early sporting spectacles often climaxed with a big finish for the second place contestant, a practice which has largely been abandoned in recent times. Modern sports are rarely this bloodthirsty. But throughout the 20th century, Americans have been equally passionate about winning and losing.
