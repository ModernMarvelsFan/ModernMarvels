### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, on a special edition of Modern Marvels, the Panama Canal gets supersized."*

> The Panama Canal. It's a marvel of the modern world. At 50 miles long, this man-made passage slices through a continent and unites the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. But now, a century after its creation, the Panama Canal is too small. The race to expand the continent The race to expand the canal is on. With the amount of excavation that we're doing, this is a mega project. We are literally rewriting the book on building block projects. The Panama Canal expansion will be a game changer. I have to see it to believe it. Well, I actually saw it and I believe it. Now, on a special edition of Modern Marvels, the Panama Canal gets supersized. Every morning, the Panama Canal opens for business. For over a hundred years, this icon of engineering has operated with little change. Crowds gather to see one of the seven wonders of the modern world. The giant shortcut connecting the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans is a crossroad of global trade. Right now about 3% of world commerce comes through the Panama Canal. We put between 38 and 42 ships per day, and we operate 24 hours, seven days a week. The ships carry millions of tons of cargo, everything from cars to fuel to food.
