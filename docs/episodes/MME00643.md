### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Bullseye on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"In warfare, the demanding and deadly challenge of extreme ballistic accuracy falls on the shoulders of the sniper. His mission, pure and simple, is to hit the bullseye. Sniper ready? Send it. Trainees learn the art, and experts hone it. Good to go. at the U.S. Army Sniper School at Fort Benning, Georgia. One of the most devastatingly accurate rifles they work with requires a two-man team to operate effectively. The Barrett M107. The M107 fires magazines of 10 .50 caliber rounds to cushion the recoil and increase accuracy. The barrel assembly itself absorbs force as each bullet exits. moving inward against large springs built into the receiver, the central mount that holds the firing mechanism. Each round speeds toward the target at 2,850 feet per second. In this exercise, the challenge for Sergeant Anthony Palkin and Staff Sergeant Rodney Medley is to hit three separate bullseyes from a range of over 400 yards. All right, give me a left half minute. Medley, the spotter, uses a more powerful scope to determine the precise coordinates. Palky, the sniper, must adjust to those coordinates. Left, half a minute. Send one more. To have a chance of hitting the bullseye. The M107's accuracy has one crucial variable working against it at Fort Benning, the wind. The wind conditions are real rough. From 300 to 500 meters, it's all open from here to 500 meters out. So you've got the wind that just pushes from the right to left. All right, 12 o'clock, 4 inches. Send another. The bigger the bullet, the greater the wind's effect on its flight. And the M107 fires a big armor-piercing incendiary round."*

</details>
