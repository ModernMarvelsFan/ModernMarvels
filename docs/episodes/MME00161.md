> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Apollo 13 on Modern Marvels."*

> American astronauts stranded in space, their lives on the line. The Pluto module was designed to only support two people. I keep counting the crew, there's three of us. I used to believe that Apollo. And it was that time when I said, it's gonna be tough, maybe impossible to get this crew back home. An extraordinary rescue effort to bring them home safely. A combination of human courage and advanced technology. Now, Apollo 13 on Modern Marvels. 2, 1, 0. We have commit and we have liftoff. 2, 13. April 11, 1970. Apollo 13 takes to the heavens. The thunder of the Saturn V rocket's five F-1 engines generating 160 million horsepower. To shoot for the moon requires raw power. The Saturn V rocket is 363 feet tall, 60 feet higher than the Statue of Liberty including its pedestal and weighs 13 times as much as the statue itself. Lifted into Earth orbit at 17,500 miles per hour is the mission's three-man crew. Commander James Lovell, Lunar Module Pilot Fred Hayes, and Command Module Pilot John Swigert. Apollo 13 would be the first mission to make a difficult lunar landing in rugged terrain. Back in 1969, Apollo 11 made history in the Sea of Tranquility. The first lunar landing, a moonwalk by Neil Armstrong and Edwin Aldrin Jr. Apollo 12 featured two more moonwalks, this time in the Ocean of Storms.
