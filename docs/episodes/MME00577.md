### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Barbarian Battletech on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"For hundreds of years before Christ, and hundreds more after, Europe's indigenous tribes did battle. Their world was harsh and savage, in which survival was won by those with the deadliest weapons and the will to use them. Their tribal names exude a certain swagger to this very day. The Huns, Vandals, Goths, Saxons, Celts and Franks. The Greeks and Romans branded them barbarians, considering them uncivilized, vile, more animal than human. The term barbarian originally comes from the Greek. And it's what the Greeks called everybody who wasn't a Greek speaker. The Romans take it up and they refer it to everybody that they considered to be non-Roman outside the empire. People themselves certainly didn't call themselves barbarians. The so-called barbarians lived during the Iron Age, an era of war and emerging technologies, when metalsmiths forged the weapons that shaped Europe. No matter when man has fought, there have been technological devices that he's used to increase his ability to fight. A club is better than the hands. A sword is better than a club. And in the case of the barbarians, we have actually very strong technology. The British Museum in London plays host to barbarian weapons once wielded in the fury of combat. Among them is the 2,300-year-old Kirkburn sword. The sword was unearthed in 1987 from a gravesite in Kirkburn, Yorkshire."*

</details>
