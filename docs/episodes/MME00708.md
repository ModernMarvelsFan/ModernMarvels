### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"From turbo-charged racing vehicles to supersonic military craft, Modern Marvels has covered a vast array of speed-boosting advances in the world of high velocity."*

> Modern Marvels is speeding up and counting down. No series is celebrated man-made wonders over so many episodes like we have. And now it's time to count down our top 10 marvels from the world of speed. On land, in the air, and on the sea, these high-velocity vehicles will blow you away as we showcase our need for speed like never before. Are you ready to count down to number one? The very fastest one of all? Don't blink or you'll miss it. It's speed, now on this edition of Modern Marvel's Mega Marvel's Countdown. Oh, God. Our fast-paced lives demand an ever-increasing need for speed. It seems the faster we can go, the better. From turbo-charged racing vehicles to supersonic military craft, Modern Marvels has covered a vast array of speed-boosting advances in the world of high velocity. Now we've selected 10 of the fastest that we're taking on a supercharged race to the finish line and the speediest of all at number one. Charging out of the gate at number 10, it's a high-velocity tank that's at home speeding in the water or on land. It's the military's expeditionary fighting vehicle, or EFV. If you thought an amphibious tank couldn't achieve high speeds, think again. It's a speedboat that thinks it's a tank. This thing is really pretty amazing. The EFV moves three times faster than any other amphibious craft of its kind. In water, it can go nearly 28 miles per hour, and on land, 45 miles per hour. Like a kid's transformer, our number 10 vehicle vehicle easily converts from a land tank to a high-speed armored watercraft.
