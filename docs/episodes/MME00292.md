### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Meet the tank crews next on Modern Marvels."*

> During World War II, American tank crews duked it out with Nazi panzers in a high explosive duel to the death. Crammed inside 30 tons of rolling steel, the GIs went up against a better armed and more experienced enemy. All too often, the tanks became a steel coffin. It was a war where guts and courage were the secret to survival. Meet the tank crews next on Modern Marvels. The tank that put the teeth into an American armored division in World War II was known officially as medium tank M4. But to the five GIs that manned each of these steel monsters, it's better remembered as the General Sherman. The crews hoped that their Sherman would live up to its Civil War namesake, cut a path through hostile territory, and leave in its wake a defeated enemy. The Sherman was conceived not too many years earlier, before America was even at war, as a weapon intended to help the infantry out of a tight spot. It was supposed to roll in and blast away at machine gun nests, enemy troops and other soft targets. but on the battlefields of europe the sherman was forced to take on a much deadlier job shooting it out point blank with enemy tanks everybody was frightened everybody was scared but it got built into you you know this is what you were going to do and you're going to see it through one way or another.
