### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Next on Modern Marvels, War Games."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Fast, furious and deadly, modern warfare is the most intensely demanding activity ever undertaken by mankind. But to wage war you must first practice it. Next on Modern Marvels, War Games. With dozens of potential hotspots scattered around the globe, today's Army must be ready to deploy virtually anywhere at a moment's notice. To achieve this extremely high level of combat readiness, equipment and personnel must be maintained at peak performance at all times. The challenge for a commander is to find a way to reach and maintain this razor's edge of preparation. The answer is rigorous force-on-force combat exercises called war games. The place where the most dynamically realistic war games ever devised are staged is a modern marvel called the U.S. Army National Training Center. Though the soldiers here may be engaging in war gaming, the goal of their deployment is deadly serious. as one veteran army general said after the Persian Gulf War. We died at Fort Irwin so we could survive a desert storm. Home to the U.S. Army's National Training Center, Fort Irwin, California is one of America's preeminent combat training facilities. The base is situated in the heart of the Mojave Desert on over 640,000 acres near Death Valley, a sprawling, parched expanse as large as the state of Rhode Island. Each year, approximately ten army rotations, consisting of roughly 5,000 men and women at a time, descend upon this no-man's land to do battle under the critical eye of their commanders. Outfitted with sophisticated weaponry and monitoring equipment, they're able to engage a powerful adversary on the premier heavy combat force-on-force battleground in the world, transforming essentially from soldiers to combat-ready units."*

</details>
