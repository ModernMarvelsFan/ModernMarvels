> *"They have saved millions of lives. Tiny pills that have the power to kill infection. But are the days of the antibiotic miracle at an end? Now, the soaring successes and unexpected failures of antibiotics, the wonder drugs on Modern Marvels. The End 1941 was the first year that penicillin was used to save a human life. Since that time, antibiotics have transformed the world. Scientists estimate that as a class of drugs, antibiotics have helped add 10 years to the life expectancy of every man, woman, and child on this planet. Hailed as wonder drugs because they killed infection without causing harmful side effects, they have become the most widely prescribed of all medications. Every year in the United States alone, more than 150 million prescriptions are written, antibiotics seem to be the answer to an age-old quest. From the earliest times, healers searched for substances in nature that were medicinal. They used a wide variety of remedies, including herbs, tree bark, and plant resins. According to early botanical sources, These had quite complex effects on the body. Nicotine, opium, and cocaine had clear effects on the body. There was ipecac and Peruvian bark."*
