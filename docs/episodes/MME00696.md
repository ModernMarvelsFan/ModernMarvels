### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Get ready for Weird Machines on Modern Marvels."*

> They are a glimpse into the future, or a throwback to the past. They can breathe fire, underwater, or just go really fast. Kind of like putting a V8 in a Go-Kat. They are strange machines built to pose two important questions. What is it? And how do I get one? The easiest description for it is actually that it's kind of like Batman's tool belt. Join us in discovering today's off-kilter. On the fringe and out-of-this-world mechanical creations. And the mad inventors behind them. Get ready for Weird Machines on Modern Marvels. It's been said that necessity is the mother of all invention. But for some inventions, the necessity remains unclear. There's a growing world of devices resting on the edges of society. the lone wolves of technology, deviating from the norm, and all sharing a common attribute. They're just a touch weird. We launch right out of the gate in Redding, California, with a machine whose look says it all. If you've ever wanted to swim like a shark or dolphin, well, here's your chance. Robert Innes and his team at Innes Space Productions have designed the ultimate aquatic thrill ride, the Sea Breacher X. This high-octane machine was born out of Innes' goal to build an underwater jet ski. This boat is about the closest you're going to get to a sensation of a dolphin or a shark. You go through the water, you jump over it, you can totally do anything that you can imagine. This one is our most extreme vehicle. It puts out about 255 horsepower.
