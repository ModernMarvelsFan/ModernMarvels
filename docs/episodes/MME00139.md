### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> From one of the most ancient achievements of flood control in history, to the largest dam in the world, and the feats of construction and engineering that made them possible. Now, China's Three Gorges Dam on Modern Mars. It will tower 607 feet in height, and its length will span nearly a mile and a half. Its volume, when completed, will equal 44 great pyramids, making it one of the largest structures ever built. At peak output, its 26 gigantic turbines, each weighing 400 tons, will generate 18,200 megawatts of electricity, equivalent to the output of 18 nuclear power plants. It will be the only other man-made object besides the Great Wall of China that is visible from the moon. This is China's enormous Three Gorges Dam. The Three Gorges Dam Project in the NC River is the largest ongoing civil engineering project in the world today. And some people have characterized it as going to be the eighth wonder of the world. The cost of Three Gorges is about $20 to $30 billion. The total concrete placement is about 27 million cubic meters. That's why the Three Gorges is called the largest dam. Just as massive structures such as Hoover Dam helped advance economic development in the Western United States, supporters see Three Gorges as the concrete key to a new China,
