Now, China's Three Gorges Dam on Modern Mars. It will tower 607 feet in height, and its length will span nearly a mile and a half. The cost of Three Gorges is about $20 to $30 billion. The total concrete placement is about 27 million cubic meters. That's why the Three Gorges is called the largest dam.
