### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Barbecue Tech will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Meat, heat, and mouth-watering intense flavors. An old-fashioned style of cooking has evolved into a modern food craze and spawned a multi-billion dollar industry. From torrents of briquettes to extravagant backyard cooking environments. Now, BBQ Tech on Modern Marvelous. Almost any weekend, clouds of smoke can be seen rising from a park or parking lot somewhere in the United States in what has become an American tradition, the barbecue cook-off. Competitors haul in all manner of cooking contraptions, from simple smokers to custom-built steel behemoths that look like Jules Verne had a hand in designing them. Eye-catching team names and equipment, along with a laid-back party atmosphere, help draw in the public. Their reward? The smells and taste thrills of real barbecue. There's something primal about barbecue. When you smell that smoke and then you get a bite of that meat, it just hits a primal button in you. Despite the fun and good food, cook-offs are serious business to the competitors. Recently, the Autry Museum of the American West in Los Angeles held its first annual cook-off. And there was more at stake than just prize money. Los Angeles is just now getting into the market or the venue of barbecue cook-offs. You have all the big-name competitors out here today. They all want to win because we all want to be able to walk away and say we were the first winner of the Autry cook-off. Each team brings the size and shape of equipment it feels will turn out the most savory, lip-smacking product possible. This is what I consider an L-shaped smoker."*
