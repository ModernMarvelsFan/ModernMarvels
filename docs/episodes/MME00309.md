### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> Never has a car meant so much to so few. It's the model that everybody wants, but only a select few are bold enough to own. Topless. Unobstructed. The convertible turns the act of driving into an experience of man, machine, and the elements. Now, convertibles on modern levels. Talk with any convertible owner and they'll tell you the same thing. Driving a convertible is a one-of-a-kind experience. It can always make you happy. You cannot be sad when you drive a convertible. You just can't. Let's put it this way. I wouldn't consider anything but a convertible. It's just an amazing feeling. Lance Zuckerbrunn drives a Ferrari F355 convertible. Only a few hundred are built each year and command a price of more than $120,000. With a convertible, there's no top. You've got the sky, you've got the road, you've got the wind, you've got the sounds of the road, the tires hitting the pavement. I mean, just your part of nature and machinery all at one time. With a 3.5-liter dual-overhead cam V8 engine, its 375 horses can produce speeds in excess of 170 miles per hour. You want me to punch it? All right, here we go. That was 65 miles an hour. What was that, about three seconds? Let's call it four and a half, just because I did get a little excited myself. But there's no sense having a car this exciting if it's not a convertible.
