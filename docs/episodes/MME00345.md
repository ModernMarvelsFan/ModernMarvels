> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, extreme sports gadgets on modern marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Defying the laws of gravity, reckless or revolutionary, insanity or twisted genius. Technology and innovation give birth to the most mind-blowing and perilous sports on the planet. Now, extreme sports gadgets on modern marvels. What are these remarkable feats have in common? None of them would be possible without the specialized equipment that has evolved and improved over the years. And like life itself, it all started here. In the ocean. If there's one historical element that inspired the no-holds-barred mentality of what's now considered extreme sports, it would have to be the ancient Hawaiian sport of surfing. Surfing has been such a huge inspiration to the world. I mean, from a little island in the Pacific, that it's influenced skiers to become snowboarders, water steers, we can wakeboarders, we've got skateboarding. And these are all children of surfing. Today, thank technology for making it possible to ride 60-foot waves with nothing but a surfboard and an iron will. Although it's not clear exactly where and when surfing began, there's no doubt that the ancient sport of Kihinalu or wave sliding was practiced as far back as the second century AD, in the area now known as the Hawaiian Islands. By carving a slab of wood from a tree and adding a bit of lateral ingenuity, the early surf pioneers turned an unfriendly element into an ocean playground. The fascinating thing is the building of a surfboard, and when you really think about it, it's a toy. This toy had ritual, it had incredible significance to the culture,"*
