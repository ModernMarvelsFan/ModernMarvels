> *"A revolutionary design concept, built to break records by a legion of brave men. Protected by an aluminum skin, it boasts the highest rooftop in the United States, with its roots deep in the bedrock of Chicago's land and history. Now, the Sears Tower on Modern Marvels. More than 25,000 people walk into the Sears Tower every day. It takes 104 elevators, some double-decker, to transport workers and visitors to the 110 floors of the tallest building in North America. The Sears Tower was considered the tallest building in the world until 1998, when the Petronas Towers in Malaysia surpassed it. by 33 feet, but only with the help of decorative spires. It rises 1,450 feet above the city of Chicago and has one of the most impressive observation decks in the world. When people come up to the Sears Tower Skydeck, on a clear day they're able to see up to 50 miles, so you're really not just seeing downtown Chicago, you're also seeing the rest of Illinois, Indiana, Wisconsin, and Michigan, and it's just an amazing, amazing view. And with the Sears Tower being the tallest building in Chicago, you're looking down on this amazing architectural mecca that is Chicago. More than a million tourists come to visit the Sky Deck annually to take a multimedia elevator ride up to the top. The Sears Tower Observatory has some of the most unique elevators in the world. First of all, they're two of the fastest. At top speed, they're going 1,600 feet per minute. And you start two levels in the basement and come up to the 103rd floor in about 60 seconds. The 4.5 million square foot skyscraper, located in the heart of Chicago's Loop,"*
