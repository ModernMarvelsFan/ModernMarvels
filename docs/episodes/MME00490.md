> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the M16 on Modern Marvels."*

> The most powerful assault rifle ever used in combat. For more than 30 years, it has been a symbol of American strength worldwide. It has outlived controversy, political infighting, and multiple redesign efforts. Yet its power and sharp aim still gives it the distinction of the military's gun of choice. Now, the M16 on Modern Marvels. The gun has played a critical role in history. An invention which has been praised and denounced, served hero and villain alike, and carries with it moral responsibility. To understand the gun is to better understand history. At first glance, it looks like a toy, not a real gun. Its aluminum and fiberglass body weighs a shade more than eight pounds, measures barely more than a yard, and when fired, has a recoil so gentle a 12-year-old could handle it. Appearances here are most definitely deceiving. This gun's destructive power is undeniable. Though the M16 became famous as Vietnam's gun, weapon designers had played with the concept of a lightweight, high-powered automatic fire weapon since at least the 1940s. But for the better part of a decade, it remained little more than a good idea. Then, in the 1950s, severe setbacks in the early days of the Korean War forced the United States to seriously rethink the nature of the combat rifle. In Korea, there was just these human wave attacks by the Chinese. The Chinese suffered one million casualties in Korea, mainly inflicted by artillery, but certainly a lot by small arms. But when you have hundreds and hundreds and thousands and thousands of Chinese coming up a hill at you, you want firepower. American infantry soldiers in Korea didn't have that firepower.
