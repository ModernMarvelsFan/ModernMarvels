Sit back, relax, and grab a candy bar, potato chip, pretzel lollipop, ice cream cone, or a cupcake while we say a mouthful about some of the world's favorite snacks. Now, Snack Food Tech on Modern Marvels. Friars that cook thousands of pounds of sizzling potato chips an hour. Machines that wrap candy faster than the eye can see. Plants that chill millions of gallons of ice cream a year. The snack food industry believes in outsized productivity and speed And it shows no signs of slowing down I always think machines are so fast, I don't know how we'll ever go faster And three or four or five years later, we're able to design new machinery that can go faster It's taken faster machines to keep pace with consumers' demands. Fifty years ago, snacks were not the grab-and-go product that they are today. Now they are everywhere in every form that you can imagine. They fit in your cup holders. They fit in your lunchbox. They fit in your sport coat. They fit wherever you are, whatever you are. And profits have followed productivity. In the United States, about $20 to $25 billion worth of confectionery products are sold every year. That's a lot of candy.
