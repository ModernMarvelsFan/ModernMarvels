<details>

> *"The Garage. A repository for tools and gadgets designed to efficiently conquer every home and garden task. Their appearance is common. Their stories are unique. Now, Garage Gadgets on Modern Marvels. More than a place to park your car, the garage has evolved into the at-home sanctuary for the modern American male. It is also the place where the tools of 21st century home maintenance are kept, our familiar yet beloved garage gadgets. The garage is becoming our favorite place. It's really the last place that we men have any control over. It's a place where we keep our toys, our equipment, and equipment is the key word because we love equipment. While most men consider themselves to be handy around the house, Fathers see it as their role to provide for and take care of the family's home and possessions. I think we go all the way back to the caveman and, you know, we beat our chest. We are the defenders. We are the protectors. And that also means that in addition to protecting, we have to fix the stuff that we broke in the process of defending and protecting. The gadgets found in most garages are so common you might not notice them. but their invention and evolution are tied to the individual sagas experienced by homeowners throughout history. The barns and carriage houses of the 1890s became America's first garages, as the automobile chased the animals out, and as portrayed in these early silent films, the garage assumed the role as the household headquarters for the challenges and opportunities posed by the four seasons of the year."*
