### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Hurricanes. Twisters. Lightning. Knowing when they'll strike means life or death. Weather prediction. Harnessing chaos. Now on Modern Marvels. A 20-foot-high surge of ocean water pounds stranded islanders as a hurricane devastates the Caribbean. 150-mile-per-hour winds blow through Puerto Rico, turning lives upside down. The path of destruction heads north through the Gulf of Mexico. By the time it crosses the Florida panhandle into Georgia, it's downgraded to a tropical depression, but still floods the state with two and a half feet of rain. That's going to be one of the top stories in our newscast today, the downgraded hurricane. On the other side of the country, Los Angeles weather forecaster Fritz Coleman monitors the storm from the newsroom of KNBC-TV. So I'll ask the machine to pull me up Atlanta airport radar site. Atlanta will occupy the middle of the screen here. And now the machine, right about there, the machine will build the loop. It'll do the last six or seven hours of frames of radar. And we can watch this tropical depression move northeast across the eastern seaboard. Like thousands of other forecasters, Coleman can quickly access an amalgamation of data on weather conditions across the country. It is gathered from radar, satellites, and weather balloons. It comes from research vessels on the ocean and specially designed aircraft capable of flying through the eye of a hurricane."*
