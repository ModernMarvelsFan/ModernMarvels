During a marathon, it only takes 30 minutes for a racer's body heat to rise above the century mark. But once he gets past the finish line, it may take only 15 minutes for his body temperature to plummet below 90 degrees Fahrenheit. This rapid shift, combined with the slightest chill in the air, can result in hypothermia. To prevent this potentially lethal condition, runners drape themselves in thermal blankets made of a special metallic material. The blanket acts as a radiant barrier. By reflecting away excess thermal radiation and trapping body heat, radiant barriers help us maintain a constant temperature. This innovative blanket has a back story that's literally out of this world. Like more than a thousand other cool products, it came from outer space.
