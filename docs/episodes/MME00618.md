### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Whiskey will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"We'll toast St. Patrick in Dublin and have a dram in Denver. And we'll find out what makes Scotch's single, Tennessee whiskey's straight, and Canadian's great mixers. Whether you call it moonshine, bourbon, or even the water of life, it's a real liquid asset. We have bottles in here that will fetch $500 a glass. Now, the untold story of whiskey on Modern Marbles. A fire rages at the Jack Daniel Distillery in Lynchburg, Tennessee. But rather than dampen it, workers stoke it with an unusual fuel. We're using 140 proof whiskey to start the fire because if we use gasoline or diesel or anything like that, it'll contaminate the charcoal. Each one of these stacks of wood is called a rick. It takes 16 ricks to fill a vat of charcoal. To remove impurities from their whiskey, distillers at Jack Daniel's filter every drop of it through vats of sugar maple charcoal they manufacture on site. This process is the one thing that we do, manufacturing Jack Daniel's, that distinguishes us from a bourbon. This lone flavor modification makes Jack Daniel's a Tennessee straight whiskey and not a bourbon. Bourbon, made with a majority of corn, can't have any flavor modification. It's just distillate and water, aged in oak barrels. The whiskey category is ripe with these kinds of subtle distinctions. There are Tennessee whiskeys, bourbons, Irish whiskeys, Canadian whiskeys, scotches, ryes, even a Colorado whiskey. Though distinct, they're all whiskeys because they have one thing in common. Well, the main thing that differentiates whiskey from the other spirits in the world is the fact that whiskeys are made from grain. A major grain producer, the United States is also the world's number two producer of whiskey behind Scotland. Canada, Japan and Ireland follow. Whiskey is the world's second most popular distilled spirit after vodka."*

</details>
