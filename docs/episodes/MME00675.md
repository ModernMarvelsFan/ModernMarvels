### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, our picks for the top 10 technological innovations of the last generation on Modern Marvels."*

> They've transformed our lives. Yet a couple of decades ago, they didn't exist. Now we don't know how we ever got along without them. How much have they truly improved the human condition? And at what price? Now, our picks for the top 10 technological innovations of the last generation on Modern Marvels. Congratulations. If you've made it this far, you're a survivor. You've lived through a generation of unparalleled technological innovation. The effects of technological change and its impact on how we live, work, and play has been accelerating dramatically, more so than any other generation in the history of mankind. However, some might say you can take this technological overload and, well, shove it. That's it. All right. Hold on. We don't pay complete attention to anything anymore because these technical revolutions that we've witnessed are all about allowing you to multitask in some way or another. For over a decade, modern marvels has showcased the rapid advancement of technology. Now we've decided it's our turn to identify and rank by importance the top 10 innovations of the recent past. We may take these technologies for granted today, but they've all dramatically changed our lives. And most weren't even around a few decades ago. We begin in the middle of our technological list at number 5. Robotics. Forget science fiction. Robots now rule the factory floor. They've helped construct many of the items we use every day. That's because by 2008, there were over one million of them.
