The effects of technological change and its impact on how we live, work, and play has been accelerating dramatically, more so than any other generation in the history of mankind. We may take these technologies for granted today, but they've all dramatically changed our lives. And most weren't even around a few decades ago. We begin in the middle of our technological list at number 5. Robotics. Forget science fiction. Robots now rule the factory floor. They've helped construct many of the items we use every day.
