### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"On Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"The house that Ruth built. The eighth wonder of the world. Baseball stadiums. American icons. Sandlocks. Wooden ballparks. Multipurpose stadiums. Concrete. Steel. Now, baseball parks. On Modern Marvels. Baseball, the national pastime, making the good times memorable and the bad times bearable. the childhood heroes, the smell of the grass, the crack of the bat, and the memories of warm summer days spent at the ballpark. It really is something that just overwhelms you, it fills you with some sort of emotion when you step out from underneath the stands and that field is before you. And certainly the imagery that the ballpark embeds on you, in your mind's eye, stays with you forever. The parks are part of the enduring legacy of baseball, each adding its own imprint to our memories. These ballparks, they're almost monuments to the game, even though they're living things. They were truly monuments to the glory of America's national pastime. Today's games are held in technological wonders. The fans experiencing the game firsthand will be subjected to blinding lights, ear-splitting noises and fiery explosions, all of which heighten the baseball experience."*
