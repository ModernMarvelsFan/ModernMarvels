<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Get yourself stuck in a rut? Feel like you're dead in the water? Need a friendly pull in the right direction? Or maybe you want to take everything with you? Towing can help. Whether it's pulling, lugging, yanking, hooking or hoisting, being a drag was never so good. Now, towing on Modern Marvels. This is the easy part. This is the hard part. And it's not just this. It's this. And this. And even this. You may think you know towing, but there's an incredible variety to it. A whole science to it. And sure enough, a history. But a good place to start is on the side of the road, with your car inoperable, and tow truck operator Rusty Morse on his way in one of his state-of-the-art trucks. Most of the towing done nowadays is done with the wheel lift due to the fact that it's mostly damage free. Many of the vehicles today are too soft to be lifted up by the bumpers of the front end, so you have to pick them up by the tire so no damage can occur. It may be nerve-wracking for a driver to watch his car being hauled away on the end of a truck, but it rarely does any damage to the vehicle. As you can see, the L-arm cradles the tire quite nicely. No part of the vehicle is touched during the towing process. We retract the vehicle. We want to put as much weight forward as we can and still leave room enough between the vehicle and the back of our tow truck to turn safely without hitting the bar on the back of the tow truck."*

</details>
