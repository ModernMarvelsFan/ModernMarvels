### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Wine on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It lifts the spirits and intoxicates the senses. Gods were created for it. Now robots bottle it. And sometimes its cost can make a prince a pauper. Not bad for something that we shake, stomp, and hide underground. Now, Wine on Modern Marvels. It's known simply as Crush, the annual fall harvest of grapes that marks the beginning of California's winemaking season. We usually start harvesting grapes in September. It's a time of year where all of our hard work from an entire year kind of comes to fruition. It's kind of the Super Bowl for us. year in winemaking regions around the world, millions of tons of grapes are gathered, either by hand or by powerful mechanical harvesters that shake the vines into submission. In California alone, more than three million tons of wine grapes are harvested annually. The decision to machine or hand pick is just one of many that every winemaker must make. At Buena Vista Carneros, for example, the harvest is done only by hand and occurs at night to preserve freshness. The warmer the fruit comes into the winery, usually the less fruit intensity you'll have in the final wine. So we're really, really big fans of getting out there when it's cold, get the fruit in here and take care of it from there. At the winery, the grape clusters are quickly sorted. Then they fall into a de-stemmer, where a series of beater bars gently knock the grapes from their stems. Once separated and clean, the grapes are now ready to become wine. Grape vines are among nature's great survivors, and often thrive in places where little else"*

</details>
