### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Runways on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They're where the rubber meets the road. The crucial interface between land and sky. They're the main drag at big city airports. And a thin ribbon of hope in the bush. They can be three miles long or as compact as a football field. Now, Runways on Modern Marvels. The runway. It just might be the most underappreciated piece of infrastructure in America. Bridges and tunnels get all the oohs and odds, but runways are the backbone of the aviation industry, which is the backbone of the transportation industry. Every day, 175,000 aircraft take off and touch down on runways in the U.S. Everything sort of revolves around the runway. Every pilot that goes out there trusts the airport proprietor, air traffic control, that they have 9,400 feet of absolutely the safest runway that we can provide for them. Runways aren't really the most glamorous of structures, certainly, but they do form the basis of the air transportation system. I mean, you really have to get down and get up from somewhere. So they are, of course, very important. I've been flying for 20 years, and the first thing you have to learn is how to take it off and how to put it back on the ground. All the rest of your mission you learn later, but if you can't get it in the air and get it on the ground, you can't go any further than that. Before we go any further, let's make one thing perfectly clear. Runways are never under any circumstances to be confused with ordinary roads, or even the interstate. In terms of layout, construction, and design, the runway is to the interstate, as the interstate is to an urban sidewalk."*

</details>
