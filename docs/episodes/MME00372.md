Hydraulics has taken us from the Model T to the SUV, from Kitty Hawk to the F-22 Raptor. Hydraulics provides the muscles that lift, steer, and drive excavators that mine Earth's precious resources. and is the force behind the machinery that fabricates the very structure of our cities. Hydraulics elevates us, thrills us. It even picks up our garbage. You look at a lot of applications and we see hydraulics. If you think about a car going down the street, of course you have hydraulic power steering, hydraulic brakes. A lot of the roofs on those cars, the convertible tops are all hydraulic controlled. Get into aircraft, all the control surfaces. Amusement parks, a lot of the rides today, there's hydraulic powered roller coasters. Oh my God! Go 0 to 120 in 4 seconds. Hydraulics is the branch of physics that studies the behavior of liquids at rest or in motion. liquid in motion. Liquid in motion is referred to as hydrodynamics. Civil engineers use this form of hydraulics in hydroelectric power plants, manipulating the flow of liquid to push turbines. Those power plants provide electricity to over a billion people worldwide.
