### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Booby Traps on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They've been in use for over 3,000 years. But they're no longer only in physical space. They're in cyberspace as well. They're crafted with sophistication or simplicity. But make no mistake, using one today buys you a one-way ticket to the penitentiary. Now, Booby Traps on Modern Marvels. The explosion ripped through the mezzanine floor, which houses the executive suites where the bomb was discovered early yesterday morning. The FBI continued to keep this area around Krasinski's cabin cordoned off from the press. They refused to deny or confirm reports that there was an explosion in this area earlier this morning. The elderly woman who opened the mailbox told her husband that the bomb looked like a flashlight. It blew up when she touched it. Each of these stories is a little different. Yet each shares one thing in common. The victims never saw it coming. A booby trap is an explosive or mechanical trap that is designed to appear totally innocent. But when it's moved or touched by an unwary person, it suddenly goes off. Anything at all can be booby-trapped, or booby-traps can be fabricated from just about anything that you can get your hands on. Dynamite wired to a car's ignition. As the key is turned, it ignites. A shotgun hooked up to a doorknob. When the door opens, it goes off. Even a water faucet, rigged so when it's turned on, an entire house explodes. Bottom line is, a lot of bad people out there with very vivid imagination. Most booby traps are meant to kill or seriously injure their intended victims."*
