> *"They are the finest rocket engines in the world, built under conditions of absolute secrecy during Russia's race to the moon. And it all began at Russia's vast launch complex, the Cosmodrome. Next, on Modern Marvels. Behind the doors of this warehouse, in a distant part of Russia, lie what for 20 years was a closely guarded secret. A cache of rocket engines designed for the most ambitious space project ever to be undertaken by the Soviet Union. Today regarded as some of the finest in the world, The engines use a unique technology developed by Russia at the height of the space race. A technology so ambitious that Western rocket scientists thought it was impossible to master. The Russians were very secretive at that time, and although they said they were doing something, they did not tell us what they were doing, and in fact it took a great deal of intelligence to really understand what their direction was. The really important things were having good, clear pictures of what they were building. In the 1960s, the CIA spy satellites were keeping regular watch on the Soviet Union's vast space launch complex, the Baikonur Cosmodrome in Kazakhstan."*
