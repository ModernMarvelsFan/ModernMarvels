Now, it's Big and Small on Modern Marvels. We're used to a world populated by machines of conventional size. But this isn't about them. This is about the super big and the super small. Take the world's largest commercial jetliner, the Airbus A380. At about 240 feet long, 260 feet wide, and 80 feet tall to the tip of the tail, The A380 has 40% more floor space than a Boeing 747. That's because it's got two full cabin decks. And depending on the interior design, it can carry anywhere from 450 to 850 passengers at a time, for flights of more than 15 hours. A bigger plane with more passengers also means a heavier plane. We've tested the aircraft. A take-off weight is over 1.3 million pounds. That's the highest take-off weight of any commercial airplane. Getting that much weight off the ground requires wings with a surface area about the size of two professional basketball courts.
