<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It changed the way the world eats and revolutionized the food industry. It keeps armies alive and your beer cold. We use it nearly 460 billion times a year to preserve everything from peaches to plutonium. Yet we almost never think about it. An unsung essential of modern life. Now, canning on Modern Marvels. Canning. It's the most important advance in the preservation of food in history. There are aluminum cans for your beer, tin-plated cans for your beans. If you drink it or eat it, it probably comes in a can. Pop, chop, or peel, the technology of opening cans has changed over the years in surprising ways. Some cans keep your food safe, while others keep you safe. And although the essential can design hasn't changed much over the decades, it's still evolving, even going so far as to cook or cool your food for you. you. Two of the newest technologies we're developing are ways to produce containers that can heat and cool beverages automatically. In the first case, a self-heating can uses chemistry to heat the contents. There are two reagents inside this container. There's a little bit of lime and a little bit of water. To activate it, the consumer presses a button on the base that breaks the seal, mixing the water and the lime, which starts the reaction. As those two reagents mix, the consumer will slowly turn the can, just allowing some mixing to occur. And in three minutes, the beverage is about 70 to 80 degrees hotter, so about 150 degrees, which would be a good drinking temperature for a cup of coffee. This is a self-chilling container, and it uses a completely different approach."*
