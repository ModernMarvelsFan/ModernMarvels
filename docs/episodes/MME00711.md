> *"Modern Marvels is going big and counting down. No series has celebrated man-made wonders over so many episodes like we have. Now it's time to count down our top ten marvels from the world of food. It's America's favorite foods. Now on this edition of Modern Marvels Mega Marvels Countdown. Modern Marvels has covered so many lip-smacking selections from the world of food that ranking them is harder than resisting them. From supersized to bite-sized, from industrial production to the homemade, these edible wonders have all got their share of surprising backstories and jaw-dropping secrets. We figure the next best thing to tasting your favorite treat We'll be finding out its place in our countdown as we gobble our way from number 10 to the tastiest one of them all at number one. Top of the list, it's a finger licking guilty pleasure that Americans eat by the bucket board. First on our countdown at number 10, it's fried chicken. Americans chow down on more than 24 billion pounds of fried chicken every year. My personal motto is everything's better fried. And if you'd rather not do the frying yourself, this fella's franchise will gladly do it for you. KFC. Since the 1930s, the Sanders Cafe in Corbin, Kentucky has fried up what Colonel Harlan Sanders considered the perfect fried chicken. His top secret recipe of 11 herbs and spices has remained unchanged. When it came to the frying itself, the Colonel defied tradition. He started out fried in skillets. Then he went to the pressure cooker. He cooked about 18 pieces of chicken for 9 minutes. The Colonel was one of the first to fry chicken under pressure. This method led to the creation of the pressure fryer, which uses pressure to speed up the cooking process."*
