> Winter Warriors fight not just an armed enemy. They battle against the fury of Mother Nature, crippling frostbite, starvation, and malfunctioning equipment. From the devastating Russian winter, the Battle of the Bulge, the frigid mountains of Korea, and the icy waste of Alaska, the danger of battle pales against the struggle to survive the cold. Now, Winter Warriors on Modern Marvels. Today's armies train soldiers to fight in the coldest temperatures on earth, preparing them for the unique challenges of winter warfare. In battles fought in freezing weather, soldiers will suffer warfare at its worst, fighting not just a savage enemy, but murderous weather and temperatures that regularly drop to 30 degrees below zero. Frostbite sets in on immediate exposure to fierce cold. It can lead to gangrene and the loss of extremities. For a soldier unprotected in sub-zero conditions, the cold can kill the body almost as fast as a well-aimed bullet.
