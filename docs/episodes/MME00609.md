### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It strikes without warning and kills in an instant. And the 24 exit wounds can stop my heart. It can shatter a plane, drive a train, and even raise the dead. When you need a shark, you need it now. Few forces on Earth compare to the raw power of an electric jolt. The lethal dose only has to be there for a fraction of a second in order to cause death. So be afraid, very afraid, because now it's most shocking on Modern Marvels. The dangers of electric shock are 100% real. Rarely do you see it coming, and never is mercy shown. Yet corral its deadly power, and electric shock can be shaped into ingenious, sometimes controversial tools. Perhaps none more so than the products made at Taser International, manufacturers of the leading non-lethal weapon technology in the world. Today, CEO Rick Smith has something to prove. He's just been completely immobilized by the shocking power of a police-issued Taser X-26. His point? That despite recent controversy, this weapon is as safe as it is effective. I've been hit with it seven times myself. It's not fun. It's very uncomfortable. It's scary because you can't control your own body. But the moment it shuts off, you're right back to normal. Pain is not what makes the taser work. And the goal is to incapacitate, then subdue quickly. Not harm. Before taser, the only other choice I have would be no tools and try and take you down physically."*
