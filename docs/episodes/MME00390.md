> In 1931, it was the biggest bridge in the Big Apple. Today, it's grown to become the busiest in the world. It's the unfinished masterpiece across the Hudson River and one of the main arteries that keeps the heart of New York City pumping. Now, the George Washington Bridge on Modern Marvels. The George Washington Bridge was the longest in the world at thirty five hundred feet in length the main suspension span was more than double the distance of the previous record holder the ambassador bridge in detroit michigan the two-level george washington referred to by locals is the g w impacts the lives of thousands of motorists and consumers call new york We have about 330,000 vehicles a day and over 550,000 people per day. And it really is nothing more than an intersection between two states. And it's important to the commerce of the two states, not only people getting to work, but about 30,000 trucks a day that have to get the freight and goods from the ports to New England and to elsewhere in the entire Northeast. So the George Washington Bridge is an incredibly important link to the economy of the entire New York metropolitan region. To keep up with the tremendous traffic burden, the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, charged with running the bridge, has to constantly maintain the 14-lane hanging highway. In 2002, the Port Authority began one of the largest projects in the bridge's history. At a cost of nearly $70 million, the bridge is getting a new paint job.
