### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Deep Freeze on Modern Marvels."*

> Extreme cold. It keeps ice cream frozen and safeguards crops for centuries. It's been referred to as the doomsday vault. Sub-zero technologies that preserve, destroy... That's a perfect way to dice the onion. ...and even clean. Supercool cables that provide supersized energy. The coldest of liquids feed the greatest controlled fires and keep cadavers chilled out in hopes of future revival. As the blood comes out, the patient cools very rapidly. Now, Deep Freeze on Modern Marvels. Thinking no extreme cold? Think again. Ultra-cold temperatures are shaping our modern world. A world where metal levitates and rubber shatters at minus 320 degrees Fahrenheit. But let's not get ahead of ourselves. Our mastery over keeping things cold is apparent in a much simpler form. Ice cream. In the small town of Lamars, Iowa, the Wells Dairy Ice Cream Plant churns out 400,000 gallons of premium brand Blue Bunny ice cream daily. Their storage freezer spans nearly 150,000 square feet and stands 120 feet high. It's one of the largest and coldest in the world. We're at the top of our giant ice cream freezer. It's 20 below, 12 stories high in the air, and we hold 53,000 pallets of ice cream in here year-round. It's so cold in here that people are only allowed to stay in here 40 minutes at a time. That's why we put in this automation to help control this for us. Working without gloves in this deep freeze warehouse could result in frostbite in as little as 20 minutes. So instead of workers using pork lids, 12 robotic farms move ice cream in and out of the Sub-Zero icebox.
