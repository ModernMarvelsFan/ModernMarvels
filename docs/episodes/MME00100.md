> *"Is it really fireproof? Crashproof? Weatherproof? Can it hold up to a sledgehammer? A ton of dynamite? A really big sneeze? Now, crash testing on Modern Marvels. We'll be right back. In the universe of crash testing, Nothing gets smashed, flipped, skidded, rolled, and generally banged up as much as cars. The government, insurance companies, consumer groups, and most of all, car manufacturers take literally thousands of shiny new vehicles each year and total them in a spectacular variety of ways. Government-band-aided frontal side and rear impact tasks are just the beginning. Car makers have staged and tested an amazing range of accident scenarios, including this test by Volvo, in which a car was actually driven off a second story roof. Cameras inside the vehicle record the plunge from the driver's point of view. In addition to crashes, of course, manufacturers have put cars through a great variety of performance tests, bouncing them along grueling roads, through water, across snow and ice."*
