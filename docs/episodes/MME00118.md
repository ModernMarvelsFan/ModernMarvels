### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Tower Bridge on Modern Marvels."*

> One of the most famous bridges in the world. An icon for the city of London for more than a century. A unique blend of modern engineering and Victorian aesthetic. Now, Tower Bridge on Modern Marvels. In today's cultural and architectural landscapes dominated by rapid change and globalization, there are a handful of monuments and buildings that stand out. They have acquired a status that goes beyond their mere form or purpose. They have become the symbols for their cities, their time, their cultures. Its long history has awarded the City of London many remarkable structures. But together with Big Ben, the famous clock tower flanking the Houses of Parliament, the most recognized symbol for this city is the Tower Bridge. Revered by Londoners, loved by tourists, the bridge on the eastern end of London's inner city is perhaps the most photographed bridge in the world. Tower Bridge is a combination of marvellous feats of engineering with a magnificent, monumental Victorian architectural structure. So the two coming together are much more than the sum of the parts, and this is what gives this Great Bridge landmark quality. Tower Bridge was called the Wonder Bridge because of the way it was automated and opened to let ships pass through. It's only when you see the machinery and you understand how it worked that you appreciate the enormity of what it was for the people that designed it in the 1890s. Although more than a hundred years old, Tower Bridge is a relative newcomer among London's bridges. The history of bridging the Thames goes back almost 2,000 years.
