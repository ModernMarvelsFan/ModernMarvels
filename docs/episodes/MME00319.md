### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Metal on Modern Marvels."*

> From this to this. From this to this. Transforming raw metal into civilization is a journey that began before the Bronze Age and takes us into a gleaming future. Human progress forged in the blacksmith's fire. Now, Metal on Modern Marvels. Brute muscle. Forged metal. Combine these two elemental forces and you've got civilization itself. The very structure of our constructed world is wrought from these basic elements. The cadence of our progress is in the measured ring of the blacksmith's hammer. Our soaring skyscrapers, our sturdy bridges and expansive ocean liners, our jet planes and rockets. You can't escape the power and necessity of metal. It is the natural material that we have reshaped into our grandest man-made structures. We have come to a point in history when metal has really infiltrated every aspect of our life. It's very difficult to avoid the simple fact that it probably is the primary material that we use to extend ourselves. Metals have characteristics that are ideal for nearly everything that makes the modern world modern. Some conduct heat or electricity. Others are easily stretched into wire. Some resist cracking and breaking. Many are shiny or exhibit enormous strength when joined with other elements. And many of them can be worked and shaped. Metal is a very resistant material. Material that I have to cajole, fight, come to compromises with, in order to manipulate, to bend it to my will, to make what I want to make with it. Metals are everywhere.
