Just bundle the right metals and caustic chemicals and you've harnessed the power to go zero to 60 in four seconds. This car is powered by 6,831 lithium-ion batteries. When you need a portable, convenient power source, count on this unassuming yet indispensable wonder to make your tech, tools, and toys surge with energy. Americans alone purchase nearly three billion dry cell batteries every year. And that doesn't take into account all the special batteries built into devices including computers and cell phones. Sure, some batteries lead a rather quiet existence, like those inside watches and clocks, providing tick after tick year after year. But others live fast and die young, like those powering smart bomb guidance systems. We all depend on batteries. But in some cases, people's lives depend on them. Dick Cheney's heart keeps on pumping, thanks to the battery inside his pacemaker. When you come down to it, just about everything requiring electrical power that isn't plugged in or attached to a power source contains a battery. From the remote control you've no doubt got clutched in your hand to the high-definition cameras recording the action for this episode of Modern Marvels. Batteries don't contain electricity, but rather the potential for electricity in the form of chemicals."*
