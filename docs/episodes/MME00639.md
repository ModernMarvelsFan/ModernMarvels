The most dangerous road of all, according to many, lies in Bolivia. Part of a highway system linking Bolivia's capital, La La Paz and Corrico. It snakes 35 miles through the towering Andes Mountains. Its official name is the North Yungas Road. But those who dare to drive it, as well as those too fearful to try, call it El Camino de la Muerte, the death road. Along this narrow strip of dirt perched over vertical drops of nearly 2,000 feet. Between 200 and 300 motorists perish each year."*
