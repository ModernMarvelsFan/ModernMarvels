### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Stink on Modern Marvels."*

> There are invisible intruders all around, making us cover our noses, gag, and even run away. I feel like someone was punching me with smell. Let's face it, life stinks. The cesspool is basically where all the waste water goes from the house. And we're taking the lid off some of the most offensive smells we encounter every day. Basically, it's just cow year and manure. To determine how much they stink. This is disgusting. And why? Nice-looking swine manure. Stink can be lethal... Hydrogen sulfide is a poison. ...or life-saving. But one thing's for sure, we're waging an all-out war against it. From B.O. to P.U. Now, Stink on Modern Marvels. This is rank. Breathe in once and you'll know. Our everyday life is an onslaught of smells. Good, bad, and somewhere in between. Everything we smell is a chemical. Different molecules arranged in countless ways to create a particular odor that our olfactory system will detect and process. Like a hot slice of pizza, or car exhaust, or week-old garbage. The human nose can detect at least 5,000 different odors, not as much as most animals, but enough to make us impressive smelling machines, especially when it comes to stink. The human nose is actually exquisitely sensitive, particularly to some odors that we would consider malodor or bad odors. Some of those odors can be smelled as little as one part per billion in air. That's why it doesn't take much of a stink for its smelly chemicals to hit us hard. That stinks. And we're going to explore some of the smelliest.
