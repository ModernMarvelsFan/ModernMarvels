### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> This sweet treat is universally loved the world over. Comes in every flavor imaginable, contains every topping and every item imaginable. It can be crunchy, creamy, chewy, gooey, even spicy. You can spoon it. You can drink it. You can put it in the dish with all your favorite toppings. I have to tell you guys, this is one of those days where I can't believe I get paid to do this, because today we're going to an ice cream factory. 98% of all US households have ice cream at home at this very moment. And in fact, the average American eats about 23 pounds of ice cream per year each. The US produces 1.5 billion pounds a year, and ice cream is an $11 billion a year industry. But ice cream is more than big business. Baskin Robbins, Dryers, Carvel, Dairy Queen, Turkey Hill, they're famous brands, but they're also part of treasured memories. And this year, your Carvel dealer makes them loaded with fudge and nuts. And you can get fudging with an ocean to serve 20 people. And today, we will answer some of your most burning ice cream questions, like, where exactly did the ice cream cone come from? How were new ice cream flavors created? And how can I get that job? Where and when can you taste ice cream at its very best? And most importantly, that delicious carton of cookie dough ice cream sitting in your freezer right now, we'll discover how it's made from start to finish. I scream for it, you scream for it, but today we get the inside scoop, because this is Modern Marvel's Ice Cream. Today I'm gonna start at the Turkey Hill Dairy.
