> *"Its golden body and frothy head have enraptured human beings for more than 6,000 years. Malt, hops, yeast and water. But add mashing and boiling, hot and cold, sweat and science. And the results are lager, pale ale, hefeweizen and porter. From microbrew to megabrew. From medieval monasteries to a pub near you. Now, brewing on Modern Marvels. If the citizens of the world of beer were to choose a capital, it might be right here, under the shaded trees of the Augustiner Beer Garden in Munich, Germany. Here, the beer is still tapped out of wooden barrels, which are rapidly drained, until the physically fit staff literally roll out the barrel again. For the waiters and waitresses who lug up to 12 one-liter glasses of beer at a time, it's quite a workout. But for the customers, it's time for the unique form of peace and contentment found only in the beer garden. The Bavarians call it gemütlich. I think the word comfortable comes closest. It's a bit more than comfortable. It's joyful. It's nice, relaxing. It's more than one meaning. We say gemütlich. And it's a wonderful break during the day to sit here and have the best beer in the world."*
