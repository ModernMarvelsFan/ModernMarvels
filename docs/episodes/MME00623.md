### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the untold story of rats on Modern Marvels."*

> They're one of the most dangerous and destructive animals on Earth, spreading deadly diseases and terrifying humankind for centuries. One's actually lurched at me, so you've got to be really careful. But they've also saved countless lives, helping to push the limits of biological science and technology. And despite all we've done to get rid of them, they're the ultimate survivors. There were so many rodents, and I felt like I was spoiling their party. Now, the untold story of rats on Modern Marvels. New York City. By day, more than 8 million people bustle through the streets. But by night, rats rule. Estimates vary wildly. from 250,000 to close to 100 million. Terminix technical specialist Bob Young's guess is towards the high end. I've walked into rooms where I actually felt out of place because there were so many rodents looking at me, and I felt like I was spoiling their party. Bob is no stranger to the danger. He's been in the rat business for more than 14 years. When I hear people tell me about the largest rodent they've ever seen, they're really fishtails. They typically range maybe half a pound to a pound, but I can tell you by no means have I seen three, four, five-pound rodents. And you'll hear people say that their eyes are glowing and that they're evil. Truly, people have a fear, an innate fear of rodents. We're in Queens, New York, going to an account, and they've been experiencing problems with Norway rats. We basically treat it, for lack of a better description, as a crime scene, and we're looking for evidence of rodent activity. And what we found here was several droppings throughout the building and also gnawing marks. The workshop is a rat's paradise. Plenty of food, water, and hiding places. As darkness falls, the nocturnal animals are starting to stir.
