### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, chemical and biological weapons on Modern Marvels."*

> They are silent, deadly, and easy to make. Launched from missiles, sprayed from aircraft, squeezed from a bottle. They kill with terrifying speed. Called the poor man's nuclear bomb, they are a growing threat. Now, chemical and biological weapons on Modern Marvels. On the morning of March 20, 1995, a new era in international terrorism began. On that day, 11 small containers of the chemical nerve gas sarin were carried by members of a fanatical Japanese cult called Aum Shinrikyo onto five different lines of the Tokyo subway. The polyethylene containers wrapped in newspaper were punctured to release their deadly contents. As the trains arrived at the stations, carload after carload of sick and terrified passengers collapsed in fits of coughing and vomiting. 5,000 people were injured and 12 died. Exposure to minute quantities of nerve gas like sarin can cause breathing difficulties, blurred vision, and severe headaches. Higher doses can cause complete loss of muscular control and death. Experts believe that were it not for impurities in the homemade nerve gas used by the Ohm Shinrikyo cult, the attack would have been much more deadly. Scientists who looked at this closely found that while it killed 12 people, they would say it was only 12 people and that we were lucky. That a pure mix conceivably could have been far more toxic and killed hundreds or even thousands of people. The good news was the group wasn't able to get it right, that it is harder. It's hard to get the dissemination right. It's hard to plan and carry out such an attack. But the bad news was that it was trying. Earlier, the Aum Shinrikyo cult had launched unsuccessful attacks in Japan with a biological warfare agent, anthrax bacteria. They had also traveled to Zaire in an unsuccessful attempt to obtain the deadly Ebola virus.
