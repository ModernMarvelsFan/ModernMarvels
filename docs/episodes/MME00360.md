### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Gangster Guns on Modern Marvels."*

> 1920s and 30s, in the big cities and small towns, they earned a fearsome reputation in a blaze of bullets. For the criminal and lawmen alike, they were a best friend or a deadly enemy. A pull of a trigger could end a life of violence or give birth to infamy. Now, Gangster Guns on Modern Marvels. John Dillinger Pretty Boy Floyd Babyface Nelson Al Capone Bonnie and Clyde The names evoke images of outlaw whiskey, bank holdups, and bloody shootouts. The guns they used were just as notorious. Colt .45s and .38s, the Tommy gun, Whippet guns, the Browning automatic rifle, and the R.C. These legendary weapons are forever linked with the storied lives of the gangsters of the 1920s and 30s. Their exploits filled the headlines, but it was their firepower that brought them fame. Gangsters employed pocket guns, which could be easily concealed. It's not a terribly effective caliber. 32 is more effective than 25 was. 25 is the most common pocket gun or pocket caliber. But it beats throwing rocks. Gangsters were also the first to use automatic weapons outside of the military.
