#### Cut A Opening Narration
> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, forensic science on modern marvels."*
>
> An age-old battle. Good versus evil. Criminal versus the law. Now, a new player is balancing the scales of justice. Science. Hair follicles, micro fibers, DNA, their unmasking murderers, solving old crimes, setting innocence free. Now, forensic science on modern marvels. The modern era of crime fighting began a century ago in the consulting room of a London physician. With his medical practice failing, Dr. Arthur Conan Doyle turned to writing stories. In 1887, he invented the most famous fictional character of all time. and with him a new scientific field. Consulting detective Sherlock Holmes deciphered physical clues, a revolutionary approach to solving crimes. What do you think? There's been murdered done. The murderer was a man. He was in the prime of life, six feet in height, has small feet wear square-toed boots, limped slightly on the left foot, and smokes Trichinopley cigars. The early police work was totally dependent on informers. And then Arthur Conan Doyle came along, and he said, you don't need informers. You don't need to hit people over the head with rubber pipes to make them confess. They're going to leave evidence behind. Once Sherlock Holmes showed what was possible, police officers at Scotland Yard followed his lead. Forensic science was born. 19th century policemen struggled with the difficulty of reliably identifying individuals, photography, body measurements, even branding offenders. All these methods fell short.
