### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, commercial fishing on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Forget about the rod and reel. Commercial fishermen mean business. Often they bet their lives on this high-stakes game. With the latest high-tech electronics and strong and sophisticated nets, landing the catch is an exercise of deadly efficiency. But are these boats cleaning out the seas? Is there a better way? Now, commercial fishing on Modern Marvels. In Alaska's Bering Sea, monstrous ships stalk schools of fish with precision and mechanical muscle. At 376 feet, the Alaska Ocean is one of the largest commercial fishing vessels on Earth. But in the North Pacific, size takes a back seat to high technology. The ship's bridge contains the latest in navigational gear and a series of monitors that display the cold, calculated hunt for fish. Sometimes you can get a hundred tons of fish inside of five or ten minutes. Every piece of electronics we've come to rely heavily on and you hate to see any of them go out. It's called fish finding technology and it's revolutionized commercial fishing. The main advantages are if you can get out and find a fish, you're saving fuel, you're saving time, you're saving wear and tear on your boat. It was World War II that opened the door on finding fish. The sonar developed to hunt enemy submarines is now making fishermen more efficient, a key to survival in a heavily regulated business."*
