> *"It makes our cities glow, our factories hum, and our telecommunications networks buzz. With the urgency of a telegram and the force of an electric shock, copper helped catapult the world into the modern era. But this superconductor sparked some bitter battles between the two tycoons who wanted to control it. Now, the Copper Kings on Modern Marvels. In the remote regions of the Rocky Mountain West, two men controlled nearly all the country's copper production. One was a ruthless banker known for preying on the misfortune of miners. The other, a self-made man with a legendary knack for knowing where to dig. Both created huge empires and lived like kings, but they fought a ferocious and often personal battle that lasted nearly 25 years. A battle that stretched from the cramped mine shafts thousands of feet below the earth's surface to the majestic dome that towers over the nation's capital. In the early 1860s, copper was the last thing on the minds of miners in Montana or anywhere else. Gold fever was raging. Caught and spread by the California 49ers, it had become a national epidemic. Fortune hunters by the thousands risked their lives crossing the great plains and perilous Rocky Mountains, chasing a dream and a fortune. Of the 100,000 miners who poured into California, most came up empty-handed, but some struck it rich, and theirs were the stories that made it back east. The California rush of 1849 really made America go gold crazy."*
