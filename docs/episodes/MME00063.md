### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Skyscrapers, a race toward the heavens on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"The Sears Tower, the World Trade Center, the Empire State Building, the tallest buildings in the world. What sends them soaring so high? A heady mixture of glass and steel, high wire act and ego, a dash of manifest destiny, and the lure of money, lots of money. Now, Skyscrapers, a race toward the heavens on Modern Marvels. The defining image of urban America is the city skyline, a team of big-shouldered friends. They're pushy, at times inspired, and there's never been anything like them, ever. The skyscraper is and was born in America. It's grown up in America. And in many respects, it's really a symbol of an American century and the triumph of capitalism and really of American values. A skyscraper is a tall, commercial building with an internal steel skeleton support system. The external walls of a skyscraper do not support its structure. But not all tall buildings are skyscrapers. Towers are also tall buildings made of steel, but towers are not built to provide places for people to live and work. The concept of constructing tall buildings is not a new one. You know, the Bible has the story of the Tower of Babel, an example of human pride that takes us too far. What's the phrase? Pride goeth before the fall."*

</details>
