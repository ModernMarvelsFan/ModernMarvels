A vista into a world of wondrous ideas Signifying man's achievement A step into the future With predictions of constructive things to come Enter a make-believe world spanning some 27,000 acres and brought to life by cutting-edge technology. What was once Swampland now boasts the world's largest theme park. The ride technology ranges from space-age centrifuges to enhanced motion vehicles powered by 3,000 PSI of hydraulic pressure. And hundreds of audio animatronics live through the power of pneumatics, hydraulics and electrical systems. Our technology in the field of robotics really is evolutionary beginning 40 years ago and continues today building on that. Walt Disney World is made up of four separate theme parks, each with its own innovations. The first, the 107-acre Magic Kingdom, was born in secrecy when Disney purchased thousands of acres of land anonymously. The Magic Kingdom conceals complex tunnels that are the invisible arteries of a park. Transportation, costuming, computer controls, and even a high-speed garbage collection system are all hidden 14 feet below ground.
