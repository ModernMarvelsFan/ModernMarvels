### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, military movers on Modern Marvels."*

> 150 million dollar C-17 cargo planes. 87 ton hovercraft Elkacs. A worldwide supply network answerable only to the Department of Defense. The military's awesome transport tools. They can speed men and materiel to any war zone on Earth. Now, military movers on Modern Marvels. The stage is set. The fighting ensues. No matter what war, no matter what battle, no matter what era, this will be the climax of the drama. But this is act three of the play. In order to wage and sustain any war, much work has to be done. Any battle actually begins long before the soldiers engage, when planners determined that it was possible to arrive at the battlefield. With aircraft, land vehicles, and ships, planners supply soldiers with weapons, ammunition, food, and medicine. And they maintain that supply using military movers, Transportation designed specifically to move military cargo. The infantry fights for what they call key terrain, but to understand these battles and where they occur, you have to understand how people get to the battlefield. Military movers can range from giant aircraft carriers, sometimes used to transport troops, to heavy-duty helicopters that lift supplies into the war zone. They have included trucks, barges, flat cars, boats, even Jeeps, and Humvees. This is the top of the line for today's military movers. A 174-foot-long, 270,000-pound C-17 cargo plane, capable of traveling at over 500 miles an hour, or over 3,200 miles on a single fueling.
