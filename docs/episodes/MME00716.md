These incomparable beasts and beauties will keep you spinning. There's a ton of adrenaline going through your body. Be there at the finish line when we crown our number one pick. Now, hot rides on this edition of Modern Marvel's Mega Marvels Countdown. America has always prided itself as a nation on the go. But it's not just about getting from point A to point B. It's about having a blast along the way. We love any hot ride that'll blow back our hair, pump us up, or quicken our pulses. Only one limitation applies to all the contenders on our countdown. They've got to stay earthbound. So no air or watercraft. Other than that, anything goes. Our choice at number 10 is more than a hot ride. It's boiling hot. Jay Leno's 1909 Stanley Steamer. She keeps a century-old classic in his garage, a huge two-building complex, housing enough hot rides to cause a traffic jam. His iconic Stanley Steamer is in a class by itself. Steam cars were quite popular at the turn of the century because, don't forget, cars back in the early days, you had to crank them, and you had a magneto, and you had to set the choke, and a lot of times they'd backfire, and people would break their arms. Where steam... Well, steam technology was 100 years old by the time this car was built. So steam technology was easily understood. You heat up water, it makes steam, it pushes the piston, and you go.
