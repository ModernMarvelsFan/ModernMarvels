> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, enter and exit on Modern Marvels."*

> From ejection seats to blast doors. From hidden doors to air locks. To get out of this lab, it would be impossible for anything to get out alive. From those built for security to emergency exits. Release seat belts. Release. Come this way. This way out. Cross your arms shut. From the mass produced to the tallest. and from those that revolve to those in your own home. How many do you pass through in a day without even thinking about it? You'd be surprised at all the ways you can travel in and out. Now, enter and exit on Modern Marvels. Wherever you go, you travel through places you don't often think about. Entries and exits. From your front door to the car, to the supermarket, to the office, and back again. We dare you to count how many you pass through in a day. It may be in the hundreds. Everywhere you go, people are entering somewhere or exiting somewhere else. Those passages can be as simple as this. Others are so extreme, a team of specialists has to operate them. Massive, powerful, and imposing, these are the tallest doors in the world. At the Kennedy Space Center in Florida, they protect spacecraft from extreme weather. They are the entrances to the Vehicle Assembly Building, built in 1965 to house the Saturn V rocket that took us to the moon. Their job is to keep all the elements that we have in here in and all the bad elements out. Closing panel on. Roger that. Del DeWeese and his team operate and maintain the equipment in the Vehicle Assembly Building. The doors don't open and close without him. We're going to open up the emergency egress in High Bay 3 first, like we always do.
