### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, more gadgets on Modern Marvels."*

> Gadgets. For the most part they come and go. But a few stick around to become necessities. They are the sharp and the sharper. They keep the darkness at bay and defy the wind. They keep the beat rock steady. They fit comfortably into the palm of your hand. And they're just getting started. Now, more gadgets on Modern Marvels. Gadgets. And more gadgets. Don't be scared of them. You can always, so far, turn them off. The changes we're seeing are extraordinary. They are happening faster every day. Don't blink. The good news is, at least for the moment, we're in control. Control. That is, to a large extent, what we desire from our gadgets. Only a tiny fraction of these novelties will evolve into valued necessities. But that doesn't stop today's global high-gloss gadget trade. Billion-dollar boutiqueries have sprung up on the lowly backs of doohickeys and whatchamacallits. But when it comes to gadgets, corporate giants and market research have yet to replace the individual entrepreneur with a heart and a hunch. Gadget is a passion. Every person comes with his own product, with that product that can change his lifestyle, that can make him feel, you know, different or do things easier. Alex El Nacave, engineer and inventor, is the CEO of Gadget Universe, the mail order catalog business he founded in 1997, and that currently generates gadget sales of $40 million a year. I like the color.
