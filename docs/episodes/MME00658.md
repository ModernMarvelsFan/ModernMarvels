### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> They're the ultimate comfort food and the world's most productive crop. Mash them, bake them, freeze them. Just make sure you duck when you fire them. From the Andes to Ireland, from Idaho to outer space, they're everyone's favorite tuber. Chips, fries, even spirits, the gang's all here. So get off that couch and keep your eyes peeled, because it's the potato on Modern Marbles. Your mother always said it's the most important meal of the day. And with any good breakfast, there has to be potatoes. Few know this better than the folks at the original Pantry Cafe in downtown Los Angeles. My dad used to cook bacon, ham, potatoes. That was every morning. Reminds me of being at home. You know, you always want potatoes with breakfast. Established in 1924, the pantry serves breakfast 24-7, 365 days a year. In fact, because they never close, There's no lock in the front door. Their potatoes are fresh and the demand constant. Each spot is peeled and steamed, not boiled. That's their secret to both great breakfast potatoes and, if you're staying for lunch or dinner, the best mashed potatoes. We do country potatoes for breakfast with a grill, and then we do mashed potatoes. We do 20 cases a day, which is 1,000 pounds of potatoes. That's more than 180 tons a year. Not bad, given the entire joint only seats 60 people at a time. Everything comes with potatoes. It's comfort food. People really like comfort food. It's not fancy. It's not trendy. It's just people like it. Not only are these potatoes popular, they also pack a nutritional wallop.
