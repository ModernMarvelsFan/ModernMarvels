### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"and the sound revolution on Modern Marvels."*

> It has become a new dimension in entertainment. It pulls us into the center of the action. With explosive sounds and computer-controlled audio design, from its humble beginnings in the basements of amateurs, stereo has grown into a multi-billion dollar industry. Now, stereo. and the sound revolution on Modern Marvels. STEREO Today stereo draws us into the action. Dynamic sound sends shivers up our spine in theaters, relaxes us at home, entertains us in the car. The story of stereo is the story of invention and transformation. At first, sound reproduction was manoral, or single channel. But since we listen with two ears, two-channel or stereo sound became the goal. Stereo means three-dimensional or solid in Greek. It means that the sound field is coming from all directions around you. The sound revolution was a quest by visionaries for the perfect listening experience. It was a quest for the impossible, audio perfection. What technology is now making it possible to do is get closer and closer to capturing the reality of how that voice really sounds. In the way that I sit here and I say something, I don't really sound like this. You know, you're getting close to it, but it's still not a perfect reality. Stereo didn't debut until 1957, but it had a long history of thrilling sound breakthroughs.
