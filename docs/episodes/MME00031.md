> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Electricity will continue in a moment on Modern Marvels."*

> Today, electricity powers the globe. 1870, the world is driven by human hands, forces, and steam, and precariously lit by open flame and gas. The event that changes the world, Thomas Edison perfects the incandescent vault and lays the foundation for the age of technology. Now, the story of electric light on Modern Models. At 30, Thomas Edison was one of America's best known heroes. The creator of the phonograph, the marvel of the age. Edison lived in Menlo Park, an isolated village in New Jersey. This unimpressive bluster of shacks would become the world's first invention factory. Menlo Park was also the isolated home for Edison's young wife, Mary Stilwell. Mary was a simple young girl Edison met when she worked in one of his factories. Unprepared for her new life and often left alone as Edison worked around the clock, Mary would soon be overwhelmed by the growing shadow of her husband's legend. By 1877, creating a legend had taken its toll on Edison, too. For the first time in his life, he was exhausted and sick. He hadn't had a vacation since his marriage six years before. Only after he added the word working to vacation, did he agree to join a group of scientists on their way to Colorado to study an eclipse of the sun, Mary was left behind in Menlo Park.
