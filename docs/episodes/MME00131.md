### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, race cars on Modern Marvels."*

> 300 mile per hour fire belching monsters. Computers and space age composites. Now, race cars on Modern Marvels. And he's over! Whether it's screaming around a two-mile oval track or thundering down a quarter-mile strip at 320 miles per hour, fans love the speed, excitement, and danger of racing. There are many types of automobile racing. America's favorite are drag, stock car, and open wheel racing. Each type employs different machines. Aerodynamically tested, supercharged, reinforced, earth-shaking vehicles. They're fast, they're thrilling, and they employ the highest of high tech. In America, the IndyCar and internationally the Formula One car are the most popular open wheel race vehicles. To the casual observer, they may look the same, but in truth, they're quite different. A Formula One car is capable of doing a variety of things. You can stop at zero and then accelerate to 180, go through a tunnel, take a sharp left, take a sharp right, and they're probably the best handling cars in the world. Well, oval or Indy racing is a little bit different. The word oval obviously means you're racing at very high speeds around oval-shaped circuits, where you're prime concerned with speed. In an oval, you can achieve perfection. The most famous oval track in the world is the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, America's arena, where wheeled gladiators go head-to-head in a 500-mile battle for supremacy.
