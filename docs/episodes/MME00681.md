They've served at the side of nearly every president. They're genetically hardwired to help us tackle our toughest jobs. And when nature meets nurture, they can perform superhuman feats. I think the best way to think of my sled dogs is a well-trained team of Navy SEALs. Now, it's time to track down the amazing story of dogs on Modern Marvel. Sit. Atta boy. Go! Lie down. They can make a hundred different facial expressions, most of them with their ears. But on a deeper level, we know the most important thing there is to know about dogs. We're made for each other. They're not a species apart. They are so much closer to who we are and what we are than I would have ever imagined. The research is showing that they mimic us, that they try to figure out what we need. They may have helped us become who we are. The human-canine bond is so strong that more than 1 million dogs around the world are named as beneficiaries in people's wills."*
