> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, The Exterminator on Modern Marvels."*

> All around us, a battle is raging. A battle for control of the planet. The occupying army buzzes and skitters, and some of them are out for blood. But in this war, we have our own soldiers, armed with the best weapons available, charged with stemming an onslaught of tiny adversaries. These warriors of the basement, garden, and field Fight for the dominance of our own species. Now, The Exterminator on Modern Marvels. models. They're hiding in your home, under your bed, in your kitchen. They're everywhere. A female rat can drop a litter today and be pregnant again to have it tomorrow. Cockroaches are nasty. Cockroaches carry diseases. They're dirty. Whenever there is a sanitation issue, you will always find cockroaches. Since man first met past, exterminators have been fighting a rogues gallery of nefarious nuisances. Though techniques improve as history unfolds, The aim has remained unchanged. To control a menace, stifle the aggressors, and at times, kill or be killed. Consider the mosquito. Point of contact, the skin. Known dangers, yellow fever, malaria, and West Nile virus. The mosquito is well engineered to sneak up on its prey and administer a stealthy bite. The male mosquito only feeds on plant juices and doesn't feed that much. The female needs a blood meal because she needs the protein to lay eggs. When they bite, they inject a local anesthetic and an anticoagulant. So you don't feel them biting and it causes the blood to flow.
