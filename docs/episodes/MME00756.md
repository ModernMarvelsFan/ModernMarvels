### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the greatest movie gadgets, next on Modern Marvels."*

> Movie and gadget lovers beware. If you're into the high-tech stuff of dreams you see in theaters, buckle your belts for a thrill ride beyond the big screen into reality. Super toys from fictional films actually exist and may soon come to a retailer near you. Cars that fly and drive themselves to spiffy spy tools with X-ray vision. Now, the greatest movie gadgets, next on Modern Marvels. What's that? Some sort of a war tank. Think for a moment about the coolest stuff you could ever imagine. Movie makers have been doing it for years. Make that decades. Motion picture serials that played in theaters 50 years ago were way ahead of their time. I still can't believe that this little machine can predict earthquakes. Not only predicts, but prevents them. One imaginative director, who must have been an ancestor of film director James Cameron, came up with the idea of a futuristic submarine exploring worlds unknown. We're down 2,500 feet. Good, another 2,000 feet and I can stop the groundbreaking rain. Oh, you're all mad. If we go any deeper, the submarine will crush like the shell of an egg. Cameron took the idea to new depths in the abyss. Touchdown! In British Columbia, a world-renowned undersea explorer is doing it for real. We're trapped! Another filmmaking magician conjured up the notion of the gun of all guns. At the time, the weapon was called a disintegrator. In the fifth element, it was the ZF-1. In field tests being conducted right now, it's the XM-29. We're taking off. And what would the world of movie-dom be without all those fabulous vehicles that fly?
