> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, tobacco on Modern Marvels."*

For starters, it's adaptable. It would be very hard to find a place, short of Antarctica or the Arctic, where it isn't cultivated. It's profitable. The tobacco industry overall every year pays somewhere in the neighborhood of $100 billion just in taxes. From the seed through the cigarette and into the local municipal state in federal taxes. It's a killer. Smoking is the single largest preventable cause of death in the United States. Tobacco is the only product, if used as directed, will kill you. And it's wildly successful. Whether grown for cigars, pipes, cigarettes, or smokeless tobacco products, six million tons of tobacco are produced each year, with the U.S. ranking fourth in production behind China, India, and Brazil. Tobacco is the largest non-food crop which is grown in the world today.
