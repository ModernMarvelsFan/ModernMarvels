> *"What does it take to turn 20 tons of steel into finely crafted automobiles in less than a day? Or to transform an empty foundation to a new home in just six hours? Every process is a one-of-a-kind journey beginning to end. From the fiery birth of high-tech golf clubs to the cataclysmic end of an old building. If a manufacturer doesn't respond to that consumer pull, they're going to be out of business and other people are going to take their market. The faster you can get something to the consumer, the more likely they are to buy your product over somebody else's. Modern business has to put the pedal to the metal. The fuel? Incredible innovations. All engineered to get us from start to finish in record time. Using the automobile as a kickoff point, 100 years ago people just wanted some kind of better, faster transportation. And in 1908, Henry Ford delivered with his mass-produced Model Ts.
