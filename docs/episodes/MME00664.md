### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Next on Modern Marvels."*

> What does it take to turn 20 tons of steel into finely crafted automobiles in less than a day? Or to transform an empty foundation to a new home in just six hours? Every process is a one-of-a-kind journey beginning to end. From the fiery birth of high-tech golf clubs to the cataclysmic end of an old building. Get ready, because we're going from start to finish on Modern Marvel. The world's longest construction project, clocking in at 2,000 years, is China's Great Wall. It's a timeless reminder that getting from start to finish has often been a long, slow journey. But in today's world, slow is not an option. Getting from start to finish is a race, and no one wants to be left behind. If a manufacturer doesn't respond to that consumer pull, they're going to be out of business and other people are going to take their market. The faster you can get something to the consumer, the more likely they are to buy your product over somebody else's. Modern business has to put the pedal to the metal. The fuel? Incredible innovations. All engineered to get us from start to finish in record time. Using the automobile as a kickoff point, 100 years ago people just wanted some kind of better, faster transportation. And in 1908, Henry Ford delivered with his mass-produced Model Ts. Tin Lizzy's were built by the thousands on the assembly line. Back then, it was a revolutionary process. But not even Henry Ford could have predicted how things would change. Kia Motors Manufacturing in West Point, Georgia
