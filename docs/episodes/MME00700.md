### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, waterproof on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"From the beginning of time, we battled against the very substance that keeps us alive. Water. It's a love-hate relationship. We live by it and die by it. Through technology and ingenuity, we're always finding new ways to stay dry. From the roof over our heads, to the clothing we wear and cases we carry. From boring tunnels deep under it to building vessels that dive deep into it. This is the amazing story of staying dry. Now, waterproof on Modern Marvels. From the terrifying force of a tsunami, to the insidious seat of a hidden leak. Water can kill and destroy. Humans have long struggled to manage water by channeling it, containing it, and blocking it. Nowhere is this fight more evident than in one of the most densely populated cities in the world that's also surrounded by water. Manhattan is an island, and the fight against water here is an everyday reality for its 1.6 million residents. But it's 140 feet beneath these crowded streets that the real war against water rages on. In New York City's subway, water that seeps into tunnels has always been the number one enemy. We'll stop working on a Saturday morning, and by the time we return to work on a Monday, we'll have two to three, four feet of water in here, and that's without rain. The Metropolitan Transportation Authority is halfway through a massive subway expansion project. We have anywhere from six to eight of these bumps constantly running to retrieve the water, and we put it through a pump out into the sewer lines."*

</details>
