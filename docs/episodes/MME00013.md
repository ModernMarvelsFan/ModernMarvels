### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now sink into the world of tunnels on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They're passageways to the underworld, where evil lurks and the devil plays. Yet today we travel in them, work in them, build cities over them. We can't live without them, so we defy nature, lose fortunes, and risk lives to build them deeper and longer. Now sink into the world of tunnels on Modern Marvels. Let's go. Nobody knows exactly how many miles of tunnel there are in the world. Their combined distance has never been measured. But it's speculated that if they were all laid end to end, stretch from the Earth to the moon. Today, tunnels are often taken for granted, even though they are indispensable to the modern world. They were vital to the ancients as well. And their development can be traced from the earliest civilizations. The ancient city of Babylon was divided in half by the Euphrates River. Each portion of the city was protected from invaders by impenetrable walls. The royal palace was located on one side. Important religious shrines and temples were located on the other. In order for the royal family to visit these sites, they were forced to leave the safety of the city's fortifications and expose themselves to the ever-present danger of attack by enemy nations. Historians tell us that sometime between the years 2180 and 2160 BC, the rulers of Babylon decided to solve this problem once and for all."*
