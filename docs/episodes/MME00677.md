### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, it's the untold story of Megastores on Modern Marvels."*

> They're colossal centers of commerce. Constructed on an incredible scale, they sell everything from house paint to giant bulldozers. They employ hundreds of thousands of people, all in an effort to keep America housed, clothed, and fed. Some are so massive, they've become their own landmarks. Yeah, it's big. It's big. I mean, it's seriously huge. This store is the number one tourist attraction in the state of Missouri. But beyond the checkout lines and barcodes lies a secret world most of us never see. Like looking at an iceberg, what you see at the top is not what goes on behind the scenes. Now, it's the untold story of Megastores on Modern Marvels. From hanger-sized hardware stores to sprawling food emporians, big box stores have become a permanent fixture across the American landscape. Today, some stores are so big, they've earned their own special designation. These giants of retail are known as megastores. A megastore, or a big box retailer, is basically an operation that's about 50,000 square feet. feet and overall size. That's roughly the size of a football field. It can be independent, it could be a part of a chain, it could be whatever, but it has to be big. And if big is what you're looking for, meet one of the most unique retailers that fits the megastore definition, Ritchie Brothers. It's the megastore that makes building other megastores possible. That's because in just one year, Ritchie Brothers sells more than 300,000 pieces of construction equipment, everything from cranes and dump trucks to trenchers and front loaders. With 43 locations in 12 countries, Ritchie Brothers is the largest auctioneer of heavy machinery in the world.
