### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the untold story of packaging on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It's all around us. So much a part of our lives, we forget it's there. But just try to survive a single day without packaging. Virtually everything produced on Earth needs to be packaged. We've taken great pains to make sure that it gets up there in one piece. To sell it, ship it, protect it, or keep it fresh. High-tech companies you never knew existed invest billions to design every box, bottle, and bag you encountered, and to crank them out by the ton to meet our insatiable demand. This isn't rocket science. It's harder. And you'll never believe how much of every dollar you spend pays for the cost of it all. Now, the untold story of packaging on Modern Marvels. Packaging is the multi-billion dollar industry we all take for granted, despite the fact that we rely on it every day. One glance down the aisle at your local grocery store says it all. Every item on every shelf is in a package, and the variety is endless. There's an amazingly rich story behind the package of every product you buy. And you probably didn't even know you're a huge part of it. One dollar of every eleven you spend pays for the cost of packaging. And one third of everything you throw away consists of packaging materials. Researchers at Michigan State University have long acknowledged a truth that most of us ignore. Look at the blue one. Packaging is a science, a combination of design and engineering. Since 1952, the School of Packaging at Michigan State has set the tone and purpose for the industry."*
