#### Cut A Opening Narration
> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, the untold story of packaging on Modern Marvels."*

We've taken great pains to make sure that it gets up there in one piece. To sell it, ship it, protect it, or keep it fresh. High-tech companies you never knew existed invest billions to design every box, bottle, and bag you encountered, and to crank them out by the ton to meet our insatiable demand. And you probably didn't even know you're a huge part of it. One dollar of every eleven you spend pays for the cost of packaging. And one third of everything you throw away consists of packaging materials. Researchers at Michigan State University have long acknowledged a truth that most of us ignore. Look at the blue one. Packaging is a science, a combination of design and engineering. Since 1952, the School of Packaging at Michigan State has set the tone and purpose for the industry. Packaging serves a whole bunch of functions.
