#### Cut A Opening Narration
> **Opening Hook:** *"Now, Power Tools on Modern Marvels."*
>
> They're the tools that make work fun. Manufactured by household names and Hollywood celebrities. Easier to use and more powerful than ever. But they weren't always like this. Now, Power Tools on Modern Marvels. Hi, I'm Ron Hazleton. Welcome to the Tool Bench. You know, my dad built his own house when he was only 19 years old. and that was long before you could run out to the local mega home center to pick up a good power tool. I gained a real respect for hand tools from my dad, but I don't think I'd ever want to give up my power tools. I've gotten so used to them, it's hard to imagine a time when they weren't around. But in the long history of tools, power, well, that's just the tip of the iceberg. Beginning with primitive tools made from rocks, human civilization could be measured by how we make, use, and improve tools. From stone to copper and later iron and steel, we've been making tools for at least two and a half million years. At the very end of this spectrum, about .0004% of the tool timeline are power tools. The ability to motorize and electrify tools made it possible to build houses, skyscrapers, and entire communities with unprecedented speed and efficiency. They've also made using tools just a little more exciting. I think the mystique is all about power and speed. speed and I don't know that males are more interested in power and speed than females

#### Cut B Opening Narration
> Power tools put the power to change the world right in your hand. They've allowed human beings to accomplish some pretty extraordinary things. I'm talking about drills, drivers, grinders, nail guns, sanders, circular saws, man saws, and jigsaws. Power tool innovators are working extremely hard to make sure that you can do more and build more, more, more efficiently and quickly than ever before. This passion for power tools has created a marketplace that was valued globally at about $30 billion. That's enough to buy the New York Yankees more than five times over. In fact, last year, DIY sales reportedly hit an all-time high of $450 billion in the U.S. alone. That's twice the size of Major League Baseball, the NBA, and NFL combined. I want to know how they're able to pack so much powerful potential into a device that you can hold in your hand. What are the next innovations? And how do they keep pushing the limits of what one person could do with one very good tool? We'll explore the world-famous DeWalt Factory and see how they make some of the best thing. best power tools on the planet. We're going to see power tools in action on a racetrack. We'll see 150 year old tools still in use today. And we'll see how power tools are used everywhere from burning buildings to the operating room and even outer space. So strap on your tool belts. This is Modern Marvel's Power Tools. Today, I'm in Fort Mill South Carolina at the factory for Power Tool juggernaut, DeWalt. Just like the company, this DeWalt factory is huge.
