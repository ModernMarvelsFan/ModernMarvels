### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Built by Hand on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"More and more of everything we make is the product of a machine. But a special class of products can be created only by this wonder of engineering, the human hand. In a world that values robotic precision and efficiency, we still revere a keen eye, nimble fingers, and a craftsman's intellect. Every aspect of this company is hands on. If you demand something truly unique, you've got to go old school. I'm a tailor for people. I just work in metal. So roll up your sleeves and limber up. Now, Built by Hand on Modern Marvels. We've spent billions trying to replicate it, and with each passing decade, we edge a little closer. But no device yet invented matches the precision and versatility of the amazing tool that's just an arm's length away. The human hand is a marvel of bioengineering, typically made up of 27 bones, hundreds of ligaments, tendons, and muscles, and millions of nerves. No other appendage in the animal kingdom is like it. We all know that humans as well as primates have an opposable thumb. But what makes us unique is the fact that we have a precision grip, the ability to touch our thumb to our pinky as well as our ring fingers. And that's what makes the human hand such an incredible tool. And this incredibly dexterous tool still reigns as the most effective means to craft some products that demand a finesse that no machine can manage. Some are even willing to bet their lives on the handiwork of others, like New Mexico glider pilot Bob Carlton. You know, this plane is hand-built."*
