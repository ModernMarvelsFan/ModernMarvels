### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Salt Mines on Modern Marvels."*

> . Blasted by dynamite and carved by mechanical titans deep beneath the Earth's surface. Huge underground caverns and cathedrals used to store everything from classic films to secret documents. From them we extract a crystal that's vital to our very existence. Now, Salt Mines on Modern Marvels. Salt. It's a simple substance, as ordinary as it is plentiful. But it's an invaluable resource with more than 14,000 uses. from de-icing our roads to softening our water. It's within each and every one of us, an indispensable part of our biological chemistry. And it stands alone as the only rock that we eat. There's a tremendous amount of salt in the Earth, especially in the United States. Eons in the past, North America was covered by prehistoric seas. 150 million years ago, when the seas evaporated, they left vast beds of salt, salt that was eventually covered by layers of sediment. Part of the enormous deposit lies 1,800 feet directly beneath Lake Erie. Workers at Cargill De-Icing Technologies Salt Mine in Cleveland, Ohio are busy tapping it. The salt that we're mining today is 400 million years old. And our mine itself is out underneath the lake. How do you keep the lake out of the mine? We've got 1,200-foot of limestone that protects the mine from the lake bottom. We actually go out three miles offshore, and we extend across the face of the city of Cleveland
