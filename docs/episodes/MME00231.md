### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the Manhattan Project on Modern Marvels."*

> To make peace, scientists built the ultimate weapon of war. To fight fascism, they unleashed the power of the atom. Theoretical brilliance and engineering grit gave birth to an ethical nightmare, the atom bomb. Now, the Manhattan Project on Modern Marvels. At 5.30 a.m., July 16, 1945, scientists and dignitaries awaited the detonation of the first atomic bomb in a desolate area of the New Mexico desert, known aptly as Jornada del Muerto, or Journey of Death. They had taken bets on how much power their creation might unleash, but many wondered whether the weapon would work at all. There was, however, one certainty. If the atomic bomb detonated, the world would never be the same. This story is the great tragic epic of the 20th century. If I were going to give it a theme, the theme would be humankind invents the means of its own destruction. And in order to do so, scientists, engineers, and the army teamed up in an effort dubbed the Manhattan Project. In the short span of two years, they built an industrial complex with sites across the country country that would rival the size of the automotive industry. To manufacture fuel for the bomb, they built the fifth largest town in the state of Tennessee that consumed one-tenth of the electrical power generated in the United States. The bomb builders spent well over $2 billion, almost $30 billion in today's market, to detonate the first man-made atomic explosion just 28 months after scientists set foot in a at the Manhattan Project's Los Alamos, New Mexico facility. Nonetheless, that July morning in 1945, many of the world's top physicists watched anxiously at the test site 200 miles south of Los Alamos, awaiting the results of their labors. Five, four, three, two, one.
