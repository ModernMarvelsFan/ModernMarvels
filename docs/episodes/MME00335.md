### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the FBI Crime Lab on Modern Marvels."*

> The nerve center of America's fight against crime and terrorism. Futuristic equipment. Cutting-edge technologies. Now, the FBI Crime Lab on Modern Marvels. In April of 2003, many of the world's greatest forensic scientists witnessed the dedication of a building that promises to be in the vanguard of American law enforcement. The new FBI crime lab at Quantico, Virginia. Our dedication theme states, behind every case is a victim, a man, a woman, or a child, and the people who care for them. We dedicate our efforts and the new FBI laboratory to all of these victims. At nearly 500,000 square feet, the lab has almost tripled the size of its former home at FBI headquarters in Washington, D.C. This state-of-the-art facility cost over $150 million to build and equip. Its intricate construction took four years to complete. It is, most experts agree, the finest crime lab in the world. It has been nicknamed Battlestar Galactica, not only for its innovative technology, but also its unique design. The design is built around the integrity and security of evidence. That's the most important quality of this building, to ensure that the evidence we're handling is secure as well as contamination-free. So the building has been designed with the offices in the front part of the building, and the back two-thirds of the building is all laboratory space. The office and laboratory sections have completely separate air handling systems. ensuring evidence is kept contaminant free. Evidence is brought into the building via special handling bays, some of which are large enough to back in a tractor trailer. All of these efforts are directed towards solving crime with modern forensic science.
