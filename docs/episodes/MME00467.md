### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"This is a test recording for modern marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Electric light and power, motion pictures, recorded sound. His innovations inspired such unlikely offshoots as the mimeograph machine and the tattoo pen. Our world is his world realized, forever changed by his genius. Now, Edison Tech on Modern Marvel. May 8, 1996. A group of filmmakers risked their very lives to deliver a large format IMAX motion picture camera to the top of Mount Everest. The expedition was so dangerous that members of two climbing just above them perished in a killer storm. The idea was mine to do it. And since we finance most of our films on our own, it's my stupidity or brilliance that puts us into those binds. The camera weighed 42 pounds. Film rolls weighed 5 pounds each and could only shoot 90 seconds of action at a time. You can't get a helicopter to fly that high. The highest a helicopter could get was about 19,000 feet. You know, the mountain's 29,000 feet. And so you're not even close with a helicopter. So we had to carry it. And so when we got five strong Sherpas, the best Sherpas in Nepal, to carry the five different pieces to the camera. So we got 10 people to the top. and shot two rolls of film. And it was stunning. As the filmmakers stood atop the highest point on Earth, they knew they also stood on the shoulders of the film"*
