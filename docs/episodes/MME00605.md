### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, just in time to save the planet, Environmental Tech 2 on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They're the towering sun temples that could power the largest metropolis, and the kites that could pull gigantic ships. They're factories that clean up our messes, the cars that run on air, the smart switches, air conditioners made out of ice, and the rooftop turbines that capture the wind. What you might call a modern marvel. Hey, that's a good idea for a TV show. Now, just in time to save the planet, Environmental Tech 2 on Modern Marvels. We're burning too much carbon, which puts us in the midst of a global warming meltdown. But help is on the way. Just as we've created the problem, we're creating the solutions. We now realize that technology is actually what's going to solve this problem for us to a large degree. And it can be done on a small scale. Windmills are nothing new. But this one, on top of Jay Leno's 16,000 square foot garage, has a secret. It doesn't care which way the wind blows. I think the really cool thing about it is you're utilizing something that has always been here, the wind. You're not changing it. You're not altering it. There's no byproduct of the wind. The wind is going on 24 hours a day. And since we're right near the airport, every time a jet takes off, 100 watts! Supplying Leno with ample wattage is Phil Watkins, inventor and owner of PacWind, Incorporated. To him, harnessing the wind is not only a passion, it's the future of home energy. You can actually see the energy being made, and just kind of a thrilling experience. You know, you look up, you know the wind's blowing, and you say, OK, am I making any electricity? You can run over to the meter. You can see it happening. What's happening is wind being used to drive a turbine that produces electricity for immediate use."*
