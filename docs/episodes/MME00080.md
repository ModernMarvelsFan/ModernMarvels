### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the quest for health on Modern Marvels."*

> Shaping Up. It's one of the most enduring human obsessions. We'll do almost anything to get from here to here. Fitness gurus like Jack LaLanne and Jane Fonda made it a mission and an industry. Now, the quest for health on Modern Marvels. Physical fitness. It comes in as many shapes and sizes as the people who pursue it. It's aggressive aerobic boxing and the fine muscle control of meditative yoga. It's a battery of machines capable of building bodies with assembly line efficiency. It's luxurious health clubs where sweating's a social phenomenon and computers click off the calories. It can be the pursuit of lifelong health or just plain strange, but its benefits cannot be denied. Keeps you alive. You handle yourself better. You can eat more food. Everything about you is better. See, you don't get old from age. You get old from inactivity. See, the only way you can hurt this body, don't do it. Sit around on your big fat behind and think of the good old days or make some excuse why you're not doing it. While exercise technology might change, the desire or even obsession to stay fit is hardly new. Much of what drives physical fitness today originated in the earliest cradles of civilization.
