That's nearly the combined volume of coffee, beer, and bottled water consumed annually in the US. A host of flavors, eye-catching containers, and claims of invigoration continually entice us to gulp away. What exactly are soft drinks? Simply put, they're non-alcoholic liquid refreshments. I think in the United States, people tend to think of a soft drink more in the carbonated sense, so carbonated soft drinks. But in reality, it's broader than that. The carbonated soft drink category is far and away the largest beverage category in the US. People drink more carbonated soft drinks than any other beverage.
