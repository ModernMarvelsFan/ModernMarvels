### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, building a skyscraper, the skeleton, on Modern Marvels."*

> Welcome to Skyscraper 101, a truly comprehensive course in mega construction. Class objective? Construct a giant building in downtown Los Angeles. Watch the real men of steel. Connectors, hook-on men, and bolter-uppers fashion 13,000 tons of steel into the skeleton of a building. Here's what two years of blood, sweat, and swearing can do. Now, building a skyscraper, the skeleton, on Modern Marvels. If you live in a city, you drive by them every day. But you never have time to peek through the security fence. There's got to be a thousand people working on them. How else could they go up so fast? How do they make all those parts fit? Who's responsible for what? If you always wanted to know what it feels like to straddle an I-beam hundreds of feet off the ground, or to run that massive tower crane, here's your chance to find out. Because we're going to construct the giant of a building from the ground up. We'll see the step-by-step process. investigate the rich history of technological innovation that got us from there to here. Because every big building today owes a debt of gratitude to the groundbreaking structures of the past. And here, in the heart of Los Angeles, we'll meet the people who build one. Power frame operator. Project that's big. Pipe fitting. Bolts up foreman. For these hardhat warriors, it's time for their closeup. Very dangerous. Very dangerous. Dangerous enough, if you make a mistake, you could die. This is two. As the building's going up, there's bound to be some trouble. Let me spell it out to you. Grab number one right here, put number two right here, so I can get one and two in order here. Here we go.
