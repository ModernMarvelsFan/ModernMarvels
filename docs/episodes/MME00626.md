### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, most dangerous on Modern Marvels."*

> Beware. You may have just seconds to live. Some of the world's most dangerous encounters are closer than you think. Danger lurks in a long, slumbering volcano, near a major American city, and in the vortex of a massive funnel cloud at your doorstep. It has highly toxic fangs and can even paralyze you with one nasty handkerchief. with one nasty helmet-to-helmet hit. And don't forget to pull your chute. Now, most dangerous on Modern Marvels. The climactic eruption of the Philippines' Mount Pinatubo on June 15, 1991, is a jolting reminder that the world is riddled with dangerous volcanoes. The blast propelled volcanic ash 22 miles high. It killed more than 700 people, many when wet ash collapsed roofs. Massive mudflows took still more lives. It also triggered memories of far more devastating eruptions eruptions in the past, like Tambora in 1815, Krakatoa in 1883, and Mount Pelé in 1902. But Pinatubo is far from the most dangerous volcano in the world today. Many volcanologists say it's Italy's Mount Vesuvius. Its last devastating eruption was in AD 79, which obliterated of Pompeii and Herculaneum. Today, Naples and its surrounding communities, population 3 million, sit on Vesuvius' front porch. Geologic evidence suggests it's primed for a repeat. But what is the most dangerous volcano in the United States?
