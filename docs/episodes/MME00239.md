> The gun has played a critical role in history, an invention which has been praised and denounced, served hero and villain alike, and carries with it moral responsibility. To understand the gun is to better understand history. In the world of modern firearms, the future is closer than you might imagine. The marriage between sophisticated technology and tradition has brought gun makers to the brink of a new frontier. The products that can come out of this are really limited by the imagination of the engineering personnel that are assigned to the project working on it. If we think we can do a ray gun, we probably can do a ray gun. It's just going to take a little more technology, a little more time. I think what we will see will be guns that fire bullets that will be able to go over top of barricades. they'll explode over top of barricades and they will strike the bad guys. I haven't been told to go build a lightsaber yet, but I'm waiting for that. Since the first crude firearms were forged by hand centuries ago, the goal has been the same. Make lighter, more durable, and smarter guns that can fire faster, farther, and with more accuracy. Ironically, in the next century, the goal will also be to make some guns less deadly.
