### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Icebreakers on Modern Marvels."*

> They are the toughest ships in the water, plowing headlong into one of nature's hardest obstacles. They call the most desolate places on Earth home, as they keep ships safe and open the frozen reaches of the planet to scientific study. Now, Icebreakers on Modern Marvels. Ice. One of nature's most formidable barriers. In the channels and shipping routes of the world, it can bring transportation to a dead stop. This is the adversary of the icebreaker. Specially designed ships with reinforced hulls and massive power to shatter winter's frozen domain. Wherever ice gets in the way of maritime commerce or scientific research, ice-breaking ships are called on to open the way. They do this by grooming channels, creating lanes through which ships follow. Other times they get up close and personal by winching the bow of a beset vessel into a specially designed padded notch and using the power of both ships to force through the frozen hardback. It is late January on the Great Lakes. A smooth, undisturbed layer of white covers the entire landscape. At this time of year it is difficult to tell where the shore ends and the water begins.
