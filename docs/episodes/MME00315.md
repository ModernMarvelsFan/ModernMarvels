> *"They are part of a new and developing arsenal. Please wait. Countermeasures designed to answer the looming threat of terror against corporate targets. Threats that cannot be specified or even seen until the damage has begun. They are breathtaking structures, titans that can rise more than 100 stories from the ground and contain upwards of a million square feet. Among the greatest monuments to wealth, commerce, and capitalism, they are the symbol of corporate America. Ever since the first skyscrapers were erected in the 1930s, these towers were heralded as efficient and centralized places to concentrate and conduct business in the modern world. But in the days following 9-11, corporate high-rise buildings everywhere emerged in the public eye for another reason, as a prime target for large-scale terrorist attack. To defend these giants and the people who work in them, an army of scientists, engineers, and security advisors have begun a vital mission, to develop the web of technologies that will come together to make corporate America safe safe from terrorist attack. Some rely on complex biological sensors, marvels that can sniff out traces of anthrax or explosives and give warning in time to save lives. Others use artificial intelligence to recognize the face of terror from a database of millions and sound the alarm. Some rely on brute strength and remarkable engineering to make sensitive targets safe from attack, at the moment it counts. When all else fails, there are extreme life-saving measures,"*
