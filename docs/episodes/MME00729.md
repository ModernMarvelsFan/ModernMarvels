### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> America is on the move in the most epic way. The United States has invested as much as $1.6 trillion a year in logistics, which is kind of a fancy way of saying transport. That's trillion with a capital T. But that's not the only number related to America in Motion that breaks my brain. Do you realize that 430 million pieces of mail are delivered every single day? Public transport makes 10 billion trips every year. There are freight trains that are three miles long. There are cargo ships that are 1,300 feet long. That's like a floating Empire State Building. But these modern, massive marvels that breathe life and give oxygen to our economy, they're not just brute beasts. Every year, they continue to advance, to innovate, becoming more aerodynamic, more intelligent, and they're always full of surprises. Today, I'm at the Daimler Truck Manufacturing Center in Cleveland, North Carolina. It's spread over 1.2 million square feet. We'll get an inside look at how 1,700 workers build their Freightliner trucks that are essential to our economy and ship nearly everything across the country. Oh, and did I mention this is the first time in history that Daimler has ever let anyone inside their factory to film? Then we'll see how one company is making a flying machine that costs the same as a high-end pickup truck. Plus, we'll get a glimpse of what transport was like in the past and what it could be in the future. How will the latest technology allow us to go further faster than ever before? I'm going to find out. So grab your seat, and let's get the truck out of here. This is Modern Marvel's Moving America. America.
