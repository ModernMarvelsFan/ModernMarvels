### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"It's Sticky Stuff on Modern Marvels."*

> Think you know sticky stuff? Well think again. Sure it's an office staple, but can it really build a skyscraper? Rip it, smear it, stretch it. Just don't step in it, cause this stuff burns. From the roads we drive to the air we breathe, everything's coming up sticky. So stick around. It's Sticky Stuff on Modern Marvels. Before there was man or history, there was sticky stuff. It seeped and oozed. It even nourished. 40,000 years after Neanderthals first used tar to affix points to spears, scientists and engineers still look to solve the riddle of why stuff sticks and then apply those answers to everyday problems. Scientifically, things stick for lots of reasons, But few things have proven as effective as a tape that many consider the strongest in the world. It's called VHB tape, created by the 3M company. This is our slammer demo. And what we're going to do here is we're going to impact a 16 pound bowling ball into two aluminum sheets that have been bonded together with a VHB tape. And we'll see how long the VHB tape lasts. You can see that it did eventually fail, but it did take a lot of impacts. In fact, that's 10 times stronger than rivets, using the same test. Now you can see, after just two impacts, how the mechanical fasteners pulled through the aluminum.
