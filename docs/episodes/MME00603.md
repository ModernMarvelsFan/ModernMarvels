### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the world's sharpest on Modern Marvels."*

> They're a cut above. They can split a single hair or a head of lettuce, lacerate entire trees in the blink of an eye, carve through hard steel, or edge it with delicate precision, chopping, slicing, cutting, or biting. Watch your fingers. Now, the world's sharpest on Modern Marvels. What is the world's sharpest? Is it the finest edge? Is it the ease by which one material can slice or puncture another? Or is it something that won't dull with repeated use? The answer, of course, depends on what needs to be cut. This gracefully curved sword is called the katana. It rose to distinction in 16th century feudal Japan, wielded by the legendary samurai warriors. They alone were permitted to carry the katana, and its mystique grew during an era of civil war as they used it to die the katana. and dismember their enemies. It is renowned as the world's sharpest weapon for slicing through human flesh and bone. This tough as bone bamboo shoot proves too difficult to cut through in a single swipe for a hatchet or a machete. But one swing by the katana blade unerringly severs the chute. Or even two at once.
