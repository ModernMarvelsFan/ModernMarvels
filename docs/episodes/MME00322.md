### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the Overseas Highway on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"More than a hundred miles of highway across the sea. A freeway of spectacular bridges, including one of the longest in the world. An engineering feat once thought impossible. A road like no other, through the paradise of the Florida Keys. Now, the Overseas Highway on Modern Marvels. At the tip of Florida, the southernmost part of the continental United States, lies the overseas highway. It is 119 miles long and begins just south of Miami off the coastal edge of the peninsula. It then winds its way in a southwesterly direction, over the Florida Keys, and ends in the city of Key West. Anyone who's ever been on the overseas highway from Miami to Key West will tell you it's one of the most amazing drives on Earth, where you're really on a spider's web of trail across the ocean. What sets this highway apart from other roadways is that so much of it is over water, roughly 20%. It features an astonishing 51 bridges. Running parallel to several of these bridges are remnants of a century-old railway. Today's modern overseas highway owes its legacy to it. The old bridges are now used as fishing piers and bike and footpaths. Next to one of these rail bridges stands one of the newest structures on the overseas highway, Seven Mile Bridge. The bridge is more than 35,000 feet long and spans 6.7 miles of water. It is one of the longest of its kind in the world and among the most spectacular."*

</details>
