We rely on rocks to make soil to grow plants. At one time, we used them for weapons. They season our food and add sparkle and wealth to our lives. If you're operating a computer, the silicon chips that make up an important part of that computer come from rocks. The Earth is one huge ball of rock 25,000 miles around and over 4.5 billion years old. But a question, what are the most valuable rocks on Earth? They very well may be NASA's collection of lunar rocks, located at the Lyndon Johnson Space Center near Houston. They're priceless, because we can't bring any more back"*
