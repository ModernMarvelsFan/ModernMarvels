### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"On a scale that amazes even today, now a saga of power, the Grand Coulee Dam on modern marvels."*

> 1933, the depression, a nation desperate for work. The answer, build a dam, a gigantic dam. On a scale that amazes even today, now a saga of power, the Grand Coulee Dam on modern marvels. The Land Do some work for me. Roll Columbia, won't you roll, roll, roll. Roll Columbia, won't you roll, roll, roll. In 1941, folk singer Woody Guthrie sang the praises of the Grand Coulee Dam. His simple lyrics were the first attempt to set that mighty structure in a human perspective. That's a challenge because there's nothing about the dam, the Columbia River, or the gorge of Cooley that is not of epic proportions. A half century after its construction, the Grand Cooley Dam is still one of the largest concrete structures on earth. It seems almost too huge to have been made by man. But beneath its awesome exterior lies a beguiling simplicity. The Grand Coulee Dam is a working modern marvel that lives up to its promise. It is a monument to the power of nature, the will of a nation, and the rugged men who built it. The Columbia River begins as melting snow
