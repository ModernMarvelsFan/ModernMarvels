### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"High Voltage will continue on Modern Marvels."*

> They perform an awesome high wire act. Only these wires rage with deadly currents. Their mission is to keep the lights on every minute of every day. For over a century, they've climbed poles, scaled towers, and handled live wires. They're an elite group of courageous workers known as electrical linemen. It's high voltage on modern marbles. These are 500,000 volt power lines. Wires that carry enough electricity to light an entire city. Countless miles of lines like these move electricity from power plants to population centers. There, a complex network of substations, transformers and smaller wires distribute the essential fuel for modern life. Electricity travels at the speed of life, an ever-ready flow of invisible energy. But there is a fundamental principle of electricity that is constantly working to disrupt this system. the principle that makes electric current so dangerous. Electricity always looks for the easiest path to the ground. It normally travels smoothly along the power lines because insulators keep the wires from touching paths to the ground, such as poles or towers. But sometimes a storm tears down a wire, or an earthquake topples a tower, or a person accidentally creates a path between the ground and a live wire by touching both at the same time. And then, in an instant, electricity chooses a deadly new route. Electricity is very unforgiving. It doesn't care if it goes through a copper wire or a human being. It just wants to get down to the ground. It wants to get back to the earth. It's not particular which path it takes. It takes the easiest path, and sometimes that can be through your body.
