### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Great Trains Plus on Modern Marvels."*

> It's the greatest freight transportation system in the world. A complex network woven into the very fabric of the United States. From the early iron horses to today's sleek steel stallions. They grind up hillsides, sneak through valleys, thunder across prairies. to deliver the goods. Now, Great Trains Plus on Modern Marvels. Welcome to North Platte, Nebraska, home of the largest rail yard in the world. Union Pacific's Bailey Yard. The yard is approximately 3,500 acres. It's 8 miles long, about 2 miles wide. No other rail facility like it in the world. Rail yards are the hubs of railroad operations. Here, freight cars from across the system are funneled in, sorted, and then reassigned to new trains that will bring them closer to their final destination. Just about anything that's produced in the United States is shipped by rail. So if you're talking about wine or cheese, fresh vegetables, televisions, radios, automobiles, automobile parts, you name it, just about everything that America uses on a daily basis comes through North Platte. The Bailey Yard is strategically located in the center of the Union Pacific Rail Network. The facility is so large, you can't see more than a third of it from any point in the yard. Well, that is, except from the computer screens of the command center, where all rail activity on the yard is monitored.
