### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Casino Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> Behind the Neon, the billion-dollar buildings, and 24-hour gambling lie a business that has evolved from a simple house of games to an extraordinary trillion-dollar industry. Now, Casino Tech on Modern Marvels. They're the houses that Lady Luck built, modern monuments to games of chance and hopes of fortune. The lure and the appeal of the casino are universal. When you get right down to it, it's about the thrill, the excitement of winning, being right, beating the house, beating the odds, getting that sense of excitement that you can't get anywhere else. Let's face it, everyone comes out for one reason, one reason only. That's to get rich and win a big jackpot. Being part of the action extends extends all over the world. The casino now exists on every continent except Antarctica, stretching all the way from the glittering Las Vegas strip to the shores of the Indian Ocean. Today, you can find casinos within a three-hour drive of pretty much everywhere in the United States, except for Alaska and Hawaii. Thanks to Native American gaming operations and riverboat casinos, there are now over 30 states in America with legalized casino gambling. Worldwide, gambling revenue is larger than movies, spectator sports, theme parks, cruise ships, and recorded music combined. You're beating the cars. You're challenging the odds. So it becomes something that is really good and exciting, you know what I mean? And it just appeals to people. And the saddest thing on land and sea is when you tell the dealer to deal past me. No longer is gambling the only attraction that draws people to the casino. Many casinos are complete destination resorts. You can get great meals. You can get wonderful entertainment. You can shop at exclusive boutiques. You can do a lot of stuff in a casino besides just play the slot machines. Casinos continue to host millions of visitors each year.
