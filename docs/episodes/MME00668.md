### Matching & Evidence Verification

1. **Spoken Dialogue Identification:** The opening narration establishes the episode title on air as *"Start your pre-flight checks and buckle in, because now it's Helicopters on Modern Marvels."*.
1. **Temporal Context & Distinction from 1998 Special:** Optical copyright card reads `© 2009 Actuality Productions, Inc.`. Dialogue cites 2008 production milestones (*'produce nearly 900 helicopters in 2008... winning Popular Science's Best of What's New award in 2008'*). TheTVDB erroneously mapped this 2010 HD documentary to an earlier 1998 classic special of the same title.
1. **Distinction from 1998/2002 Classic Documentary:** This entry represents the 2010 HD remake of Helicopters (optical copyright © 2009). TheTVDB conflated this 2010 production with the 1998/2002 classic documentary ([MME00210: Helicopters](MME00210.md)). Streaming releases on History Vault (#49120) carry the 2002 documentary.
1. **Catalog Numbering Divergence:** TheTVDB catalogs this production as **S16E16**, while TMDB anchors it at **S16E12**. Air dates and subject matter confirm they describe the same documentary.
1. **Production & Copyright Evidence:** On-screen copyright cards date the production to **2009**, corroborating the broadcast window.
