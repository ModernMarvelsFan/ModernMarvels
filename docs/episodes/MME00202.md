### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Louis Arch on Modern Marvels."*

> It rises boldly over the Mississippi River. 40,000 tons of steel and concrete. The biggest man-made arch in the world. Nothing like this had ever been built before or attempted since. Its simple and elegant form a testament to remarkable construction and engineering milestones. Now, the St. Louis Arch on Modern Marvels. The St. Louis Arch. This spectacular structure overlooking the famed Mississippi River in the city of St. Louis, Missouri, is one of the most innovative arches and recognizable icons in the world. Since 1967, when the arch was first built, more than 65 million people from around the world have come to visit. And for those who have seen it, the impression is lasting. The St. Louis Arch, known officially as the Gateway Arch, is America's tallest national monument and the only one commemorating America's westward expansion. And the arch itself is like a piece of outdoor sculpture, if you will. It's a gigantic and very much a symbolic memorial to westward expansion because it takes the form of a literal gateway. It's a portal through which people could literally pass if they wish. It stands 630 feet tall, 25 feet taller than the Washington Monument, and more than twice as high as the Statue of Liberty. One side of the arch faces due east, the other west, so that its polished stainless steel exterior reflects both the rising and setting sun. I don't think there's been any two days that I've gone down there that it's looked exactly the same. The morning and evening light when it's pink
