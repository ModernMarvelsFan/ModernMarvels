### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Mints the Money Machines on Modern Marvels."*

> The Root of All Evil. The Stuff Dreams Are Made Of. Money. By the Billions Coins and Dollars Flow from Secretive Government Facilities. Constantly Changing Their Look to Defeat Sophisticated Counterfeiters. Their Storied Past Is As Fantastic As The Fortunes They Make. Now, U.S. Mints the Money Machines on Modern Marvels. The physical act of making money in the United States is big business. Many of the techniques used by the modern-day money makers were actually developed centuries ago. when making money was a confusing and often frustrating experience. The U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing, which prints paper money, and the U.S. Mint, which makes coins, are some of the busiest manufacturing plants in the world. The Mint's two production facilities and the Bureau's two printing plants operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, turning out coin and currency. The Bureau of Engraving and Printing produces approximately 40 million notes a day. These notes have a face value of between $600 and $700 million, depending on the denominations that we're running. At the U.S. Mint, officials oversee the production of multiple fortunes. We produce up to 20 billion coins a year, and we sell another $400 million worth of gold, silver, and platinum bullion coins and collector coins that we sell all over the world. Plus, we protect about $100 billion worth of assets of the federal government at Fort Knox and at our other facilities around the country. Working in conjunction with the Department of the Treasury, the Bureau and Mint continually feed the economy's voracious appetite for new money.
