Yet there's a lot more to these colorful trucks than meets the eye. Years of technological innovation have transformed them from simple carts to ingenious kitchens on wheels, Filling a need for workers who don't have time for a sit-down restaurant. And tempting others with an array of unusual foods. It tastes seriously like the cake donut.
