<details>

> *"In 1939, Nazi Germany unleashed an awesome war machine. As the most savage battle in history unfolded, Nazi leaders forced German scientists to come up with an even weirder array of weapons. Some were revolutionary, some were simply crazy. All of them were designed to bring devastation to all corners of the globe, and sustained the Third Reich's domination for a thousand years. A global bomber that could fly through space at 13,000 miles an hour to bring destruction to every major city on the planet. The world's first assault rifle that could shoot round corners. A vortex gun designed to bring down an aircraft by creating a tornado. In the 1920s and 30s, rockets and the idea of space travel was all the rage in Germany. Somehow, the idea had taken hold that one day soon, man would be able to travel to the moon. Well, maybe not real soon. Germany underwent a rocket craze in the 1920s. But behind the scenes, there was very serious work going on in an academic and engineering sense by people who really thought that we had a future based around rocket flight. These pioneering scientists planned to develop all manner of rockets, planes, and spaceships. Their vision included space travel to the moon, to Mars, and beyond. Space stations from which they could control Earth's climate and rocket planes that could carry passengers around the Earth in around a couple of hours. Their sole purpose was the exploration of space."*
