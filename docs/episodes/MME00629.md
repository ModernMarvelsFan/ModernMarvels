### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Corpse Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> They save lives, find murderers, and teach a new generation how the human body works. They can be buried, embalmed, donated, and cremated. They can even create a new habitat. If you work hard in life, just wait until you see what you can do after you're dead. Now, Corpse Tech on Modern Marvels. What happens after we die? This simple question has fueled wars, mass fear, and great debate since man first walked the earth. But it's the living, not the dead, that raise all the ruckus. The dead are dead. You can't help them. You can't help them, but maybe they can help you. They might even help catch a killer. I know that we have helped solve several cases by the research we have performed here. At the University of Tennessee, a unique and what some might call creepy forensic research facility exists. to answer questions few dare to ask. Behind a locked fence laid dozens of corpses in varying stages of decay. It's known to some as the body farm. This one-of-a-kind facility covers almost three acres. So you're right now at the Anthropological Research Facility, which is a part of the Forensic Anthropology Center here at the University of Tennessee. We started this facility in 1981. The main focus of our existence is to study human decomposition. Under each of these plastic tarps is a body. But some also lay exposed. Some are placed in cars.
