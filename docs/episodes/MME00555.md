### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Distilleries 2 on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It's an art. It's a science. It's a marriage of vapor and spirits. From the elite to the illegal. From the ban to the celebrated. The distillation of spirits is a $100 billion a year business. Brandy, moonshine, absinthe, liqueurs, and even restaurant distilleries. Cheers! Now, Distilleries 2 on Modern Marvels. How do you capture magic in a bottle? For centuries, people have been obsessed with doing it. The desire to distill and develop the perfect liquor knows no bounds. Even time can be arrested and stored in a glass. if you have the know-how. Well, the cellar in the basement of our chateau is the Paradise Cellar, and this is a very important one for us where we have actually created a reserve of the very, very old cognacs. These are cognacs that can go back over 200 years. The oldest date back to the end of the 18th century to the French Revolution. Since ancient times, the process of distilling the spirits of natural ingredients like fruits and grains, into alcohol has been both a science and a mystery. Distilled spirits are called spirits because they represent the essence of what they're made from, and so the whole idea of spirits was a little bit mystical. The process itself is quite simple. In distillation, liquids are separated from each other when they're converted into vapor at different temperatures. In the case of fermented liquids, the distiller must keep his fermented liquids below water's boiling point of 212 degrees Fahrenheit in order for alcohol to boil off at 173 degrees Fahrenheit. The alcohol vapor rises up into a condenser, where it cools and returns to a liquid state."*

</details>
