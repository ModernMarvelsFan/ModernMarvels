> *"It was the world's first specialized gun, created for the upper class and designed to shoot birds for sport. But its reputation for reliability and durability led to much greater roles. Hunter, Protector and Warrior. For more than four centuries, if a man could afford to own just one weapon, he chose the shotgun. On the frontier, or the battlefield, it was one of the most frightening sounds ever heard. It's machinery in motion, and it's the machinery of death in motion, and it sounds like nothing else. Powerful enough to strike fear into the bravest soldier or the minnest outlaw. No one's going to walk into a darkened room knowing that that Clatch Clatch is in there. That's because those facing the barrel end of a shotgun knew what was coming next. Ease of use and versatility are the qualities that made the shotgun popular, but the ability to fire a lethal spray of ammunition with a single pull of the trigger made it legendary. America's earliest settlers used shotguns to carve out a life in the New World."*
