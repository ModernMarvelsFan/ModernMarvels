### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Engineering Disasters will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Killer chemicals ooze from a toxic waste dump into a residential neighborhood. World Trade Center Building No. 7 is reduced to a pile of rubble on September 11th, but it wasn't attacked. Patriot missile software problems leave U.S. soldiers defenseless. A nightclub turns into an inferno that kills over 400 people. And the science of building implosion is put to the test and fails. Now, Engineering Disasters on Modern Marbles. World Trade Center Building No. 7. On September 11, 2001, it was one of the casualties of the terrorist attacks on that horrific day. Building 7's sudden and shocking collapse has been overshadowed by the fate of the Twin Towers. But from an engineering standpoint, it is the greater mystery. We were surprised that Tower 7 collapsed, we being the team that investigated what occurred on that day. There was some damage to Tower 7 caused by the debris that hit it from Tower 1, but the damage was certainly not similar in scope or magnitude to that caused by the aircraft hitting towers 1 and 2. The only positive aspect of this building's demise is that it fell seven hours after the two main towers. Everyone had been evacuated and even though the 47-story building collapsed in on itself in under 10 seconds, no one was killed. World Trade Center 7 was completed in 1987. It had approximately 1.8 million square feet, 47 stories, and was roughly 600 feet tall. Each of the floors was approximately 40,000 square feet, which is roughly the size of a football field and equal to the size of the floor plate on each of the towers. The building was a good building. It was soundly designed. It would still be standing if not for 9-11, and it was state-of-the-art at the time, absolutely."*

</details>
