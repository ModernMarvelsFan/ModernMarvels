### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now on Modern Marvels."*

> Weapons. From handguns to atomic bombs. Each designed to protect and kill. How do we learn to direct and control their deadly force? Whether it's by traditional methods or through high-tech simulators. The training ground is a place called a firing range. Now on Modern Marvels. They respond to a call and don't know who or what is waiting for them when they arrive. Years of training will be tested in a split second. Stop! Stop! The armed suspect is gunned down in a blaze of bullets. Only this time, there's no blood, no body bags, no chalked outline of a corpse. This is a modern-day high-tech firing range, a simulator that combines all of the lights, camera, and action of a Hollywood movie. For the law enforcement community, the most important aspect of it is the realism, the interactivity, and the ability to create scenarios that they may face in their day-to-day job. Is there anything better to do than stop us on a Sunday morning? Just need to see the ladies' driver's license. Just relax. Using actors, real-life scenes are videotaped, transferred to CDs, and then projected on a large video screen. Computer hardware and software control the scenarios so they can be programmed for the suspect to respond in many different ways. They can lay down their weapon or fire back. Hold it! Stop! Officers use training weapons that have been modified to fire laser beams.
