### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the untold story of Ultimate Gadgets on Modern Marvels."*

> We're living in a gadget revolution. Virtual keyboards, iPod eyewear, appliances with personalities. Close me now. New and unimagined gizmos solve problems that we didn't even know we had. This is a thousand dollar Swiss army knife. They emerge from cutting edge think tanks and tinkerers basement workshops. I reek with imagination. I just reek with it. Will they usher in a techno-utopia or enslave us to pocket-sized technology? From the time-tested stalwarts of the past to the very latest in digital necessities. Now, the untold story of Ultimate Gadgets on Modern Marvels. Las Vegas, a place to take a gamble. That's especially true every January, when the Consumer Electronics Show rolls into town and 20,000 new gadgets are unveiled across an area the size of 38 football fields. The International CES started in 1967 in New York, and now it's grown to the largest trade show of any type in the Americas. It has 1.85 million net square feet of exhibit space, 2,700 exhibitors, 140,000 people, including a record-setting 4,000 media. Each gadget that premieres at CES hopes to hit the jackpot by becoming the next big thing. But what exactly is a gadget? According to the Merriam-Webster dictionary, it's a small or electronic device with practical use, but often thought of as a novelty. Some of the novel gadgets that will take their bow at CES will be embraced by the public. Many more will crash and burn. But only the geekiest gadgets will be selected for sale by the online retailer ThinkGeek. Its senior merchandiser, Ty Liotta, decides which goofy gizmo should appeal to their customers. A lot of the things that we sell are not something that you really need to have.
