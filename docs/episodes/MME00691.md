### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Inside Your Walls on Modern Marvels."*

> We take our walls for granted, but just under their surface, inside lies a secret world you have to see to believe. It's a highway for technology that might be one spark away from disaster, a haven for the hideous. The wall is the highway for bed bugs to move from one room to another. Discover the secret of this wall-hugging robot, and the drama that played out in this hidden chamber on Alcatraz. From here, they went up pretty much just like a jungle gym. Fire! Now, Inside Your Walls on Modern Marvels. A few walls around the globe have earned an enduring place in history, like the Great Wall of China, the Wailing Wall in Jerusalem, the Berlin Wall, even Fenway Park's Green Monster. But we rarely consider the ordinary walls that surround us. For centuries, they fulfilled our basic human need for shelter against the elements and to divide the spaces where we live and work. But there's more going on inside your walls than you'd ever imagine. Let's start here. Every time you flick on a light, you're tapping into a complex network of wiring you seldom consider, but upon which you're completely dependent. And for the most part, it's entirely safe. But sometimes faulty engineering inside your walls can trigger catastrophe. 70,000 electrical fires are reported annually in the United States, killing 500 people and causing $800 million in property damage.
