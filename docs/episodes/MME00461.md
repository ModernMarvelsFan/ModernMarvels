### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Civil War Tech will return on Modern Marvels."*

> American soldiers rely on the most sophisticated technology available. If you think this is the result of 21st century innovation, think again. Try the 19th century. It was unprecedented Civil War advancements in weapons, communications, transportation, sea power, and even news coverage coverage that set the technological standard for the art of war. Now, Civil War Attack on Modern Marvelance. Iraq, 2003. America invades with the most modern weapons and tactics ever conceived. However, the technology employed to bring the enemy to its knees in only a matter of weeks is directly descended from another conflict fought more than 140 years ago, the American Civil War. The Civil War was the first of the great modern wars. In its four short years, warfare evolved some 500 years to what it had been. Beyond any shadow of a doubt, there's a very direct correlation between the technology used in the American Civil War and the technology being used in today's military. In fact, newfound discoveries, such as the first Union ironclad warship, the USS Monitor, raised from its 140-year-old watery grave in 2002, reveal a quantum leap in technology beyond any previous conflict. Today's modern warship was born right here. This is the first instance of the movable turret that allowed every gun to be brought to bear on any target.
