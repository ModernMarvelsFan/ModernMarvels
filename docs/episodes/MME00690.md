### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, American Trucking on Modern Marvels."*

> They're the rolling titans, holding America together. Without them, the nation would come to a screeching halt. Just put your nose to the grindstone, in you go. They deliver, in every sense of the word. The men who drive them make it so. America is trucking, and trucking is America. They rescue and repossess and rack up miles on roads where anything can happen. An enduring heritage of horsepower and hard work. I don't know how to do it. Buckle up for a high-octane adventure. Now, American Trucking on Modern Marvels. ! During show nine of a whirlwind 10-city tour. Country superstar Brad Paisley mesmerizes his fans with his elaborate stage production. A video screen projects images two stories high. Lasers light up the arena. Dozens of speakers blast from the stage. In just 10 hours, all of this equipment, and much more, has to be moved 300 miles to Tulsa, Oklahoma, for tomorrow night's concert. The monumental task is the responsibility of nine trucks and nine truckers. They know that when tonight's performance ends, it's their turn to shine. The frenzy of activity soon to begin is all in a day's work for Jay Renaud.
