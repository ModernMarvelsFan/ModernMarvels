### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Lighthouses on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Lighthouses cut the sky with a shaft of light to guide distant wanderers to safety. Technological and engineering masterpieces, their greatest achievement may be their haunting beauty. Now, Lighthouses on Modern Marvels. At the junction of opposing forces, land and sea, light and dark, safety and peril, calm and chaos sits a lighthouse perched solidly on the very precipice of ruin lighthouses offer all a guidepost to safe passage sending out a beacon of light whose fundamental symbolism is so evocative it sometimes overshadows the structures themselves. Despite ever-advancing technology that threatens to make them obsolete, lighthouses continue to serve as aids to navigation. Roughly 800 still stand in the United States alone. Their resiliency is testimony to the marvel of their construction and the power of their appeal. I think of it as like a primal resonance of the image of a light flashing in the darkness, that guiding light in the darkness or in a storm that is only there for one purpose. They're the antithesis of the city, the city with the sirens and the jackhammers going off and all of the noise and the stress."*
