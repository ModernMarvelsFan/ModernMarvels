When the going gets tough, count on the world's arsenal of wheeled workhorses to muscle in and meet the challenge. No matter how dirty or daunting the task, trucks ride to our rescue. From transporting our most dangerous cargoes, to carrying military and emergency teams, to supporting the mightiest civil engineering feats. But first and foremost, we rely on trucks to deliver. A network of multi-ton vehicles transporting goods and raw materials between producer and consumer is the economic lifeblood of the nation. More than 80% of all freight in the United States moves by truck. You know, as the economy grows, one of the things that takes off in parallel is truck driving. Because this is the way that most goods are transported across the United States. In fact, pretty much anything you can think of has been brought to you at some point by a truck. America's love affair with trucks is more than a century old and still going strong. Trucks not only symbolize the romance of the open road, But also, Thunder Along is the very epitome of power and endurance. One recognizable phrase sums it up, built like a Mack truck. Mack trucks, now owned by Volvo North America, conjures images of ironclad strength and unwavering dependability.
