### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Beans on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They've been called the near-perfect food and the miracle crop. From the mighty soybean in all its forms to the world's most widely consumed legume. That's enough to feed about two million people. They supply a plentiful source of protein, and they also produce gas in more ways than one. That's why our beans are so much better. Now, we're spilling them. Beans on Modern Marvels. Beans. The world produces nearly 500 billion pounds of them a year. That's an impressive 70 pounds for every man, woman, and child on Earth. They're one of the world's oldest crops, a source of cholesterol-free protein, rich in fiber and complex carbohydrates, low in fat, and packed with vitamins and minerals. Hundreds of bean varieties grow around the globe. The number one bean grown in the U.S. is also our most versatile, the soybean. American soybean growers annually harvest nearly 75 million acres of the bean, or around 180 billion pounds. Like other beans, soybeans come from a family of vegetables called legumes and are harvested for the seeds, or beans, inside the pods. Sure, you can turn soy into soy sauce and tofu. But soy foods don't stop there. How about hot dogs and burgers? Want cheese with that? More soy. Don't like burgers? How about chicken or meatballs? And for dessert, soy ice cream. Still, much of the soybean's value lies in the oil it produces. Surprisingly, almost 80% of all edible fats and oils consumed in the U.S. come from soybean oil."*
