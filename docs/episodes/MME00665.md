But soy foods don't stop there. How about hot dogs and burgers? Want cheese with that? More soy. Don't like burgers? How about chicken or meatballs? And for dessert, soy ice cream. Still, much of the soybean's value lies in the oil it produces. Surprisingly, almost 80% of all edible fats and oils consumed in the U.S. come from soybean oil.
