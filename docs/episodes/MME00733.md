> Today I'm in East Longmeadow, Massachusetts, in the Cardamundi factory. Now, you may not have heard of them, but I can guarantee you heard of what they make right here. One of the top games of all time, played by more than a billion people. It's a little game from Hasbro that you might know called Monopoly. Some of our top toys and games have endured for generations, centuries, even millennia. And the business of play earns some serious money. The global market for toys and games is more than $92 billion a year. And that's not monopoly money. That's the real thing. I want to know how this industry continues to sell our favorite top toys and games while innovating and creating completely new ones every year. How does Hot Wheels design the 130 new car models introduced every year? And how is Mattel using cutting edge tech to bring He-Man to a new generation of fans? The spinners landed on me and it is my turn to find out. Game on! This is Modern Marvel's Top Toys and Games. Hasbro was founded in 1923 with three brothers with the last name of Hassenfeld, hence Hasbro. In the beginning, their fledgling Rhode Island company made ends meet by selling textile remnants, pencil cases, school supplies. Eventually, they branched out to include paint, crayons, even toy doctor's kits for little boys and girls. Today, Hasbro is one of the largest toy manufacturers in the world with annual revenue in the neighborhood of five billion dollars and one of their all-time best-selling items the classic beloved board game Monopoly it's as popular as it ever was it's available in 114 countries and it's been translated into 47 languages to keep up with that massive
