### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"From the cow to the cone, it's the untold story of ice cream on Modern Marvels."*

> It's everyone's favorite. Big time, small time, anytime. Whether it's vanilla, chocolate, or whatever. We need some M&Ms. How about brownies? Sure, all right, why not? We've got the scoop on this centuries-old dessert in all of its flavorful forms. That's good. That's a 10. From the cow to the cone, it's the untold story of ice cream on Modern Marvels. Welcome to a world like none you've ever seen. A magical land of ice cream floats, chocolate waterfalls, and frozen fantasies. Charlie and the Chocolate Factory ain't got nothing on us. This is the Dreyer's Plant in Bakersfield, California, the world's largest and busiest ice cream factory. Each year, the 28 production lines churn out 90 different ice cream products, totaling over 100 million gallons. That helps satisfy the world's 4 billion gallon a year appetite. Not surprisingly, one of the leaders is the good old USA, USA, where the average person downs 23.2 quarts a year. We're all kids when it comes to ice cream. We all love it. You know, it's comfort food. You've had a bad day? Have a bowl of ice cream. You've had a good day? Celebrate with a bowl of ice cream. Today, ice cream production is a sophisticated and technologically advanced science. But the same basic principles from hundreds of years ago still apply. The way we make ice cream in the manufacturing scale is the same way that you would make ice cream at home in a hand-turned freezer. The differences are, obviously, we do it in scale in a continuous process rather than a batch process at home. The process begins with ice cream's most important ingredient, milk.
