### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"It takes Swamp Tech, now on Modern Marvels."*

> In a timeless place where nature rules, ancient knowledge meets modern science, and cutting edge takes on new meaning. I've been bit 90 times. It's a struggle for balance between man and the swamp. It takes more than luck to survive here. It takes Swamp Tech, now on Modern Marvels. About 6% of Earth's dry land is actually a waterlogged wilderness, inaccessible and unforgiving. These are our wetlands, swamps, and marshes. More than 40% of the world's species live here, including man. In the United States, the biggest swamp lies deep in the heart of Louisiana. It's 150 miles long, and it's called the Atchafalaya. Here, the way anywhere is tangled and plant choked. choked. And just getting around is a huge challenge, unless you have one of these, an airboat. With an airboat, you can run marsh areas. So many places you can go that you can't go with anything else. You could walk it, which would be quite miserable and take forever. But with an airboat, you can run the canals as fast as you'd like. Get out there, run the marsh, cross the land. There's just nothing like it. You can't beat it. Airboats are an important part of life in the swamp, and Mark Robichaux and his son Brent have been building them for years. An airboat is basically a flat-bottomed boat propelled by a giant rear-mounted fan.
