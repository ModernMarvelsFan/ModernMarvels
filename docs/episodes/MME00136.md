### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the Erie Canal on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"363 miles of water highway, eight years of construction, thousands of man hours of labor. But by the time it was done, 3,000 canal boats traveled the new corridor from Albany to Buffalo. Now, the Erie Canal on Modern Marvels. In the woods off the interstate in western New York, a narrow ditch surrounded by a tangle of weeds is all that remains here of one of America's greatest engineering achievements. Celebrated in story and song, the Erie Canal began construction in 1817. It was the first commercial link between New York and the American West. Thousands of canal boats made the journey from the Hudson River to Lake Erie during the canal's 150-year history, making the Erie Canal America's first superhighway. Though the Erie Canal went through three separate rebuildings and upgrades, it was the original canal of the early 19th century. 40 feet wide and 4 feet deep that changed our world and consolidated the strength of a young nation. And in a very real sense, it united the country in ways that no other event of its time could do. It was indeed a world-famous engineering marvel. It truly is the backbone of the Empire State. It led to the creation of the Empire State, helped New York become the financial capital of the world. One analogy I like to make is that it's very much like the Internet today, that people really viewed it as something that was amazing, that was unprecedented, that was very much changing their lives."*

</details>
