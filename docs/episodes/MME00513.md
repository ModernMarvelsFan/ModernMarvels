> It's a discovery as old as fire and the wheel. Born of ancient alchemies, it provides shelter, protection, and warmth. Durable, pliable, and organic. It is always about envy and fascination. Now, leather on modern marvels. Near the dawn of civilization, a solitary man began a journey here in the rugged terrain of the Swiss Alps. Whatever his destination, he never made it, dead from an arrow wound in his torso. Remarkably, for the next 5,000 years, his mortal remains stayed beautifully preserved by the frigid cold, until they were discovered in 1991. But not only was his body intact, so too were bits of his clothing, a satchel filled with tools, and a pair of waterproof boots, all made of ancient leather. Leather making goes back to the origins of man for clothing, for protection, for hunting. We definitely would not have made it through the ice ages without the ability to tan hide. We had to be able to figure out how to clothe ourselves and leather's essential for that. Born of our ingenuity and will to survive, leather may well have been the first man-made product. Its warmth and protections so vital to ancient peoples that it became sacred. Today, leather is our daily companion. Nearly all of us walk in it, ride on it, and wrap it around ourselves. Yet the essence of the material remains the same as it was at the dawn of civilization. raw animal skins. That is what's so special about leather manufacturing, because first of all, we're dealing with an organic material. We're dealing with a substrate that has come from a live animal.
