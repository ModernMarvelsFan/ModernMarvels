Now, The Hardware Store on Modern Marvels. It has been part of the American landscape since before the Civil War. In today's 14,300 modern hardware stores, you can find anything for inside and outside the home. From kitchen faucets to barbecue grills. Some of today's hardware giants stock over 40,000 different items. But what were the original products? What is hardware in its purest form? To find the answer, we've come to Placerville True Value Hardware in Placerville, California. Originally opened in 1856, it is one of the oldest hardware stores in America. Outposts like this supplied the raw materials that helped build our first ranches, farms, and towns.
