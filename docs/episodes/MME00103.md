### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"We now return to fireworks on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Explosive fire. Bursts of color and light. Unique artworks. Joyous celebrations. Their language is universal. Their appeal timeless. Fireworks. Now on Modern Moders. They make the old feel young again, and the young feel glorious. Even the simplest sparklers demand attention. Aerial spectaculars hold us spellbound with their power and beauty. To the Japanese, they are hanabe, flowers of fire. There's something magical that goes back to our basic roots as human beings and the mystery of fire. To see the sky explode from darkness into this bright light and feel these concussions. It gets somewhere right in the center of our brain and says, now this is something special. An ancient epigram says that he who has smelled the smoke can never again escape it. And for many enthusiasts, their fascination begins in childhood. The fun of fireworks when you were a kid was the first time you were an alchemist. You could touch something to a fuse and a rocket would go up in the air. The sense of power almost, you could send a thing soaring into the heaven or scare the cat or scare your parents in particular. Whatever their age, fireworks craftsmen delight in thrilling crowds. They are constantly striving to create bigger shows, more dazzling effects, and appreciative audiences. Early each spring, two weeks of festivities precede the most famous horse race in America, the Kentucky Derby."*
