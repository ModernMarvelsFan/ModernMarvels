### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"More military movers will return on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"They rush soldiers to the battlefield and haul the supplies they rely on to wage war. They're massive, fast, agile, and innovative. The Osprey, the Expeditionary Fighting Vehicle, the KC-135 Stratotanker, Roll-On Roll-Off Ships, and Chinook helicopters. Wherever they're deployed, they can make or break the fight. Now, more military movers on Modern Marbles. Whenever we consider the vehicles used in war, We think of aircraft carriers, jet fighters, and tanks. But these stars among war machines could never battle effectively without the support of a different breed of military mover, the workhorses that can move mountains, mountains of supplies, arms, and high-tech equipment to battle zones anywhere in the world. Amateurs talk tactics. Professionals talk logistics. Getting the right mix of men and materiel to the right place at the right time is the essential precondition for victory in battle. Modern armies are transporting men and materials further and faster than ever before. In the case of American military operations, these tasks are often twice as difficult as those of other countries. The America's founding fathers, in their infinite foresight, Foresight had the wisdom to put the United States on the other side of the planet from everybody else. So logistics is important for American military operations in a way that it is for no other country. American forces have to travel thousands of miles even to get close to the battlefront."*
