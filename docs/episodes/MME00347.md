Join us as we reveal the bizarre rituals, the genteel history, and the over-the-top histrionics of one of America's most inexplicable pastimes. Now, tailgating on Modern Marvels. The frigid pre-dawn hours of a December Chicago morning. Seven men prepare for a grueling odyssey. Their destination? A frozen expanse of barren tundra, 215 miles north. Lambeau Field. They bring with them the fervor of the devout football fan. This is my Bears shrine. Roy Taylor is a 32-year-old technical account manager from Carroll Stream, Illinois, and a diehard Bear fan since 1979. A few items of Bears-Packer rivalry here. We, of course, have the shirts that we're not going to walk into Lambeau Field with unless we're completely crazy. The Green Bay sucks button, of course. And not in complete support of it, but somebody gave me that one. Apparently somebody thinks that Osama wears a cheese head, which we won't argue with. With 7 1⁄2 hours left before game time, it's time for these Chicago Bears fans to hit the road. See you tonight. We'll make it back safe as long as we don't get abused too much.
