### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Find out now as we rev up for maximum horsepower on Modern Marvels."*

> From the fire-breathing fastest cars on land, to some of the most extreme engines on water, to the cutting-edge technology of today's hybrid automobiles, horsepower has defined the way we move for two centuries. Is there really the power of 200 horses under your hood? Find out now as we rev up for maximum horsepower on Modern Marvels. For some, faster has always been better. Speed's very addictive. It can make you do things that some normal people wouldn't want to do. When they step on the accelerator pedal, they want the vehicle to move. They want it to go fast. They want the feel of raw horsepower. And nowhere can you find more raw horsepower than on a Sunday afternoon at the drag strip. These are among the fastest accelerating vehicles on the planet. Top-fueled dragsters and their counterparts known as funny cars can walk at the speeds of more than 300 miles per hour while scorching a quarter-mile track in under five seconds. They are powered by supercharged engines of up to 11,000 horsepower. In the recent history of this testosterone-fueled sport, one of the top handlers of horsepower is a woman, former drag racer Melanie Troxell. This car doesn't know whether there's a male or a female in the seat. It's, you know, equal all the way around for all the drivers. When you're talking 330 miles an hour, 8,000 horsepower, I mean, it is such an animal, these cars.
