> *"Modern Marvels is going big and counting down. No series has saluted technology over as many episodes as we have. Now, let's kiss the conventional goodbye and go in search of the bizarre as we count down the top 10 strangest Marvels we've ever featured. This head scratching assortment of oddities proves that the inside scoop on innovation often lies outside the norm. These are weird machines. Join our coast-to-coast cavalcade of some of the oddest tech ever devised until the big moment when we unveil the most unusual wonder of them all. It may be a little too intense for younger children. Now, America's strangest marbles on this edition of Modern Marvel's Mega Marvel's Countdown. Most technology, though fascinating, can be classified as conventional, applying science and engineering in a way that matches our concepts of normal. But this countdown turns the spotlight on tech that turns the art of innovation upside down. dreamt up by mavericks and misfits daring enough to be different. Each of our top 10 contenders is weird in its own unique way, and our inherent fascination with them shows we all have a soft spot for the strange. The strange marvel in our number 10 spot gets us off to a high-flying start. Is it a bird? A plane? No. It's the aerodynamic miracle that enables mortal man to soar through the skies, The wingsuit. A wingsuit's bizarre but highly effective assembly of webbed surfaces transforms a skydiver into a human version of a flying squirrel. The only thing stranger than the suit itself is the exhilarating sensation experienced by the person using it."*
