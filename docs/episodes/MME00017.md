### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, the Golden Gate Bridge on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It's the Queen of Bridges, dramatically spanning a mile of turbulent water. But its birth was a fiery mix of high-minded idealism, brilliant engineering, and daring construction work. Now, the Golden Gate Bridge on Modern Marvels. Its grandeur is often shrouded in a creeping cocoon of dense fog. But when the fog lifts in San Francisco, the Golden Gate Bridge beckons as a shining example of how engineering and architectural design can work together to transform functionality into artistry. When completed in 1937, it instantly became symbolic of the American dream. Well, certainly the Golden Gate Certainly the Golden Gate Bridge is a critically important piece of transportation infrastructure. It's also a remarkable engineering achievement. But I think the thing that sets it apart from other big bridges around the world is the love affair that the people of San Francisco, of California, even the United States have with this bridge. And our workers say that the Golden Gate Bridge is alive and that it lives and breathes. And when you get out there on the bridge, you find out that that's really true. It does. We estimate we have somewhere between 9 and 14 million visitors a year who either come ride a bicycle over it, drive over it, or just come and park and look at it and really enjoy the experience of seeing this modern marvel of engineering. The bridge is a major attraction. San Francisco's signature landmark spans the mouth of the picturesque Golden Gate Strait and inspires awe as the world's most majestic example of suspension bridge design. What that means is you have main suspension cables that start on the land, that rise up over the top of a tower, drape down in mid-span, rise up over the next tower and come down again on land. On the end of each individual cable is a mass of concrete that pulls it to the ground."*

</details>
