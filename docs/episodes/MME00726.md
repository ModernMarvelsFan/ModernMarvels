### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> The United States is and always has been a melting pot of cultures, customs, ideas, and yes, food. One of the things that I love most about food is that it can and always does evolve. Coming up with new inventions, new innovations, that's an American tradition. And we've been revolutionizing the world of food just like every other part of our society since our founding days. In fact, there are a lot of iconic foods you probably associate with other countries that were actually invented right here in the U.S. of A. Like English muffins? Yeah, that's ours. The Cuban sandwich? That's an American innovation. Even salad dressings like Russian, French, and ranch invented here, too. American innovations also include the first chocolate that didn't melt in summer heat, the first condensed soup, and so much more. Today, we're getting a look at American inventions and innovations up close. We'll go behind the curtain at world-famous Dippin' Dots, where we'll see how a process originally created for feeding cattle gave us a brilliant reinvention of ice cream. Plus, we'll see how Blue Apron is changing the grocery gate by using innovative machines on the assembly line. You won't believe how they're shipping millions of specially designed packages every month across the country. And we'll see behind the curtain at the Jelly Bean Company that blew up the jelly bean world by figuring out how to pack hundreds of crazy new flavors into the center of their beans. This is Modern Marvel's Inventions and Innovations. What is innovation? To me, it's that spirit of asking, is there a better way? How can this improve? Maybe it's improving a process or making something more efficient, or maybe it's creating something completely new. The biggest and most beloved innovations have changed the world as we know it, like the light bulb, the automobile, and the supermarket.
