> At the end of every year, we buy the gifts, deck the halls, and trim the tree. Thousands of lights, miles of wire, latex, motors, hardware, software, and fruitcake. Ho, ho, hold on tight as we unwrap the year-round planning, planting, and packing that makes it all come together. Now, Christmas Tech on Modern Marvels Christmas is observed by nearly one-third of the world's population and probably more if you count the non-Christians who incorporate some of the more secular traditions into their winter season. So as holy days and holidays go, Christmas is a pretty big deal. Holiday makers go all out. Trees heavy with decorations, elaborate lighting schemes, edibles and presents galore. In a city as culturally diverse as New York City, many regional traditions come together to form one spectacular display of Christmas spirit. Each year, the Big Apple tries to outdo the last, with millions of colored lights, illuminating buildings and streets, and impressive store window decorations encouraging revelers to buy, buy, buy. Four, three, two, four. Hey, Vincent! The focal point of it all, the Rockefeller Center Tree, presides majestically over a seasonal skating rink and attracts throngs of visitors to midtown Manhattan. It usually stands between 75 and 90 feet tall. and is at least 35 feet wide.
