### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, hand tools on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Hand tools, older than man himself, crafted from chipped rocks and cherry red steel. Now, hand tools on Modern Marvels. Tools, one syllable, five letters. A simple word that can refer to virtually every human achievement, a countless number of objects, and even our own evolution. Hi, I'm Ron Hazleton. Welcome to the Tool Bench. You know, when I was growing up, I used to play in my dad's workshop. Tools were like toys for me and my brother. We spent countless hours making soapbox derby cars. Today, I help people all across the country with do-it-yourself home improvement projects. I'm always struck by the special kind of bonding that occurs when a family uses tools to make something in their lives better. And I guess it shouldn't come as a surprise. Tools have been a vital part of the human experience. December 12, 1998. Astronauts Jerry Ross and James Newman disembark the space shuttle Endeavour. Their destination is Unity, the recently installed American made portion of the International Space Station. 250 miles above the Earth, Ross and Newman attach a space age toolbox to Unity. Inside are more than 50 hand tools, wrenches, ratchets, and clamps, which will be used in at least 150 future to complete the construction of the space station. These tools are similar to those used on Earth, with a few differences. Handles are wider, since the bulky gloves worn by the astronauts make it difficult to grip narrower objects."*
