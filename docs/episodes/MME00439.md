### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, World's Biggest Machines on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Four and a half acres of floating military might. Enough brute force to move massive ships across the globe. A mammoth 300-foot fire-breathing metal melting monster. An unprecedented design that blew the roof off the traditional sports stadium. And giant robots taking over the world. Forget what you've heard. Size does matter. Now, World's Biggest Machines on Modern Marvels. The USS Ronald Reagan is the biggest and most technologically advanced aircraft carrier in the world. Also known as CVN 76, she is the ninth ship in the Nimitz class, the largest designation of flattop. USS Ronald Reagan, length 1,092 feet, flight deck area 4.5 acres, aircraft capacity 80, personnel capacity 6,000. Powered by two nuclear reactors and driven by four bronze propellers weighing over 66,000 pounds each, the USS Ronald Reagan reaches speeds of over 30 knots. All the Nimitz-class ships are almost identical. What makes this one the biggest is its flight deck. The deck angle has increased from 9.05 degrees to 9.15 degrees from the center line. This allows unobstructed simultaneous launching and recovering of aircraft. Home to the men and women who take to the seas to defend the United States, CVN 76 is practically a floating city. While underway during their employment cycle, there is little or no access to outside provisions and services. Self-sufficiency is crucial."*
