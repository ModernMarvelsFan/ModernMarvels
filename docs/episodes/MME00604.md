### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Engineering Disasters on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"A mysterious explosion beneath Manhattan drives New Yorkers to the brink of panic. A Minneapolis bridge crowded with cars abruptly collapses. Tiny particles trigger a helicopter crash that kills 46 people. And did natural gas drillers unleash a torrent of hot mud that destroyed a city? Now, Engineering Disasters on Modern Marvels. It started with a roar. Then, the Earth opened up and a powerful force emerged, propelling a violent torrent of blistering mud, rock, and steam 80 stories into the sky. That is like the pit of hell. This engineering disaster struck the heart of New York City. The explosion occurred at 6 p.m. on July 18, 2007. It erupted through the asphalt at Lexington Avenue and 41st Street in Midtown Manhattan. The blast tore a huge crater in the middle of the block and blew out windows in the surrounding buildings. The black sky and falling debris triggered memories of the 2001 terrorist attacks. When we first heard it, we thought the explosion was in Grand Central Station. And we also heard the word massive explosion, and obviously we were concerned at what that might be. Officials quickly allayed the public's worst fears. There is no reason to believe that there was any terrorism involved whatsoever. It is probably just a failure of part of our infrastructure. One person died from shock, and 44 others were treated for injuries. The disaster was the result of an underground steam pipe explosion. The impact of something like this steam explosion, the consequences could be very similar to the impacts of a break in any infrastructure if there was a terrorist attack."*

</details>
