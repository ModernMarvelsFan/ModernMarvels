> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"The Pentagon, next on Modern Marvels."*

> The Pentagon. It's a global landmark, yet one of the world's most mysterious buildings. Penetrate the walls of this fortress of American military might and go only where insiders go to uncover its top secrets. The Pentagon, next on Modern Marvels. It has three times the office space of the Empire State Building. It's twice the size of the Chicago Merchandise Mart. Five Capitol buildings can fit inside its walls. It's the Pentagon. The name alone conjures up an imposing image of American power and prestige. As the largest office building in the world, it serves as the headquarters of the United States Armed Forces. Every day, more than 24,000 men and women come here to work on the vital and sometimes top secret business of national defense. Beyond the Pentagon's massive size and awesome purpose looms its impenetrable mystique. It is a building that has transcended its original meaning, its original function to become a symbol. And as such, it is. It's still an extremely important part of what is the American national identity. Despite possessing an image of invincibility, the Pentagon in reality has become quite vulnerable. It fails to meet current health and safety codes. Major building systems have deteriorated to such an extent that they pose a threat to national security.
