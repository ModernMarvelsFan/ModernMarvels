### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, The Pig on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"Fuel a $100 billion a year business. We may rearrange his DNA in the name of culinary progress, but this intelligent creature remains a trusted ally. He might even save your life. Now, The Pig on Modern Marvels. The aroma of deep-fried foods and the shrieks from thrill riders say it loud and clear. It's time for the fair. Surprisingly, Los Angeles, California, hosts the largest county fair in North America, each year attracting over 1 million visitors. No visit is complete without paying homage to the fair's traditional mascot, the pig. The show pig competition is serious business. business, each competitor trying to produce the ultimate physical specimen. Behind every champion pig is a doting mother. This sow is a wonderful example of what we hoped that a production sow would look like. She's 10 years old, she has a phenomenal udder line on her still, she's produced 18 litters of pigs. The sow is the most prolific reproducing animal on the farm. between 6 and 12 pigs per litter, with the capability of producing more than 250 piglets over her lifetime. She will spend two-thirds of her life pregnant. What you're seeing is dual activity. Mom is doing what she does best, and that's sleeping. And the pigs are doing what they're doing best, and that's eating. When they're little, within the first, oh, I'd say 12, 24 hours, they actually lock onto one particular teat that is theirs, and they go to it each time. They'll fight to the death to keep that teat. And usually what happens is the bigger, stronger babies that are born root the smaller, weaker babies to the teats that don't give as much milk."*

</details>
