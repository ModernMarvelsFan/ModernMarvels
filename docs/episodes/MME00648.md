### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Victor has agreed to a modern marvels challenge."*

> It's where we wash our cars. It spins. It sprays. It's powerful enough to suck up a bowling ball. How about some tips for doing it yourself? Or a challenge to clean this stuff off a $160,000 Porsche? And how do you clean a train that carries radioactive waste? It's time to clean up. Car Wash Tech on Modern Marbles. Why do we wash our cars anyway? It drives better when it's clean. It's a pride thing. I don't really like to drive a dirty car. You just feel better about the car and your appearance. You get up in the morning and take a shower, don't you? Every weekend, millions steer their dirty vehicles into one of the 60,000 car washes across America. All my potential clients take a nice look at my shiny black truck. I'm graduating tomorrow with my bachelor's degree, so I want it to look really nice when my mom and everybody comes in. The car wash industry washes nearly 8 million passenger vehicles per day, and more than 2 billion cars are washed by professional car washes every year. Have somebody else do it, you know, and save your nails. It's a business whose owners clean up with $14 billion a year in annual sales. The options, full serve, self-serve, and in-bay automatics. Quite a success story for an industry that began in 1914, with a couple of employees washing cars by hand.
