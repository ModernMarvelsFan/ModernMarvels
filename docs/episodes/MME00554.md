From fine print to big business, this ancient substance has written its way into the very history it was used to write. Now, Inc. on Modern Marvels. Inc. is one of the most ubiquitous man-made substances on Earth. Just look around. It's everywhere. Inc. is one of those things that's around you. it surrounds you and people don't think about it. For example, if you go into the grocery store, you're surrounded by ink. You have ink on the money. You have ink on the packages. You can have ink on the tiles on the floor, plus whatever magazines you might buy. So ink is all around us, but when I say I work for an ink company, people ask me about pens, or they try to score a computer printer type ink for me. I think the best way to define an ink is any kind of liquid or paste that carries colorant in it. And after the ink dries, it leaves the colorant on the paper. A close cousin of paint, ink is primarily used for writing and printing. Ink is made up of a few basic components. The colorant, pigment or dyes that can come from animal, plant, mineral and chemical sources, and the vehicle, usually a water or oil-based substance which can control viscosity and affect the dry rate.
