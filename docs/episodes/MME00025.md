### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Paving America on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"It's a transportation system unequaled by any other countries in the world. 600,000 bridges, 400 tunnels, 2 million miles of roads. Now, Paving America on Modern Marvels. Arguably, no structures have done more to change the physical and cultural landscape of America than the concrete and asphalt ribbons that make up its vast road network. America's roads traverse waterways, push through mountains, across barren deserts. They connect the metropolitan areas to the suburbs, and the suburbs to the farmlands. And they've helped transform a nation once fragmented by rugged terrain into a financial superpower. Every day you're affected by these paved roads because everything you eat, everything you touch, everything you buy, sell, everything you do, including going to work, is done on those paved roads. The explosive growth brought on by America's road infrastructure has forced cities across the country to expand on what is already the largest engineered transportation system in the world. These expansions represent the culmination of over 200 years of road building evolution. One of the fastest growing cities in America is Denver, Colorado. Each day, approximately 100,000 drivers make their commute along I-25 from the suburbs, headed for Denver."*

</details>
