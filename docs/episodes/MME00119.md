The Channel Tunnel is one of the engineering marvels of our time. It took an army of some 7,000 workers seven years and almost $14 billion to drill this 31-mile link between England and France. They had to drill not one but three underwater tunnels in the harshest of environments, fighting bad rock, unexpected fissures and serious leaks."*
