> *"On one side, pounding surf. On the other, looming mountains and rugged gorges. Building it pushed road and bridge engineers to new heights. Now, buckle up and enjoy the ride on the most spectacular roadway in America, the Pacific Coast Highway. It took decades for construction crews to carve out, build up, bridge over, tunnel through, and blast their way up all of America's west coast. The Pacific Coast Highway has become the generally accepted name of the nearly 1,700-mile-long highway system that runs from Mexico to Canada. The overall route is made up of three designated roads. The southernmost 79 miles is now the Five Freeway, a multi-lane thoroughfare to accommodate all types of traffic, including commerce, military, and commuters. Just below California's San Juan Capistrano, Highway 1 begins its 639-mile scenic route. It moves through exclusive beachfront communities, including the movie star colony of Malibu. Built to celebrate some of the most beautiful coastline in the world, the Pacific Coast Highway is an integral part of the Southern California lifestyle. In Central California, Route 1 takes on a whole new personality as it clings to the very edge of the coastal mountain range and passes over some uniquely devised bridges. The bridges were all designed by engineers from the Division of Highways, and they constitute some of the most spectacular bridges that we have in the state today. Halfway up California, the road crosses over one of the greatest engineering feats of all time,"*
