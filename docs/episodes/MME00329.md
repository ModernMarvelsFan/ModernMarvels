> *"Auto bridge in the world. Two parallel roadways spanning a lake 24 miles wide. Made entirely of thousands of prefabricated parts. Hidden in each span, the techno secret of the bridge's success. An engineering breakthrough that made possible a record-setting triumph. Now, the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway on Modern Marvels. Auto- In the land of voodoo and Mardi Gras, Jambalaya and Zydeco is an engineering marvel that seems to go on forever. Head toward the lake and drive on to a concrete span, one of two that crosses the largest inland body of water in Louisiana. Speed along the highway, two lanes in each direction, toward the north shore of Lake Pontchartrain, and you'll lose sight of land in all directions. That's because at the 12-mile mark, you've only reached the halfway point on what is the longest overwater highway bridge in the world. The bridge actually traverses roughly one one-thousandth of the Earth's circumference. Whatever the causeway may lack in visual flamboyance, it makes up for in engineering and endurance. The single most important thing about this bridge is that it is 24 miles long, and it was built using manufacturing methods, where they built modular beams and piles and pile caps and slabs in a very mechanized way. The beauty of the causeway is in the eye of the beholder. The parallel span stretching out to where you can see no land anywhere in sight"*
