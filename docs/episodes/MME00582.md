By friction or robot, even underwater, these are the tools that the world can't live without. Now, welding on Modern Marbles. Las Vegas, Nevada. Perhaps nowhere else in the world does the old come down as spectacularly. And the new rise up as quickly. Right now you're in the east wing, the new Plaza Hotel. We're on the 38th floor. There's just shy of 70,000 tons. Adrenaline runs high for welders working at such heights. And with so much electricity in use, the dangers are real. This particular machine runs on 483 phase.
