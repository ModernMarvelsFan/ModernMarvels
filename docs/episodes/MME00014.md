#### Cut A Opening Narration
> Mean                                                                          The dark images are part of our imagination, towering, oppressive stone monoliths where the pious, deformed, broken men find solace and shelter among the grinning gargoyles. But in reality, Gothic cathedrals are celebrations of the spirit. They are structures of overwhelming strength, designed to teach and inspire through the beauty of their architecture and remarkable technical virtuosity. Here at Notre Dame in Paris, the genius of Gothic beauty is abundantly clear. The Gothic architect wanted your eye to ascend toward heaven, drawing your eye upward. That beautiful arch draws your eye up as high as it can possibly go. Because people in the Middle Ages really wanted to build these great castles of the spirit, if you will. And they believed that a cathedral should be a place of comfort, where you could come in from the outside, leaving your cares, your worries, your sins behind, and enter into the presence of God. That's what they were trying to achieve in the Middle Ages, was a convergence of heaven on earth. They wanted to build these great cathedrals to the glory of God, to be, if you will, a city of God here on earth. And so there is, in a sense, a convergence. One is imperfect, but we are created in God's image, and so we do our best to create these beautiful cathedrals

>
> The human search for perfection is a quest born of the need to return to the pure transcendent source, to help us endure life and make it less mundane. It can lead to obsession or to frustration. But the striving for perfection often results in great beauty, which reveals a joyous and spiritual purpose. Religion is a celebration of faith. The faithful come together in glorious buildings which have great spiritual power. They come under many names, temples, mosques, synagogues. Over the last 1,000 years, the Gothic cathedral has been a special awe-inspiring building, heaven's perfection on earth. Notre Dame in Paris, Chartres Cathedral in the French countryside, the National Cathedral in Washington, DC. Each is assembled from stone, held together by faith, shrines for beauty and truth. Tonight on Modern Marvels, The Gothic Cathedrals.
