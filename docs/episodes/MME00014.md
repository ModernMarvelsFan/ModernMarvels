But in reality, Gothic cathedrals are celebrations of the spirit. They are structures of overwhelming strength, designed to teach and inspire through the beauty of their architecture and remarkable technical virtuosity. Here at Notre Dame in Paris, the genius of Gothic beauty is abundantly clear. The Gothic architect wanted your eye to ascend toward heaven, drawing your eye upward. That beautiful arch draws your eye up as high as it can possibly go. Because people in the Middle Ages really wanted to build these great castles of the spirit, if you will. And they believed that a cathedral should be a place of comfort, where you could come in from the outside, leaving your cares, your worries, your sins behind, and enter into the presence of God. That's what they were trying to achieve in the Middle Ages, was a convergence of heaven on earth. They wanted to build these great cathedrals to the glory of God, to be, if you will, a city of God here on earth. And so there is, in a sense, a convergence.
