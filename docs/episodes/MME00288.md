### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"The Ball Turret Gunners, next on Modern Marvels."*

> They called it flying the ball. Others called it crazy. Enclosed in a small rotating plastic sphere with two .50 caliber machine guns, these gunners defended the B-17s and B-24s from enemy fighter attack. They were small in size but big on bravery. The Ball Turret Gunners, next on Modern Marvels. The American bomber crews who flew raids against Nazi Germany from 1942 to 1945 knew, all too well, the dangers they faced. From their air bases in England, the men of the Army Air Force attacked heavily defended targets deep inside enemy territory in broad daylight. Once they started on a bomb run, they had to fly straight and level in order to drop their deadly cargo on target. This made them sitting ducks easy prey for German fighter planes and anti-aircraft gunners. Tens of thousands of airmen perished in the skies over Europe. The entire United States Marine Corps didn't suffer as many casualties in the war against Japan as did the airmen who flew bombers over Germany.
