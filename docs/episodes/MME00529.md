> **Opening Hook:**

> Their customized ships cut through the oceans like sharks. From their use of big guns, to hand grenades, to navigation, the technology they developed to practice their murderous trade helped revolutionize naval warfare. Now, learn the three R's of pirate tech on Modern Marvels. Resting 20 feet beneath the surface of North Carolina's Beaufort Inlet, researchers believe they found remains of the Queen Anne's Revenge. This legendary vessel was once the flagship of the notorious pirate Blackbeard. Blackbeard was probably one of the foremost pirates of his era and certainly today is an icon of piracy. I think it was a little of everything that made Blackbeard the pirate that he was, operating essentially a small flotilla of ships, anywhere from 300 to 400 pirates under his command at any one particular time. No one knows for sure how the ship sank. One theory speculates it was not during battle, but on a routine maintenance voyage. he brought his flotilla to Beaufort Inlet he tried to get the Queen of his Revenge into the inlet so he could clean her bottom but in the process of trying to get her in she got stuck on a sandbar and he had no choice after trying to get her off he had no choice but to abandon her right where she had gotten stuck dead men tell no tales
