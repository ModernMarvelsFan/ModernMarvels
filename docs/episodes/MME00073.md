### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, medical imaging, the voyage inward on modern marvels."*

> Amazing images from inside the human body. They're made possible through medical imaging, a life-saving technology that began with the accidental discovery of the X-ray. Now, medical imaging, the voyage inward on modern marvels. Here is the baby's hand and the baby's face. Here's the eye and you can see some movement. The baby's sucking his thumb here. From the womb to old age. What we're looking at is a human pelvis, which has a fracture on one side and is normal on the other side. Medical imaging has allowed doctors to see deep inside the body, nearly eliminating the need of exploratory surgery. I don't think anybody really understands how life-saving and how fantastic medicine has become as a result of the imaging devices. It's been an amazing, just an amazing impact on medicine. The technology that has made medical miracles routine was discovered by accident. completely unexpectedly. The last quarter of the 19th century saw an explosion of technological achievement. Motion pictures, Alexander Graham Bell's telephone, Thomas Edison's phonograph. Physicists believe they were on the verge of understanding everything about the invisible forces that power the universe. But then a group of scientists started experimenting with electromagnetism. They correctly suspected that a connection between electrical particles and magnetic fields resulted in a spectrum of energy waves, including visible light.
