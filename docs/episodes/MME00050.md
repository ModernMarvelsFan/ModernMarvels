### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Radar, the invention that saved democracy on Modern Marvels."*

> Radar. Abstract blips on a screen that translate into planes, bombs, and surprise attacks. Summer, 1940. The Nazi blitzkrieg sweeps through Europe and Britain shudders on the verge of collapse. In Liverpool, a lone British physicist has the prototype for England's last hope for survival. Now, Radar, the invention that saved democracy on Modern Marvels. May 1940. Germany crushes French forces, conquering the country in a matter of weeks. Hitler rides triumphantly into Paris. Just miles away, 20-year-old Pat Hancock finds his Royal Air Force Squadron squarely in the teeth of the Nazi advance. Germans were advancing madly, and from then on, we literally retreated, if not every day, every two or three days. They had already, of course, swept through Norway before that. Then they occupied Belgium, Denmark, and they were having a great old time. Hitler is on the verge of completing his lightning conquest of Europe. Only Britain, a tiny island surrounded by the Nazi blitzkrieg, stands in his way. Defying the grim odds, the British refuse to surrender. England was under severe air attack. And of course, the purpose of this attack was to knock out the Royal Air Force as a prelude to a cross-channel assault on England itself.
