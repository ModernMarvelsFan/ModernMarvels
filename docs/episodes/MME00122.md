### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Prisons on Modern Marvels."*

<details>
<summary><b>Click to view full opening narration (~2 minutes)</b></summary>

> *"From underground dungeons to modern high-tech security cells from workhouses to work details from heavy leg irons to electronic bracelets These are the ways society over the centuries has dealt with crime through punishment. Now, Prisons on Modern Marvels. Today, 8 million people are behind bars around the world, 2 million in the United States alone. Some are housed in high-tech maximum security fortresses. Others are in more medieval-looking structures. Inmates are also secured inside prison walls through a variety of sophisticated technology. I think technology is the key to the future. You've got to give officers in jail and prison systems anywhere in this country, the key behind their success is giving the officers the tools necessary to do their job. A lot of that is about technology. Microwave sensors use a transmitter that emits high frequency radio waves between itself and a receiver. The waves form an invisible electric field. Anything entering that field disturbs the waves, which weakens the signal to the receiver and triggers an alarm. Robots equipped with explosives can blow up walls in prison riots or hostage situations. A lot of technology has been developed just for the correctional industry, but a lot of the development that has come out of defense activities has been now applied to the correctional industry. The toughest and most incorrigible inmates are housed in super maximum security prison units,"*
