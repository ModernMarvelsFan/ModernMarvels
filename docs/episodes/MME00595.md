### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Bedroom Tech on Modern Marvels."*

> It's the place where we... Where science meets engineering in pursuit of perfect sleep. We'll make your bed with power tools and see an alarm clock blast off. Whether you snooze in the sky, in a pod, or in a bed fit for a king... It is where I spend my best hours. It's where sleepers are attacked, dreams are made... And cut short. Now, Bedroom Tech on Modern Marvels. For centuries, the bedroom has been a sanctuary of sorts. A respite from the outside world. And nowhere is that more true than at the famed Playboy Mansion in Homeby Hills, California. in California. Home to the original Playboy himself, Hugh Hefner. And some of his more unusual bedroom accessories. My favorite room of the Playboy Mansion is the bedroom. That's certainly where I spend the best time, the most creative time, both work and play. The first Playboy Mansion was in Chicago. And there the bed was round and rotating and vibrating. The bed here at Playboy Mansion West is actually more traditional in its shape, but much larger in terms of size. Hef built an empire dedicated to the pursuit of pleasure, working from his rarely photographed bedroom. The bedroom is more than simply for sleeping and more for the real entertainment center of my life. the bed is actually set up like a movie theater. Mr. Hefner can control anything from his bed just by his control panel. He's got a DVD player, a laser player, a beta player, a 2VHS, and he can control the lighting. He can control the drapes.
