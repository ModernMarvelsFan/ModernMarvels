### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> **Opening Hook:**  
> *"Now, Nuclear Submarines on Modern Marvels."*

> They are invisible, all-powerful, the most lethal forces on Earth. They secretly roam the oceans, able to rain unspeakable destruction anywhere on the planet. Now, Nuclear Submarines on Modern Marvels. These steel sharks operate in a harsh, unseen twilight world. They maneuver throughout 70% of the Earth's surface in a deadly game of stealth, intrigue, and menace. Some carry more firepower than all the bombs dropped in World War II. They are marine monsters that can be as quiet and unthreatening as a blowfish, but aroused can fire the world's most deadly most deadly cargo from more than 100 feet underwater. A submarine is a platform whose mission is to arrive by stealth, to kill without warning, and to escape without damage to herself. They contain a whole lot of capability into a very small space. They allow 100 or more people to live in that small space comfortably and productively for relatively long periods of time. They're just amazingly efficient machines. Nuclear submarines are priceless military jewels, owned and operated by only a handful of countries. They are driven by the near limitless energy of atomic power and with other advanced naval technologies are the most complicated mechanisms ever built. They operate in a more treacherous environment than space. There's 4,400 pounds per 100 foot of depth pressure on the ship. It's cold. It's dark. There's currents. There's storms that can come up. We're submerged. We have to make our own air. We have to filter our air, remove carbon dioxide. We make our own power, our own water. There's any number of things that can go wrong, and it's truly an unforgiving place.
