### 🎙️ Show Introduction

> Since the dawn of time, prophets and warriors have gone to the desert in search of visions. There is ancient magic in the arid isolation that seems to make the most outrageous visions come to life. Welcome to Las Vegas. 23 million people visit every year for a taste of its intoxicating blend of high stakes, pop culture, and big entertainment. Here is the world's largest amusement park, a glittering celebration of America's pioneering personality and the reckless pleasure of putting all your hopes and dreams on one roll of the dice taking that tumbling ride wherever it leads nearly five billion dollars are wagered here each year a figure larger than the gross national product of many countries gambling on this scale is more than just gaming. It's a ceremony, a ritual, a high drama of winning and losing, of needing to play that next hand. Gilded palaces and exotic temples rise from the desert floor to create an astonishing landscape, a suitable setting for a mythology as colorful as the neon that illuminates the night air. Superstars, billionaires, gangsters have all left an indelible impression on this improbable oasis. Las Vegas will always have that little tinge of, are there truly mob figures still there? And I think if you took that away, it would hurt us, even though I don't know of any mob figures here. Through a combination of careful design and natural selection, the city has transformed itself into the world's most spectacular venue for fantasy.
